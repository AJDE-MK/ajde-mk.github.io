## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'луди' here means men or lads, my reading. 'Јанета' is a name (Jana, Janeta); who gave her the pistols is unclear to me.
