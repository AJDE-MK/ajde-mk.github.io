## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Marko was walking near Black Sea,
> czar's daughter saw him
> and said:
> - Hey you, prince Marko,
> convert to islam (become a Turk) and I'll be your love.
> I'll take you to a colorful mosque,
> I'll call for nine agas,
> I'll name you with the greatest name,
> you can choose from Asan or Asanaga.
>
> Princ Marko said:
> - Hey you, czar's daughter,
> convert to christianity and I'll be your love.
> I'll take you to a colorful church,
> I'll call for nine priests.
> Nine priests, nine monks,
> they'll name you with the greatest name,
> you can choose from Sevda or Sevdalina.
