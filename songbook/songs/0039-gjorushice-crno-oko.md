## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Силвин' (Kocho) is my reading as 'handsome/tall'; 'вити порти' read as ornamented or twisted gates. Both are guesses.
