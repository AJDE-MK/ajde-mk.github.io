# Леле Јано

*Lele Jano* · *Oh my Jana*

Pesna.org #49: <https://pesna.org/song/49>

## Background

A mother-daughter dialogue with a twist. Why do you sit all day at the embroidery frame? Not a silk headscarf, mother: I am embroidering a Macedonian flag, so I can become a young *komita* and cover (mourn) the voivoda Delčev, who died for Macedonia. The hidden object in the embroidery is a trick that appears in many Balkan songs.

## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Lele* Jana, lele dear daughter,
> why are you sitting all day at that embroidery frame?
> Why are you sitting all day at that embroidery frame,
> what are you embroidering, a silk kerchief?
>
> Lele mother, lele old mother,
> I'm not embroidering a silk kerchief.
> I'm not embroidering a silk kerchief,
> but I'm embroidering a Macedonian flag.
>
> I'll become a young Komitadji*,
> so that I can cover young Delchev Vojvoda*.
> To cover Delchev Vojvoda,
> he died for Macedonia,
> for Macedonia.
