# Ој Вардаре македонски

*Oj Vardare makedonski* · *Oh, Macedonian Vardar*

Pesna.org #336: <https://pesna.org/song/336>

## Background

A river-song. The Vardar runs through the middle of Skopje, its cities strung like pearls on its banks, its water healing the wounds of heroes. Wherever it passes it brings luck and joy, from its source to Thessaloniki. Gentle and good for singing along.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Midst of Skopje, Vardar flows,
> Vardar, the singing river.
>
> Oh, Macedonian Vardar,
> From its source to Thessaloniki.
> Oh, Macedonian Vardar,
> You adorn Macedonia.
>
> Your cities like pearls,
> Have decorated your banks.
>
> Oh, Macedonian Vardar,
> From its source to Thessaloniki.
> Oh, Macedonian Vardar,
> You adorn Macedonia.
>
> The fierce wounds of heroes,
> Your water can cure.
>
> Oh, Macedonian Vardar,
> From its source to Thessaloniki.
> Oh, Macedonian Vardar,
> You adorn Macedonia.
>
> Wherever you pass, oh Vardar,
> You bring happiness and joy.
>
> Oh, Macedonian Vardar,
> From its source to Thessaloniki.
> Oh, Macedonian Vardar,
> You adorn Macedonia.
