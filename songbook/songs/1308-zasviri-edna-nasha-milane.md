## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear: sounds like a modern novokomponirana (newly composed) song, but I cannot name an author. 'Засвири една наша' means play one of our songs (my reading of 'наша' as 'our song').
