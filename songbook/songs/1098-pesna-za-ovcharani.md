## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; I cannot name an author. Ovcharani is a village in the Lerin (Florina) area in Greece. 'Мамо' and 'мајко' are both rendered 'mother'.
