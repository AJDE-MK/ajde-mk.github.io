## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Pechalba (labor emigration) song, origin unclear and sounds like a composed song though I cannot name an author. 'стојам простум' (I stand simply) and 'печалбарска рана' (migrant's bread/wound) are my reading; 'рана' can also mean wound.
