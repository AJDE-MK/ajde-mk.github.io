## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Ко ќе иди' is read as 'when he/she comes', and the snake's wish as 'let me bite', my reading. 'отворено' (open) is rendered as 'unattended'.
