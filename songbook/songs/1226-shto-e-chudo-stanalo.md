## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional revolutionary song about the voivodes Ivancho Gevgeliski and Apostol Petkov, per the source note. 'Фатиме пусија' I read as 'catch (them) in an ambush'.
