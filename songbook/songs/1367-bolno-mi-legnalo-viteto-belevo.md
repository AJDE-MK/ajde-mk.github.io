## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. Lines 5-6 of stanza 5 and the Rafail stanzas are unclear; my reading of 'сабја го пресече' is that Rafail cut himself or someone in the heart. 'копан' translated as 'beetle' (laundry beater) per the source note; 'Витето Белево' is a name (Vita Belevo).
