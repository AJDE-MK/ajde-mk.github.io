## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Dialogue between mother and Donka, then the suitor's oath. 'Се колни' is translated as 'swore' (my reading).
