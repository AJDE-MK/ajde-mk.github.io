*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Literal line-for-line translation. The last stanza's 'giving me away' is my reading of the marriage sense; the speaker's voice seems to shift to the girl.
