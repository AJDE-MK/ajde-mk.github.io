## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'масен мазник' (oiled apron) and 'Генџовчето' (son of Gendzo, or 'the young Gendzo') are my readings. 'Делинасу' and 'Булубаша' are names or titles I do not recognize; I left them transliterated.
