## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'ран бел мој' is my reading ('my early white one'); the bracketed first line says 'ран ден мој' ('my early day'). The final stanza is a bit unclear; the speaker seems to be the boy, so 'ќа' is read as 'ќе' (will).
