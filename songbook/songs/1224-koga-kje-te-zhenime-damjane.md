## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear: patriotic, modern-sounding lyrics, but no author I can name. 'Здравје боже' is my reading as 'health to you, God' (possibly 'God grant health'); 'на мајка ти личен' is rendered 'like your mother'.
