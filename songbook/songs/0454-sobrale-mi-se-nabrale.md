## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Jutta Malzbender)*

> Once there gathered,
> sixty young men
> in the plain of Kondiska
> in a cool inn.
>
> They drank strong brandy
> and solemnly pledged allegiance:
> If one of us gets ill
> we will bear him in our arms.
>
> Young Goce fell ill,
> and the young leader Goce Delchev
> spoke to his group of friends:
> My true friends, who are gathered here,
>
> When you come
> to my Kukush, my home town,
> tell my mother,
> that I died at young age,
> that I died at young age,
> for mourning Macedonia.
