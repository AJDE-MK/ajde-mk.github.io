## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Last night I saw, mother, the pretty Stojne,
> last night I saw, mother, the pretty Stojne.
> - Stojne hey, Stojne, step on my foot,
> belle bachelor, come sit on my knees.
>
> The pretty Stojne, mother, has golden forehead,
> Stojne's face, mother, a bagel from Solun.
> - Stojne hey, Stojne, step on my foot,
> belle bachelor, come sit on my knees.
>
> Stojne's eyes, mother, black cherries,
> Stojne's eyebrows, mother, sea leeches.
> - Stojne hey, Stojne, step on my foot,
> belle bachelor, come sit on my knees.
>
> Stojne's body, mother, thin as poplar,
> Stojne's neck, mother, pretty mirror.
> - Stojne hey, Stojne, step on my foot,
> belle bachelor, come sit on my knees.
