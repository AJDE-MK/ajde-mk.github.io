*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Ќе падни' and 'елај' are dialect forms. 'Ние да се земиме' means 'let us marry'. Origin unclear, looks traditional.
