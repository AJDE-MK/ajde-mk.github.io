## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Капела' is rendered 'hats' (my reading, plural/collective). 'Грабнала' could also mean 'taken'; the sense of getting goods free is my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| мома | girl, young unmarried woman |
| грабнала | grabbed, snatched up |
| чорапи | stockings, socks |
| без пари | without money, for free |
| блазе | blessed, lucky |
| ашколсун | well done, bravo (Turkish loan) |
| капела | hat |
