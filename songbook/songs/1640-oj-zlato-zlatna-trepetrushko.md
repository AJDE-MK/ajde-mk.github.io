## English

*From Pesna.org*

> - Oh Zlata, Zlata, golden pipit, golden pipit,
> golden pipit, silver bluethroat,
> come on, Zlata, take me.
>
> - Oh, you crazy, big fool,
> if I know how, then I'll become, crazy, a wood partridge,
> and I'll fly away, crazy, down to the swamp.
>
> So I'll fly away, crazy, down to the swamp,
> and I'll hide, crazy, under the green grass.
> Where will you find me, crazy, to take me?
>
> - Oh Zlata, Zlata, golden pipit, golden pipit,
> I know I'll become, Zlata, this gray falcon,
> and I'll fly away, Zlata, down to the swamp.
>
> So I'll fly away, Zlata, down to the swamp,
> and I'll look for you, Zlata, under the green grass.
> Run away wherever you want, Zlata, you will be mine!
