## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Jana, dear innkeeper,
> I have a great desire, Jana,
> to look at you
> serving Jana, some red wine
> with your Jana, white hands.
> You are moving graciously, Jana, your tall body,
> and your white face, Jana, smiling.
>
> - Young Gjorgji, bon vivant,
> you who drink red wine
> get up and go.
> There is no benefit from looking at me Gjorgji,
> from that desire, Gjorgji, you'll be empty-handed,
> I am already, Gjorgji, engaged,
> and happily married for two years.
>
> - Jana, dear innkeeper,
> bring me another one,
> put some poison in it, poison me.
> So that I don't go to the tavern,
> so that I don't see your tall body,
> so that I don't burn Jana from desire,
> so that I die, Jana, don't torment me.
