## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** A tribute song for the singer Nikola Badev; the lyricist is not named in the text, so origin unclear and I translated it. Refrain switches between 'his' and 'your' song, kept as in the original.
