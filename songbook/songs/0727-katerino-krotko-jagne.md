## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Katerina, my gentle lamb,
> Why are you so yellow and pale?
> Ah, yellow and pale....very pale,
> Ah, is your mother-in-law evil?
>
> Manoilo, my first love,
> My mother-in-law is a gentle lamb,
> Ah, you lead me, but love another,
> Ah, that’s why I am yellow and pale.
>
> Katerina, my gentle lamb,
> Don’t listen to gossip,
> Ah, gossip and lies,
> Ah, I only love you.
>
> Manoilo, my first love,
> I don’t listen to gossip,
> Ah, I saw you with my own eyes,
> Ah, my eyes do not lie.
