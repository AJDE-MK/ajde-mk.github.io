## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> I come drunk from the city,
> I run into a maiden in the meadows.
> I jump off my horse,
> so I could kiss the maiden.
>
> - Last night I was by your house,
> and I sat at the table.
> I drank sweet wine,
> sweet wine, brandy.
>
> I will pray for your mother,
> I will bless your dad.
> I don’t have money to pay,
> but I’m not leaving without you.
>
> The maiden began to flee.
> - Maiden, do not run away,
> you are mine, only mine.
