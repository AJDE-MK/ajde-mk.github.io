## English

*From Pesna.org*

> May you burst, old mother,
> Burst and explode,
> For you've born me beautiful!
> I can't go outside, old mother, on the porch,
> Because of those damn bachelors,
> Those young lads!
>
> They gave me a knitted scarf
> But wanted my white face.
>
> They gave me a red eleče*
> But wanted my black eyes.
>
> They gave me golden coins
> But wanted my tall body!
