## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; it refers to the 1963 Skopje earthquake. 'Шееситрета' is my reading of 'the twenty-sixth' (26 July 1963); that word is garbled in the source.
