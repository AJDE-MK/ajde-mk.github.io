## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-style revolutionary song about Gotse Delchev; 'бурмајлија' as 'signet ring' and 'сандак' as a chest are my reading.

**Words to learn**
