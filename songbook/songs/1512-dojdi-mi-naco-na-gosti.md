# Дојди ми Нацо на гости

*Dojdi mi Naco na gosti* · *Come Visit Me, Nacho*

Pesna.org #1512: <https://pesna.org/song/1512>

**Notes.** Скути (скутина) е престилка, чест дел од македонската народна носија. Дерт е турцизам и значи мака, тешкотија или грижа.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Скути' is read here as 'lap' (my reading; the source notes also mention an apron sense). Last line 'my sweetheart has come to an end' is my reading.
