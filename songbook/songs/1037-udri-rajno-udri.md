# Удри Рајно, удри

*Udri Rajno, udri* · *Strike, Rajno, strike*

Pesna.org #1037: <https://pesna.org/song/1037>

**Notes.** Песната е дел од народните обичаи во Струшка Малесија.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Song of Struga Malesija customs (per the source note). 'Рајно' is a name or call; 'кој ќе се наджниет' as 'who will outdo the other' is my reading (dialect, unsure).
