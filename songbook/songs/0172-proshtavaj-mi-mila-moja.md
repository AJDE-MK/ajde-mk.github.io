## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Revolutionary-era style; I treated it as traditional but it may be a later composed song. 'Сакет да исфрлат' is dialect for 'they want to throw out'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| проштавај | forgive / farewell |
| комити | komitas (irregular fighters of the revolutionary movement) |
| дружина | company, band of comrades |
| борци | fighters |
| ропство | bondage, slavery |
| маки | torments, sorrows |
| тесно им е в' градите | their chests are tight (they are oppressed) |
