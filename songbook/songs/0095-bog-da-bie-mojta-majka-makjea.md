## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Ерменско' (Armenian village) is taken literally; the verb forms ме зедова, ме пратива are dialect and I read them as 'they took me, they sent me'.
