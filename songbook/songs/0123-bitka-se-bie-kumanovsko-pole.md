## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. Pelince and Pchinja are places/river near Kumanovo; the personal names are as written in the song. 'показаа пети' is the idiom 'showed their heels' (fled).
