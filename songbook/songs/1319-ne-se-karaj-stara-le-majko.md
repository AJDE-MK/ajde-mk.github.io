## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, sounds like a modern tavern song; no author known to me. 'мако моја ти' is my reading as an exclamation ('woe to you, my...' or 'my dear'); the refrain may be addressed to the woman, not the mother.
