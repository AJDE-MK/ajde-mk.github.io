## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, per the note the first recorded Macedonian folk song (Kostur, 16th century); archaic Kostur dialect. 'зарви ме' and 'дарви ме' are my reading ('embrace me', 'grant it to me') and 'станви' is a guess.
