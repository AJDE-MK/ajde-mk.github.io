## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. 'аресала' meaning 'took a liking to herself' is my reading from Turkish 'beğenmek'. The coined verbs разглавувам, разженувам, распопувам are playful and I render them by sense.
