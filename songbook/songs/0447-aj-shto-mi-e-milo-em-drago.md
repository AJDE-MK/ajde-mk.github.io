## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** WWII partisan song, treated as anonymous folk song; I do not know of a named author. 'Лавчанската корија' as 'Lavci grove' is my reading of a place name.
