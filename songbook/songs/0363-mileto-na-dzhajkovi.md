## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Mile from Dzhajkovi,
> Vase from Vasevi,
> Vase honey, Vase my heart,
> let's get married.
>
> Mile from Dhzajkovi,
> generous without money,
> Vase, Vase, Vase my soul,
> let's get married.
>
> Because of that damn generousity,
> he sold his jacket,
> Vase, Vase, Vase my soul,
> let's get married.
