## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Karanfil (Carnation) girl
> I want to drink some water.
> - If you want to drink,
> go and have a drink.
>
> - Carnation girl
> with your mouth you give me to drink.
> - My mouth is full
> with lokum candies.
>
> - Carnation girl
> I want to drink some water.
> - If you want to drink,
> go and have a drink.
>
> - Carnation girl
> with your palm you give me to drink.
> - My palm is full
> with golden rings.
>
> - Carnation girl
> give me some drink with the jug.
> - The jug is, you crazy boy
> for your mouth.
