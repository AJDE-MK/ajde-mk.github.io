## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Лика, прилика' is rendered as 'match, likeness' (my reading: a suitable husband). 'Лудо младо' is read as 'young fool', a term of address for a young man, as my reading; 'навадиш' as 'pull up' is a guess.
