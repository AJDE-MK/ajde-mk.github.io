## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Дембел чаршија' taken as a place name (market street in Bitola). 'Пукнала, треснала' is a curse, rendered in my reading as 'may you burst, may you crack'.
