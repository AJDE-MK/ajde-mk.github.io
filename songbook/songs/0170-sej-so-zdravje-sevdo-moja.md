## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The last stanza is unclear and my reading; 'денеска си го љубело' may refer to the face having kissed someone. The note on the source says 'коноштисам' means 'to meet'; I translated it as spending the night, which is a guess.
