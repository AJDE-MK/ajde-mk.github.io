## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Бабо' is addressed to the girl's father (a request to see the daughter). The 'сè болно легнало' line is my reading: whoever saw her fell sick (with love).
