## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Промена' as a kerchief and 'брачеди' as cousins are my reading. The dance speaker explains why they cannot join hands as kin.
