## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Knead, Velа sister, knead,
> knead nine loaves,
> twist nine pies.
>
> They will come, Velа sister, they'll come,
> the village policemen will come,
> you must take all their horses.
>
> But Asan's, Velа sister, Asan's,
> Asan's horse, don't take it.
> Asan, he killed your brother.
