## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> A young maiden lies ill,
> she’s been ill for three months.
> A young maiden lies ill,
> she’s been ill for three months.
>
> The young maiden then speaks,
> the young maiden, to her mom:
> - I’d like to drink cold water,
> to drink, so I can die.
>
> Call for me my young lad,
> so I can see him and then die.
> The young lad came,
> she saw him, she kissed him.
>
> She quietly tells him:
> - I am dying for you!
> She wasn’t done speaking her words,
> when at once the maiden died.
>
> She suffers, she aches,
> she suffers,
> she aches for the young lad.
