*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; sounds like a newer composed folk-style song, but I cannot name an author. 'Ле' is a vocative filler after names.
