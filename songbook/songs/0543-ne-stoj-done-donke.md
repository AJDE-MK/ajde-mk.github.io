## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Доне' is probably a pet name or vocative next to Донке, unsure. 'Дај дуни, дуни, дуни' is my reading (dunuti = blow); it may be a refrain filler. 'Ал' as 'red' is my reading.
