## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The first line is ambiguous in who fell in love with whom; my reading is that the two brothers fell for Dzhefa. 'ја пушти' rendered 'let her go' (my reading, release her from the marriage).
