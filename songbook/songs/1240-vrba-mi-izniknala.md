## English

*From Pesna.org*

> A willow tree has sprouted,
> in the Solun1 Field,
> a wide field.
>
> Its veins were,
> stretching as far as Voden2,
> its veins were,
> stretching as far as Voden.
>
> On the tip there is,
> a colorful chicken,
> on the tip there is,
> a colorful chicken.
>
> The chicken sings
> a very sad song,
> when they met,
> brother and sister,
> they didn't recognize each other.
