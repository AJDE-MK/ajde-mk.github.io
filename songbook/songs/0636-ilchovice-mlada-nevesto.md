## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Ilchovica, young bride,
> have you heard, have you understood?
> Have you heard, have you understood,
> that the agha has come?
>
> That the agha has come,
> the agha, the bey with the mustache?
> Ilcovitsa, young bride,
> you have a big village.
>
> You have a big village,
> you have beautiful maidens.
> Slim and tall in build,
> with white and red faces.
>
> Ilchovice, young bride,
> you please me, I like you.
> You please me, I like you,
> young bride of Ilcho.
