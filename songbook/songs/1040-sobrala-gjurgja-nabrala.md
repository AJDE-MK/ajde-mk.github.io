## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Last stanza has 'Тренда' where the earlier stanza has 'Тренка'; I treated it as the same name. Ѓурѓа as a woman's name and Николица as a village are my reading.
