## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The speaker changes: the mother's curse is quoted in the fourth and fifth stanzas, then the girl turns back to the lover. 'Митро ле' is a vocative; who is addressed is my reading. Traditional.
