## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Balkan Wars era song (Kumanovo, Lozengrad/Kirklareli, Janina fell in 1912-13); the Ottoman Osman is comforted, then told the lands are lost. 'Анче' and 'анамо' are words I am unsure of (Turkish ana, 'mother/dear'?); 'little dear' and 'my dear' are my reading.
