## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Modern patriotic song, origin unclear (I cannot name an author). Chento is Metodija Andonov-Cento, first president of ASNOM, which met at the Prohor Pcinski monastery in 1944.
