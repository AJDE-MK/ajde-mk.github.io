## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional ballad. The husband is called 'момче' in the early verses and Stojan later; 'Го заклаја два џелата' is read in the child's voice (my reading), with the lines about the head being a supernatural voice.
