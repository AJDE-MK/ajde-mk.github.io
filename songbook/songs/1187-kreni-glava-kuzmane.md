## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. The last stanza ('шчто те тебе' text is 'што те тебе, Кузман, предадоа') is read as a curse on the comrades who betrayed Kuzman (my reading); 'распули се' is a guess.
