## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Нakачи горе' (hang it up there) is my reading of 'take up/carry it to'; 'во гора сонце огреа' is literally 'the sun shone in the forest'. 'влегоја' is a dialect past plural ('they entered').
