## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Вардаринке' rendered 'of the Vardar' (my reading, a girl from the Vardar). 'Мирче Ацев' and 'Страшо Пинџур' are names of historical revolutionary fighters as far as I can tell, but I am not certain.
