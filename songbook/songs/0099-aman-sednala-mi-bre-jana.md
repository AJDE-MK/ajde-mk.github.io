# Аман седнала ми бре Јана

*Aman sednala mi bre Jana* · *Oh, Jana sat down*

Pesna.org #99: <https://pesna.org/song/99>

## Background

Jana sits under the shade of an arbour, braiding a wide rush mat while she quarrels with her mother. She asks for the two new jugs so she can go to the cold well, cool her throat and forget her troubles. The water-fetch is an excuse to leave the argument. The *aman* and *lele* sighs between the lines are typical of urban songs.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Oh, Jana sat down
> under that shadow wild wine,
> oh, that white wild wine
> she was knitting
> and arguing with her mother.
>
> Hey, didn't you hear me, Jana,
> oh, you old witch,
> oh you old, old woman,
> hey you witch, give me
> those new pitchers.
>
> Oh, let me go
> to the cold spring,
> I will drink some water
> to cool my throat
> and forget my worries.
