## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Kojnik is a Veles neighbourhood. Stanza 3 lines 3 and 4 ('зик тончето', 'на Спасеви') are unclear dialect; my reading only. 'Бузанчанки' is my reading as girls from Buzanci.
