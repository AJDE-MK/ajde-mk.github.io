## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (wedding song in folk style, no author I can name). 'девет рала тапани' is a guess: I read it as 'drums for nine ploughs' (a lot of drums); 'рало' is a plough.

**Words to learn**

| тапани | drums (tapan) |

| зурли | zurlas (reed pipes) |
| аирлија | good fortune, blessing |
