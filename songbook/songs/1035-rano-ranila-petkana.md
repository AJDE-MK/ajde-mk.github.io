# Рано ранила Петкана

*Rano ranila Petkana* · *Petkana rose early*

Pesna.org #1035: <https://pesna.org/song/1035>

**Notes.** Песната е дел од свадбарските обичаи во Струшка Малесија.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional wedding song (per the note, from Struga Malesija). 'стребрената' is read as 'сребрената' (silvered); 'стројници стројвет' is my reading and could mean those who dress or prepare the bride.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| рано | early |
| ѓемија | boat, ship |
| кавал | kaval, end-blown shepherd's flute |
| пиле шарено | speckled little bird (endearment) |
| најнапред | first of all |
| стројници | preparers (of the wedding, my reading) |
| јунаци | heroes, brave young men |
| пеаје / минаје | crowed / passed (dialect past forms) |
