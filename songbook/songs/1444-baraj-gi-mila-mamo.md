## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; sounds like a modern pop-folk song, no author I can name. 'да се изгубени' uses the short Macedonian dialect form for 'that they are lost'.
