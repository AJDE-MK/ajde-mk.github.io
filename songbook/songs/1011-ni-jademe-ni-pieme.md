## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. The last two stanzas are confusing: 'два прстена да размени' (exchange two rings) sits oddly with 'separate us', so those lines are my reading. 'Дејгиди' is a dialect exclamation I am unsure of.
