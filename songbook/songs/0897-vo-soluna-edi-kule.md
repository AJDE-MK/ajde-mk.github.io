## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-style revolutionary song, no author known (origin unclear). 'Еди куле' is Yedi Kule, a fortress in Salonica; 'до две' read as 'two'. 'Осветат' read as 'avenge' (my reading).
