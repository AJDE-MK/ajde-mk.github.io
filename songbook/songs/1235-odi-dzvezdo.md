## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, sounds like a modern song. 'Срцево ме стега' is my reading: 'my heart squeezes me'; the last stanza line 'ѝ го дадов' is read as 'gave it to her'.
