# Чува мајка до две лути ќерки

*Čuva majka do dve luti ḱerki* · *A mother raised two fierce daughters*

Pesna.org #867: <https://pesna.org/song/867>

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional curse song. 'Чува мајка' is my reading as 'a mother raised'; 'Што ми биле' line is rendered literally as 'What were they'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| лути | fierce, bitter |
| т'нко преле | they spun thinly |
| се колнат | they curse each other |
| рода | kin, offspring |
| ги ожениш | you marry them off |
| снаи | daughters-in-law |
| удовици | widows |
| кукавици | cuckoos (also wretched women) |
