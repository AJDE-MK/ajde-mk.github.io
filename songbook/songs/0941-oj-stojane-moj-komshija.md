## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional humorous song, southern/eastern dialect (су = са/се, гу = го). Stanzas 2, 3 and 5 contain lines I am guessing at, marked 'my reading'; the joke seems to be that the rooster did not crow.
