## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Ливаѓе' is my reading (meadow or fruit-garden). The final stanza is read as bitter irony: he will arrive still teased by dogs and girls.
