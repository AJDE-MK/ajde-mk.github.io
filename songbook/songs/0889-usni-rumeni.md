*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; the polished wording may be a newer composed song but I cannot name an author. 'Сал јадови... душа пламнаа' is my reading ('only sorrows set my soul aflame'); 'зборој' is a dialect plural of 'зборови'.
