## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Достојно' is used as a vocative epithet; my reading is 'worthy one'. 'Не се турчам' rendered as 'I will not turn Turk' (convert), as in the notes about the Nizams.

**Words to learn**
