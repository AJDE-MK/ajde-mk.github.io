## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The second version's stanza 'до твојто либе две момчиња' and the last line are my reading. 'Тешка куќа на глава' is likely a figure for the burden of a household.
