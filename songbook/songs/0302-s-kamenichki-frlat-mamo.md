## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Stanza 2 line 1 'Дал' да одам' I render as a question (my reading); 'намаме' in the last stanza is taken as a typo for 'немаме'.
