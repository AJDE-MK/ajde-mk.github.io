## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'ми ги пејат ... книги старославни' I read as 'they sing for us from Old Slavonic books'; 'попој' is my reading of 'popovi' (priests).
