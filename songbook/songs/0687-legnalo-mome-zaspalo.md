## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The last lines of stanzas 3 to 5 ('како него да се тркала', 'низ него да се провира', 'со него да се ќердосам') are my reading as the girl's wish: to roll like the apple, to slip through the ring, to enjoy herself with the kisser. 'ќердосам' is a dialect word I am unsure of.
