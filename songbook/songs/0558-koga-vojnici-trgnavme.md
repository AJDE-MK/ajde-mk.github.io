## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Likely a First World War era song ('three-year war' is from the text); 'жална од осум месеци' I read as 'sorrowful, eight months along', i.e. pregnant (my reading).
