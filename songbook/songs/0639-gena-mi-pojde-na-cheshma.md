## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Haiduk (outlaw) ballad in folk style; origin unclear, no author I can name. 'накачи патот кон дома' is my reading as 'took the road home'.

**Words to learn**
