## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Да си ојме' is my reading ('to go home'); 'вампир шета' may be a jocular warning that someone is prowling at night.
