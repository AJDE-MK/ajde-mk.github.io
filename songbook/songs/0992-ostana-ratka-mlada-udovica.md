## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Aleksey Dvorecki)*

> Ratka was left a young widow
> at the age of seventeen.
> Ratka hid her white face with a veil,
> and mourned for her first love.
>
> Ratka's mother speaks to her:
> - Don't mourn Ratka, don't grieve.
> You my daughter, you are young and beautiful,
> you'll fall in love with another young man.
>
> Ratka cried to her mother and said:
> - Don't talk to me mother, don't burn me.
> There is no one like my love anywhere,
> not in Muslim lands, nor in Christian lands!
> Not in Muslim lands, nor in Christian lands,
> not in all of Macedonia.
