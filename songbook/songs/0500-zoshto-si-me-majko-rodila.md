## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear: no author I can name. The second stanza is hard to follow; 'Херој биди мила мајко ти' is my reading ('be a hero, dear mother'), possibly meaning 'be proud'.
