## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Glamorous girl, beautiful,
> the master tailor sent me.
> Give me some thin silk threads,
> so that he can sew your dress.
>
> - Apprentice, you little devil,
> where did the master see me?
> Where did the master see me,
> how did he cut my dress?
>
> - When you were going to the bathhouse,
> you passed by the shop.
> Your shadow entered his shop
> and the master took the measurements for your dress.
