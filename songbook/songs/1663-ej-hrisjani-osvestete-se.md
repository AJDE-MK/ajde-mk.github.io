## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> What a desolate time has come, we have lost our ways,
> brothers are separated from brothers, not to see each other,
> mothers and fathers are not taken care of,
> everyone sells everything they can.
>
> Hey Christian brothers, wake up,
> don't sell your houses, your native land,
> that money is no good, there is no happiness in it.
> Your grandchildren, great-grandchildren will curse you,
> that for money, you sold everything.
> Hey Christian brothers, wake up,
> again (enemy) eyes are hovering over the Balkans,
> again they will want to sew us a fez,
> day and night they are preparing,
> they are waiting for the right time to destroy us,
> hey, wake up.
>
> Under the Ottomans it was also difficult,
> honor and Orthodox faith were defended with blood,
