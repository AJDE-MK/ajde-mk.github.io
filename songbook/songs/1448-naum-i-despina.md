*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, sounds like a modern composed song but I cannot name an author. 'руба даровна' and 'остави душа за понадица' are my readings; 'судбина' is used as feminine here.
