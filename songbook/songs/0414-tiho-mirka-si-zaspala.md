## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Partisan-era song about Mirka Ginova (Second World War). 'Почепските планини' is a place name I read as the Pocep mountains; Воден is the town of Edessa, Greece (Voden).
