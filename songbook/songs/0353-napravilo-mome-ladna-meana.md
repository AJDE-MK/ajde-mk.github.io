## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Јало ми, пило' is my reading: 'he rode, he drank', possibly a rhyming filler phrase. 'Испило коњчето' read as drinking away the price of the horse.
