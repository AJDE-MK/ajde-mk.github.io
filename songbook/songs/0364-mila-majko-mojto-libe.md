## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Dear mother, my darling
> is not home,
> he went abroad,
> oh, big sorrow.
>
> Three years have passed by
> as he is gone,
> neither he writes, nor he comes
> to see me.
>
> If I can see him once
> to come home,
> or to see him in my dreams
> like in real life.
>
> He is dressed in long clothes,
> long until the ground,
> if I meet him, if I see him,
> I won't recognize him.
