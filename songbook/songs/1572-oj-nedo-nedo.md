## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional humorous song, origin unclear; the boasts are mock-riches (frog's leg of silver, mouse-skin of wine). 'Кучке' is an insulting address (bitch) and 'од глувче меше' and 'едно рало гаќи' are my reading. 'Слекува' is dialect for taking clothes off.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| чорбаџија | wealthy man, rich householder (Turkish-origin) |
| богатство | wealth |
| срма | gold or silver thread embroidery |
| глувче | mouse |
| меше | skin bag for liquids (my reading) |
| маана | fault, flaw (Turkish-origin) |
| свадба | wedding |
| рало | plough |
