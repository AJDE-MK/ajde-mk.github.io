## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Do you know, you rascal
> why do I come to you so often?
>
> - I know, you rascal, I know,
> but I am just a little girl.
>
> - If you are "just a little girl",
> then why do you have a gold coin on your forehead,
> and a necklace on your white neck?
>
> On your slender body you wear a dress,
> over your thin belly, a felt dress.
