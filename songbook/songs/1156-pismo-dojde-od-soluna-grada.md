## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Dialect forms: јазе, ју, је. 'Ју писмото пише' is my reading: 'the letter writes'. The song is a dialogue between the son (Simo) and his mother.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| писмо | letter |
| кум | best man, godfather (ritual sponsor) |
| кумство | the bond/office of a kum |
| рода | kinship, family tie |
| најголема | greatest |
| јазе | I (dialect form of јас) |
| је | is (dialect form of е) |
