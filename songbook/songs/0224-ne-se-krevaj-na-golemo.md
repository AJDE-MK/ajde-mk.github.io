## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Колено' is a pun or double sense (knee / lineage), my reading. 'Калешо' I read as 'falcon' (калеш/калеш-птица); this is a guess, possibly dialect.
