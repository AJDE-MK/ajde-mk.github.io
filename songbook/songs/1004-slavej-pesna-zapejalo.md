## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional ballad of twins separated and unknowingly married; 'ми' is a filler in many lines. 'Лута змија' (fierce snake, i.e. a very fussy child) and 'се дење - ноќе сè да плаче' are my reading.
