## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Parashka went to the Bair1
> to fill a bucket in the spring
> a jug of cold water to take home
> to knead some fresh bread.
>
> When Parashka came to the spring
> the drummer Kiro stood in front of her:
> - And now Parashka, let's see what you're going to do,
> you've been bothering me for three years.
>
> - Mind your own business, I ask you, Kiro,
> let me fill a jug of water from the spring.
> I'm about to bring it home
> to knead some fresh bread.
>
> - Why do you keep running away from me, Parashka,
> and you don't even see me on the promenade?
> We'll soon finish building the new house
> and then Parashka will get engaged.
>
> - Oh, oh, oh, look how he's bragging,
> and he doesn't even have sandals on his feet.
> If you want, brother, buy yourself a little car,
> you're older, Kiro, than my uncle.
>
> You can build the new house whenever you want
> and get engaged with Trajanka.
> I'll marry a young boy,
> a young beardless boy.
