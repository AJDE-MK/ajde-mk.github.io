## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional love song. 'Вечера ти најдов' I read as 'I found only supper (not you)', my reading; Кита is a girl's name and also 'bouquet'.
