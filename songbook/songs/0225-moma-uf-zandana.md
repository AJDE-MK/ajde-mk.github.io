## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> It was never heard and seen before,
> a girl to be imprisoned,
> in Salonika's dark dungeons.
>
> A girl to be imprisoned,
> for her father's debts.
>
> An old woman came in the dungeon:
> - Is there a girl here to lie?
>
> - Here I am, old mother, here I am,
> here I am, old mom, because I am.
>
> My hair is falling,
> my white face, mother, has darkened.
>
> Sell, old mother, your property
> to the Turks, mother, give the money.
>
> - The Turks, daughter, do not want money,
> the Turks, daughter, want you.
>
> - Nine years, mother, I have been lying,
> and I will lie for another nine.
>
> Mother, I will not give my faith to the Turks,
> mother, I will not become a Turk's wife.
