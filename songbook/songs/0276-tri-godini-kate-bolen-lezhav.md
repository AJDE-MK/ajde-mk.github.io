## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Three years, Kate, I have been lying sick, come on,
> you didn't come, Kate, to see me.
> Finally God brought you to me, come on,
> to bring me, Kate, some treats.
>
> To bring me, Kate, some treats, come on,
> in the middle of summer, Kate, a yellow quince.
> In the middle of summer, Kate, a yellow quince, come on,
> in the middle of winter, Kate, a watermelon.
>
> Move up a little bit, Kate, this pillow, come on,
> turn it, Kate, towards the lake.
> Let me see the lake, Kate, come on,
> how are pounding, Kate, the waves, the waves.
>
> How are pounding, Kate, the waves, the waves, come on,
> the same way is pounding, Kate, my heart.
> The same way is pounding, Kate, my heart, come on,
> my heart, Kate, for yours.
