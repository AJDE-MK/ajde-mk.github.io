# Лисаво

*Lisavo* · *Lisava*

Pesna.org #1089: <https://pesna.org/song/1089>

**Notes.** 1 Џан е турцизам и значи душа, живот.

2 Кадана (турски: kadın) е угледна жена, добра домаќинка.

3 Кокона е дотерана жена, дама.

4 Срп е рачен алат за жнеење.

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Lisava, Lisava, Lisava, mother's daughter,
> my soul, white lady,
> cocotte from Solun (Thessaloniki).
>
> When you sing, mother's Lisava,
> it is heard far away, even in our field,
> even in our field, even on our plum.
>
> When I hear you, mother's Lisavo,
> I throw away the scythe, I climb on the stack,
> my heart is burning, how well you sing.
>
> When you sing, mother's Lisava,
> it is heard far away, beyond three mountains,
> beyond three seas, the Solun field.
