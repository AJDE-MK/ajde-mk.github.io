## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Southern dialect (-t verb endings). 'Чамче' and 'елбасанче' (probably a dance tune, Elbasan) are my guess; 'шчуро' left as '...'; 'дан' ти го' read as 'or I'll'; 'летниците' read as 'the summer ones' (my reading).
