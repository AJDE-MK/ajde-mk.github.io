## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'со Бога ли си вечерала' is an idiom for 'are you so divinely beautiful'; my reading. 'ле' is an interjection rendered 'hey'.
