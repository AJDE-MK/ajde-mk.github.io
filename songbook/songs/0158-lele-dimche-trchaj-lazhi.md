## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, sounds like a humorous urban folk song. 'личен' (handsome, nice) and 'ту бре' (a filler, my reading) are guesses; 'јаз'к да ти е' is my reading as a mild curse, 'a curse on your tongue'. The first-person lines in stanza 3 slip between 'he' and 'I'.
