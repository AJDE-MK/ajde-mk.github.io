*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; I cannot name an author. The first line is awkward (it addresses the mother but says 'loved me'); my reading is that the narrator is telling his mother how he loved Elena. 'Ones first love' is rendered as 'one's first love'.
