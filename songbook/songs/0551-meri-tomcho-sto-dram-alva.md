## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Шеќерлија' could mean a confectioner or a sweet-lover; I read it as a confectioner/sweet-seller. Tomche is a personal name.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| мери | measure (imperative) |
| драм | dram (old unit of weight) |
| алва | halva (sweet) |
| шеќерлија | sweet-maker or seller; one who loves sweets |
| однеси | take (it) there |
| преку река | across the river |
| ако те питат | if they ask you |
