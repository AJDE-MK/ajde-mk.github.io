## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Alan Moore)*

> A Nightingale sings early
> Early singing before dawn
> and wakes a crazy lad,
> crazy lad, not married.
>
> - Get up, get crazy lad
> to go to the mountain peak,
> there are three girls,
> three Macedonian girls.
>
> The first girl gathers wood,
> the second girl lights a fire,
> the second girl lights a fire,
> the third girl roasts lamb.
