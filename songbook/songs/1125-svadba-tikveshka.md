## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, no author I can name. Tikvesh is a region of Macedonia known for wine. 'Ај да те моме кандисам' is my reading (the speaker wants to win her over).
