## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. Лело is used as an address/refrain; the speakers' roles (Gjorgi, Todorka) are my reading. The last two lines are a proverb-like image, my reading: a beautiful girl has no 'family' because everyone claims her.
