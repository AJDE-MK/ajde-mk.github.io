## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> A young man is about to go
> to the market in Solun
> so he asks his girlfriend
> to make an order.
>
> Tell me girl, order,
> what do you want me to bring to you,
> a silver bodice, girl,
> or a necklace?
>
> Buy dear, buy honey,
> whatever your heart wants
> whatever your heart wants, dear,
> as long as we don't have it here.
