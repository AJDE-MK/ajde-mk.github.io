## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Stand up girl and dance
> let me see you with joy,
> a couple of playful dances,
> our dance is beautiful.
> Opa nina ninanai, nina nai na
> nina nai dear girl nina nai nai.
>
> Curl your body, girl,
> your beautiful body,
> let me see you, let me sing,
> because I live only once.
> Opa nina ninanai, nina nai na
> nina nai dear girl nina nai nai.
>
> We live just once
> this cunning life,
> while I am alive, let me rejoice
> and if I die, I won't complain.
> Opa nina ninanai, nina nai na
> nina nai dear girl nina nai nai.
