## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Refers to Jane Sandanski (revolutionary leader in Pirin); the song itself is traditional with no author named, so translated.
