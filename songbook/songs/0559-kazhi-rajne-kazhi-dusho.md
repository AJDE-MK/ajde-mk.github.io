## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Нишан' is rendered as 'mark'; it could also mean a token or love-bite, so the sense is my reading. 'Главатар' as 'leader' is my reading.
