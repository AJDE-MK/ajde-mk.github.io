## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. The mother's first speech is my reading (she reproaches Jankula's hospitality). 'Мутавче' (a woven kerchief/rough cloth) and 'угу' (I read as 'only') are guesses, as is 'со капина опашана'.
