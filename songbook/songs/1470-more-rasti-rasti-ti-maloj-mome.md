## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** A shape-shifting chase song. 'јаска' is my reading of 'I myself'; the last stanzas say 'lets fly' of a fish, which is in the original and rendered literally. Origin unclear, looks traditional.
