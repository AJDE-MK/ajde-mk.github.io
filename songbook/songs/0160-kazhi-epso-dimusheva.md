## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Tell me, Epsa Dimuseva,
> where were you engaged to be married?
> - I was engaged to be married
> in Gjigota, for Dushan.
>
> Don't cry, young Mato Kuzmanov,
> don't cry, don't burn me.
>
> - Tell me, Epsa Dimuseva,
> who was your fiancé?
> - My fiancé was
> the young Ljube, son of Stojko.
>
> Don't cry, young Mato Kuzmanov,
> don't cry, don't burn me.
> Don't cry, young Mato Kuzmanov,
> you burned my heart.
