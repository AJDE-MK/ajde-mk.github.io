## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Dialect forms such as 'речит', 'заш/зш' (why), 'страмев' are Ohrid-area speech; 'з' едно' I read as 'with/over one', and 'милувал' as courting rather than literal caressing, my reading.
