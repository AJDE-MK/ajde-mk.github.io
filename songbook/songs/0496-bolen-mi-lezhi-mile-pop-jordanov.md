## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Alan Moore)*

> Sick lies Mile Popjordanov
> by his head stands his old mother,
> sadly grieving him, crying for him:
>
> - Get up for me, get up my dear child
> your friends go through the streets
> singing sad songs, to remember you.
>
> God forgive Mile Popjordanov,
> Mile Popjordanov for people killed
> for people killed, for Macedonia.
>
> His sisters dressed in black,
> to make a wedding, brothers to marry,
> brothers to marry for Macedonia.
>
> His mother dressed in black,
> to make a wedding, the son to marry
> the son to marry for Macedonia.
>
> Translated by Alan Moore
>
> Mile Pop Jordanov is ill on his bed
>
> Mile Pop Jordanov is ill on his bed
> His old mother beside his body
> She is in sorrow and crying so dearly
>
> Rise, o rise, my dear son
> Your friends are on the streets
> Singing sad songs, songs about you.
>
> God Forgive Mile Pop Ordanov
> God Forgive Mile Pop Ordanov
> Mile Pop Ordanov, who died for the people
> for the people he died, and for Macedonia
>
> His sisters dressed in black
> Call for their brother's wedding
> Their brother's wedding to Macedonia
>
> His mother dressed in black
> She is going to his wedding
> Her son's wedding to Macedonia.
>
> Translated by Martin Atanasovski
