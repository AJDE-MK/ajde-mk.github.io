## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, no author I can name. 'Ја кршам' (break the figure) is my reading as a folk image of embracing tightly; 'ја љуби' uses ја as a repeated object marker.
