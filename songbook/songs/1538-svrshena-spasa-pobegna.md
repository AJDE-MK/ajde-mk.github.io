## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'с'јоди' in line 'Трајче да с'јоди до дома' is my reading (hurry/ride home). 'Бабинче' means 'of Babino' and Babino/Sapotnica are village names. Origin unclear, looks traditional.
