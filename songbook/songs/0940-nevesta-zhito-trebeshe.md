## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> A bride was cleaning grain,
> feeding nine doves
> and was asking them:
>
> - Doves, my brothers,
> you are flying high,
> you can see far.
>
> Did you see my husband,
> my husband is a soldier?
> - Hey, darkish bride,
> we saw your husband.
>
> He was in his father's yard,
> preparing his horse,
> thinking of his home.
