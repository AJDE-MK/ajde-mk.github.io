## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Доне, Донке' is a repeated vocative on a name (Donka); 'Ни година не пројде' is my reading of 'not even a year passed'. Could not confirm whether this is anonymous folk or a composed song.
