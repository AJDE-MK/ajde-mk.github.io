## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Where have you gone, oh dear Gjurgja,
> where are you going,
> take a look, stop for a second,
> at least speak to me.
>
> Desire, my desire may pass,
> like you, my dear Gjurgja,
> the eye has not seen.
>
> You shine like the sun, oh dear Gjurgja,
> when you smile,
> take a look, stop for a second,
> at least speak to me.
>
> Desire, my desire may pass,
> like you, my dear Gjurgja,
> the eye has not seen.
