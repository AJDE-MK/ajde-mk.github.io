## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> A dream won’t come to the foolish lad
> A dream won’t close his black eyes
> A dream won’t close his black eyes
> And he just can’t fall asleep.
>
> They brought him a colorful pitcher
> A colorful pitcher of cold water
> The foolish lad can’t catch a wink.
>
> - Bring him a cluster of grapes!
> They brought him a cluster of grapes
> The foolish lad can’t catch a wink.
>
> - Bring him sweet apples!
> They brought him sweet apples
> The foolish lad can’t catch a wink.
>
> - Bring him a colorful quilt!
> They brought him a colorful quilt
> The foolish lad can’t catch a wink.
>
> - Bring him a young maiden!
> They brought him a young maiden
> The foolish lad then fell asleep.
