## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional Bitola town song. 'Шарклија' as 'decorated' and 'анамче' as 'young lady' are my reading (unsure); 'Ајде' is a filler, rendered 'Come on'.
