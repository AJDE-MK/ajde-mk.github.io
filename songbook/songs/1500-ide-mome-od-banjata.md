# Иде моме од бањата

*Ide mome od banjata* · *A girl comes from the bathhouse*

Pesna.org #1500: <https://pesna.org/song/1500>

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, origin unclear. The third stanza is ambiguous; 'рудо јагне' (fleecy or ruddy lamb) is my reading, and who lets the lamb go in the last stanza is also my reading (the girl).
