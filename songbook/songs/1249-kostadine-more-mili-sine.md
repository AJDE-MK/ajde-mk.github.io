## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Dialect of the Nevrokop/Razlog area (Pirin Macedonia). 'да постелеш, мале' is my reading (mother addressed mid-line). 'малкај мома' read as 'little girl'.
