## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; reads like a 20th-century patriotic song but I cannot name an author. 'Македонско злато' as Prilep tobacco is a guess.
