## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Jutta Weber-Karn)*

> Nevena dear, Nevena,
> Nevena dear, Nevena,
> hey, pampered name, lele,
> hey, pampered name.
> Nevena dear, Nevena, lele,
> hey, pampered name.
>
> May God strike dead, Nevena,
> may God strike dead, Nevena,
> your old godparents, lele,
> your old godparents.
> May God strike dead, Nevena, lele,
> your old godparents;
>
> Who gave you the name,
> who gave you the name,
> the name of pretty Nevena, lele,
> the name of pretty Nevena.
> Who gave you the name, lele,
> the name of pretty Nevena.
>
> Nevena, marigold in the garden,
> Nevena, marigold in the garden,
> Nevena, mother’s only daughter, lele,
> Nevena, father’s only daughter.
> Nevena, mother’s only daughter, lele,
> Nevena, father’s only daughter.
>
> As the marigold withers in the garden,
> as the marigold withers in the garden,
> may the boys wither, lele,
> for our pretty Nevena.
> May the boys wither, lele,
> for our pretty Nevena.
>
> Note: the word "дејгиди" comes from the Turkish “dey gidi”, and is something like “hey”, but with emotions towards the object.
