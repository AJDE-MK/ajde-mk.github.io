## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Нива ми беше твојто маало' is my reading; literally 'a field was your neighborhood to me', contrast with 'not dear to me' in the next stanza. Vaska is a diminutive of Vasilka.
