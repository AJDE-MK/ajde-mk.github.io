## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Да се кити аџи Зоран' and 'на Ѓурчета' are my reading. The last two lines ('сакаш дојди, сакаш не иди') are unusual wording and probably 'come if you want, stay away if you don't'.
