## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Царе/Царо' is a pet name or a name of the addressed girl, my reading. 'робанчето' (little slave/servant boy, probably the musician) and 'да ги пукнам' (knock out / drive wild) and 'најпојќе' (most of all) are my guesses.
