## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Ју пазува' and 'на лудото што помина' are my reading (the sweetheart/the one who passed on), uncertain; 'пазува' I take as 'guards/watches', 'пазува' could also mean 'carries in the bosom'.
