## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'непара го пијам' is my reading as 'I drink it without paying'; 'аџамија' (here 'green, unbroken') is a Turkish-derived word I am not sure of. 'тук' сум јунак' I read as 'here I am a hero', a guess.
