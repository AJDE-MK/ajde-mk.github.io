## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Unchecked machine translation. This is a revolutionary-era ballad about Taki Daskalot; I treated it as traditional folklore but could not confirm it is not a later composition. 'Сардисаа' as 'surrounded' is my reading.
