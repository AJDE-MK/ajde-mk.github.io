# Василица

*Vasilica* · *Vasilica*

Pesna.org #477: <https://pesna.org/song/477>

## Background

A New Year's Eve round for the Old New Year (St. Basil). The greyed New Year wanders; the household answers at the door with the call "open up, host", and the wishes at midnight are for a good harvest.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Looks like a New Year (Vasilica) carol; that context is my reading from the closing call to the householder. 'Сива' may be a term of address rather than literal grey hair.
