## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> Come on Stojna, you blondie Stojna,
> bring the dance closer to me.
>
> So I can pour you a cup of wine,
> in the glass, a gold ring.
>
> To wear it on Easter day,
> after Easter I will marry you.
>
> On the holidays after Easter,
> so I don’t miss out on the working days.
