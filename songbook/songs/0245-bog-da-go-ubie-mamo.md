## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** A girl wishes ill on the tailor-neighbour for cutting her garment tight, then asks to be given to him; this reading of the humour is mine. 'Џамаданче' is a dialect garment word I am not sure of.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| комшија | neighbour |
| терзија | tailor |
| скрои | cut (cloth), tailored |
| елече | little vest |
| џамаданче | little jacket or bodice (dialect) |
| тенка половина | slender waist |
| поарно | better |
| аман | mercy (Turkish-origin exclamation) |
