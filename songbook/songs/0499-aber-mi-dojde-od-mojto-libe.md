*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The gender of the beloved is ambiguous in the Macedonian text ('либе' is neuter); I rendered it as 'him' (my reading, since the song seems sung by a woman).
