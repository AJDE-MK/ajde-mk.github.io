## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The last line 'назад не греби' is my reading ('will not come back', literally 'not rake/row back'). 'ка ја што' = 'as I do' (dialect). Origin unclear, looks traditional.
