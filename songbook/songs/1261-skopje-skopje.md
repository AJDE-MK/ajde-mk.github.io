## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; apparently about the 1963 Skopje earthquake, but I cannot name an author. 'Свеста ни ја зеде' is my reading: 'took our senses/consciousness'.
