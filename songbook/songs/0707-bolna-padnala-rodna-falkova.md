## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Стројници' read as matchmakers/suitors' envoys (my reading, dialect). 'Кога не ми се' rendered 'When I am not (willing)' is my reading and uncertain; the stanza may be Rodna's reply.
