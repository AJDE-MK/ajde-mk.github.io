## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Бре горска вило' is read as 'forest fairy' used as an affectionate address. 'Каља/Калина' is the same girl. 'Сенка држи' read as holding shade over her; the first line's opening 'ајде' is rendered 'come now'.
