*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; a gurbet (emigrant) style song. 'мој'та / мојта' means 'my' with the definite article attached.
