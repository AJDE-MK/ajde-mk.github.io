## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-style historical song about the 1903 events in Armensko (Armenohori, Lerin region); I cannot name an author. Names (Arni Pasha, Kuzo Blachev, Vlahov, Chakalarov, Kocho) are rendered as written, not checked against history.
