# Ја подај ми моме тенка пушка

*Ja podaj mi mome tenka puška* · *Give me the thin rifle, girl,*

Pesna.org #1607: <https://pesna.org/song/1607>

**Notes.** 1 Каик е кајак, чун, кајче или чамец.

2Караорман (Црна Шума) е планина меѓу струшката котлина, долината на реката Црн Дрим и областа Дебрца, т.е. помеѓу селата Врбјани, 'Ржаново, Делогожди и Ботун.

## English

*From Pesna.org*

> - Give me the thin rifle, girl,
> so I can be on my way.
> Let me leave, girl, let me pass,
> into a foreign land.
>
> - How will you cross, dear, Vardar River,
> muddy and bloody?
> - I will cross, girl, I will leave
> I will get into a boat.
>
> - How will you cross, dear, Shar Mountain,
> full of shepherds?
> - I will cross, girl, I will leave,
> I will play the kaval.
>
> - How will you cross, dear, Karaorman1,
> full of bandits?
> - I will cross, girl, I will leave,
> I will throw a rifle.
