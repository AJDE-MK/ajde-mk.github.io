# Лазаре, Лазаре

*Lazare, Lazare* · *Lazar, Lazar*

Pesna.org #1047: <https://pesna.org/song/1047>

## Background

On Lazarus Saturday (the Saturday before Palm Sunday), groups of girls called *lazarki*, in white with floral wreaths, went door to door singing blessings for the house and vineyard and asking for a white *pita* and two eggs. The best-known line of the genre is the one this song starts with.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Лазаре' is a refrain, likely tied to the Lazarus-Saturday (Lazarica) custom songs, but I am not certain. 'У в' Раткини' read as 'in Ratka's'; Ratka is probably a person's name.
