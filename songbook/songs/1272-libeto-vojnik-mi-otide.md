## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. The last verse is spoken by the soldier; 'јас ќе умрам тебе' (literally 'I will die to you') is read as 'I will die for you', my reading.
