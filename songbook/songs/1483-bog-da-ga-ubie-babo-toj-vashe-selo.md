## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Mixed Serbian-style dialect (куде су ти, твоје, на руку). Guesses: 'буѓава' (scabby/mangy), 'жуљави уста' (sore mouth), 'калешо' (unknown, maybe 'invited/chosen one'), 'приварде' (see to the horse). Origin unclear.
