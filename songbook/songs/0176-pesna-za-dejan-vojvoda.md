## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (patriotic song about a historical Dejan voivode, no author I can name). 'нестите' is probably 'невестите' (brides), 'малихери' my reading as Mannlicher rifles; 'спаилаци' as spahi landlords.
