## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'леле' rendered 'oh my' as a lament interjection. 'Ќе ти умрам' is literally 'I will die on you', an idiom of grief or devotion.
