## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional ballad. Dialect forms (са, у нејно, как') are Southern/Western. Tar and oil were used to disguise her; 'мангусар' I translate as rascal/vagabond, fairly sure.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| грабиле | seized, abducted |
| мангусари | rascals, vagabonds |
| намачкале | smeared |
| зејтин | oil |
| катран | tar |
| просија | begging |
| дарувај | give (alms) |
| пресно брашно | fresh flour |
| ѓупка | derogatory word for a Roma woman |
| тук' | but (dialect) |
