*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'На Цинциеви' as 'at the Cincis' place' is my reading. 'Да пукнам, ич не се жалам' read as 'let me burst, I do not complain at all'.
