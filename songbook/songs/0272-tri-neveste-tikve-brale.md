## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Aleksey Dvorecki)*

> Three brides picked pumpkins, hey
> Three brides picked pumpkins, hey, hey, hey
> They picked pumpkins, hey!
>
> They fell asleep on the spot, hey
> They fell asleep on the spot, hey, hey, hey
> On the spot, hey!
>
> Their mother-in-law woke them up, hey
> - Hey you, lazy brides, hey, hey, hey
> Come and stand up, hey!
>
> The brides got angry, hey
> So they sweared, hey, hey, hey
> So they cursed, hey!
>
> The first shouted: Hey you, mother-in-law!
> Hey you, fat mother-in-law!
>
> The second shouted: Hey you, father-in-law!
> Hey you, father-in-law, wild boar!
