## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. The wordplay is trlo/grlo, ovci/oci, cifliko/liceto: she says she is worth as much as everything he owns. 'Деј гиди' rendered 'come on' (my reading).

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| трло | sheepfold, pen |
| овци | sheep |
| чифлик | farmstead, estate |
| будало | fool (vocative) |
| деј гиди | hey, you (exclamation) |
| грло | throat |
| очи | eyes |
| лице | face |
