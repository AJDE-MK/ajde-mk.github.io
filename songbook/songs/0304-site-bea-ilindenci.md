## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; reads like a 20th-century patriotic song but I cannot name an author. 'Кукушанец' means a man from Kukush (Kilkis); 'Мечки Камен' is the place name Mechkin Kamen; the line about the clod in his chest is my reading.
