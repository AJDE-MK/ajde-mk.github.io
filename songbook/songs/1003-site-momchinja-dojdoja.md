## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. 'топ' here means a bolt of cloth, not a cannon. 'пуст да остане' is my reading: 'let it remain unwanted'.
