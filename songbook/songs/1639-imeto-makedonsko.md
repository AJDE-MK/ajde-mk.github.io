## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> In the distant 1913th,
> the border divided our homeland,
> changed our names, banned our language,
> tortured us, shamed us, until they forced us to leave.
>
> Now we are abroad, in free Canada,
> we will not be afraid to speak our native language,
> we will have faith in our church,
> and we will proudly bear the Macedonian name.
>
> We came as peasants with a suitcase in our hands and without money,
> we found work and quickly became citizens.
> Buff, German and Banica, they remained in our dreams,
> with beautiful memories of times gone by.
>
> Now we are abroad, in free Canada,
> we will not be afraid to speak our native language,
> we will have faith in our church,
> and we will proudly bear the Macedonian name.
>
> We are Macedonians and we are all proud of the new sovereign Macedonian republic.
> There are three parts left in Pirin, Aegean, Albania
> that still do not have justice, like us in Canada.
>
> Now we are abroad, in free Canada,
> we will not be afraid to speak our native language,
> we will have faith in our church,
> and we will proudly bear the Macedonian name.
