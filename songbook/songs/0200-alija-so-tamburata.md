## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'калешите невести' rendered 'betrothed brides' is my reading ('калеш' = invited/engaged). 'алал ти биле момите' read as 'blessings on the girls', my reading. Verb endings in -ит (викат, чинит, свирит) are dialect.
