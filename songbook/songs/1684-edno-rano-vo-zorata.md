## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Early at dawn,
> the village of D'mbeni was besieged;
> battle started in the dead of night.
>
> Grandfather Mitre twirls his mustache,
> Pando Klasho looks on, amused.
>
> Vangel Vlao beats the drum,
> Haji Asa is breathing his last.
>
> The comitadji emerged
> and shouted to the Turks:
>
> Aghas and Pashas,
> come on then, try to catch us!
