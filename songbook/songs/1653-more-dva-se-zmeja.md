## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Hey, there are two dragons, they are flying in the sky.
> A ruddy river flows below them,
> below them flows a ruddy river.
>
> Hey, under them flows the ruddy river,
> straight it goes to the city of Thessaloniki.
>
> Hey, flowing straight to Thessaloniki city,
> to Thessaloniki, right past the dungeons.
>
> Hey, in the dungeons there is water up to the knees,
> hey, in the water, there is a hero.
