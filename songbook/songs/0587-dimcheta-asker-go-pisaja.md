# Димчета аскер го писаја

*Dimčeta asker go pisaja* · *They Enlisted Dimche as a Soldier*

Pesna.org #587: <https://pesna.org/song/587>

## Background

Social history in dialogue form. The authorities conscript young Dimče; in three years he is betrothed and married in three evenings. The bride goes straight to the governor's court and offers florins from her own throat to free him. The answer: the emperor does not want florins, he wants young men like Dimče who know how to carry a rifle and swing a sabre.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*
