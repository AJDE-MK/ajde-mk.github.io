## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Patriotic emigrant song, origin unclear. 'Еди Куле' is the Yedi Kule (Salonica) prison; 'Кукуш' is the town Kilkis in Greek Macedonia.
