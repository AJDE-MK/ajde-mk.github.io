## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; sounds like a newer composed party song, but I cannot name an author. 'Рана душевна' is literally 'wound of the soul'; it may be meant as 'balm' (my reading).
