## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional humorous song; the lad seems to be a hazelnut, which the mice eat, my reading. 'Месив зелен зелник' (kneaded a green zelnik) is slightly odd, my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| лешник | hazelnut |
| зелник | pie with greens (leek/cabbage/spinach) |
| орање | ploughing |
| ридче | little hill |
| скутале | apron / lap of the skirt (dialect form) |
| амбар | granary, storehouse |
| глувци | mice (dialect; standard глувци = deaf men, my reading) |
| изгризаа | they gnawed off |
