## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, sounds like a modern or semi-composed song but I cannot name an author. 'пасој да менвиш' is my reading (something like 'change the burial cloth/passport?'); 'посврши' is my reading of 'married off'. Several dialect forms (најдвевме, кажвавме).
