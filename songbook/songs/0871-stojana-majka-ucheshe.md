## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional haiduk/komita song. 'Куќа не врти' is my reading as 'doesn't keep a household'; 'кумита' is a dialect spelling of комита.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| комита/кумита | komita, guerrilla fighter |
| учеше | advised, taught (imperfect) |
| куќа | house, home |
| врти | keeps going, turns (here: runs/keeps) |
| нарами | slung over the shoulder |
| рамо | shoulder |
| планина | mountain |
| замина | left, set off |
