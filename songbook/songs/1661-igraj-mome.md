## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Dance, dance, you girl,
> let the earth shake,
> only when you dance,
> my heart trembles.
>
> Come on, girl, give me your hands,
> and dance on the dance floor,
> ra-ta-ta-ta-ta-tam,
> come on, dance chochek1,
> lala-la-la-la-la-la,
> I have a great desire, you know.
>
> You have a tall and slim body,
> take everything you want from me,
> only when you dance,
> my heart trembles.
>
> Come on, girl, give me your hands,
> and dance on the dance floor,
> ra-ta-ta-ta-ta-tam,
> come on, dance chochek,
> lala-la-la-la-la-la,
> I have a great desire, you know.
