## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> This soul only knows
> how much love I keep for you.
> A tear shines for you
> I have very sad days.
>
> Zhaklina (Jacqueline), my love,
> Zhaklina, my desire.
>
> Years, summers have passed,
> where are you, I want to see you.
> Think, you were happy,
> long nights I love you dear.
>
> Zhaklina, my love,
> Zhaklina, my desire.
>
> Sing a song,
> it's hard for me, my soul burns.
> Sadness is not easy
> when I sit alone until dawn.
>
> Zhaklina, my love,
> Zhaklina, my desire.
