## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Е' in 'лулела е' is a dialect auxiliary; 'је' in 'го је лулела' is a dialect form of the auxiliary/pronoun, my reading. Stanza 2 is a curse-wish on the child.
