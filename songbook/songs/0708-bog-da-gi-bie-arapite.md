## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Куци Арапи' is my reading as 'wretched/lame Arabs'. Dovledzik is a place name near Bitola, left as written.
