## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> The white monasteries
> are up for sale,
> the white monasteries, dear mother,
> the black nuns.
>
> There was none
> to buy the monasteries,
> to buy the monasteries, dear mother,
> and build a street with stores.
>
> Finally there was
> the young unmarried boy,
> to buy the monasteries, dear mother,
> and build a street with stores.
