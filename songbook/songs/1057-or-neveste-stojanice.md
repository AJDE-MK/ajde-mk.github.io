## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Несте' is a dialect vocative of 'невесто'. 'Да ми пукнат ... твоите очи' in the first oath is my reading: the line seems to say 'may your eyes burst', and I kept it as written.
