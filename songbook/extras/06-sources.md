# Where the extra notes came from

The song pages are built from Pesna.org. These notes were added from two earlier drafts:

- **Your 6-song songbook** (`Macedonian Folk Songbook.md`): background for 6 traditional songs and 4 Hristovski songs, plus the Ilinden notes and the transliteration key.
- **Qwen's draft** (`makedonski-pesni/`): background for about 45 songs, a glossary, a ritual calendar, regional notes and a list of open questions.

## What I took

Short background notes, listening links, the glossary and the guide material. Each song page says which draft its notes came from.

## What I left out

- Qwen's lyrics and English translations. The archive already has the Cyrillic, and the English on the song pages is Pesna.org's or my own.
- Claims I could not check. Examples: who arranged *Македонско девојче*, and which rock band redid *Јовано, Јованке*.
- Qwen's claim that its Cyrillic was copied from Wikisource. I could not confirm that.

## What is unchecked

The notes are summaries, not scholarship. Places and dates in the Qwen notes are as Qwen gave them. The links marked "from Qwen's notes" are specific YouTube videos that I could not open to check.
