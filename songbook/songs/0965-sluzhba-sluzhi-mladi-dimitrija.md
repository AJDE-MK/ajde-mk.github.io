## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. 'Служба служи' is my reading as 'is in service / does his duty'; 'име си чека' I read as awaiting his name day. 'Да нè служи' is 'to serve us'.
