## English

*From Pesna.org*

> I went out, mother, for a walk,
> I went out, mother, for a walk,
> along the D'mbeni1 meadows, mother,
> by the Kosinec2 springs, mother.
>
> There I found, mother, my love,
> there I found, mother, my love,
> with another lover, mother, she was speaking,
> my words, mother, she was saying.
>
> So I felt, mother, very bad,
> and I felt, mother, very sad,
> so I cursed him, mother, bitterly,
> and I cursed him, mother, bitterly.
>
> - Nine years, love, may you lie down,
> nine years, love, may you lie down,
> nine beds, love, may you change,
> nine pillows, love, may you throw away!
