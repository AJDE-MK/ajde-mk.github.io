# Девојко, мори девојко

*Devojko, mori devojko* · *Girl, hey girl*

Pesna.org #1505: <https://pesna.org/song/1505>

**Notes.** - Ибришим (турски: ibrişim) е тенок свилен или памучен конец за шиење или везење.

- Коприна е свила.

- Алајки (турски: halayık) се слугинки.

- Поли се долните делови од облека со две страни.

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Girl, hey girl
> the artisan sent me to you
> to give me some silk,
> some silk.
>
> You'll give me some silk,
> he wants silk
> the artisan will make you
> a wide dress that touches the ground.
>
> - Hey apprentice, you hero,
> where did the artisan see me,
> where did he see me
> where did he took my measure?
>
> - Girl, hey girl,
> when he was going to the spa
> with twelve female servants,
> with thirteen female slaves.
>
> The servants were sweeping the road,
> the slaves were knitting.
> It was then when the artisan saw you,
> and took your measure from your shadow.
