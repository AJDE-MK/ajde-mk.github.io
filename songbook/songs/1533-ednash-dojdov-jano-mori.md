## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Ваше село' uses the polite/plural form, probably just 'your village'. 'Виеше' (from вие 'twist') is used for dancing the oro. Origin unclear, looks traditional.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| ваше село | your (pl./polite) village |
| дома | at home |
| оро | round dance, the dance |
| виеше | you danced (the oro) |
| танец | dance |
| девојки | girls |
| невести | brides, young wives |
| ергени | bachelors |
| душо | my soul (endearment) |
