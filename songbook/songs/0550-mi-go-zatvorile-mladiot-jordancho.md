## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Коски до рамена' is literally 'bones to the shoulders' (my reading: skeletal remains or a grim image). 'Ми' in verbs is a narrative filler.
