## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional pastoral song, no author known. 'Двечки' I read as an affectionate form of 'two'; 'мори' is a filler, here part of the name refrain 'Maro oh Maro'.
