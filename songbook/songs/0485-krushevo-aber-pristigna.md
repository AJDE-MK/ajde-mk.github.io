# Крушево абер пристигна

*Kruševo aber pristigna* · *News Reached Krushevo*

Pesna.org #485: <https://pesna.org/song/485>

## Background

A call goes out for families to send their grown sons to fight at Smilevo. Stojan has no sons, only his daughter Todorka. She asks her father for a Berdan rifle (*berdanka*, a Russian-made rifle common among the rebels) and a saber, joins the *komiti*, and fights for three years without anyone realizing she is a girl. See the Ilinden background in the guide *History, places and customs*.
