## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional bawdy song. Line 2, 'на љупење кочање', is a guess (my reading); 'жити теткина' is a term of endearment, roughly 'auntie dear'.
