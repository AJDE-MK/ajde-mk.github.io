## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, no author known. 'Годину да му направам' I read as holding the one-year memorial for him; 'Иван Планина' is Ivan Mountain, as written. Standard-sounding Macedonian.
