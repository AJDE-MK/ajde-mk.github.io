## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Лудо' for the mother is my reading as 'foolish' (affectionate or scolding). 'Не ме даваат' could mean 'will not let me go' or 'will not give me in marriage'. 'Маре земиме' is my reading as 'take each other'.
