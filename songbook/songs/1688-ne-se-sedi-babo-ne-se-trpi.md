# Не се седи, бабо, не се трпи

## English

*From Pesna.org*

> - I cannot sit still, Grandma, I cannot endure it,
> this bachelor’s life of mine, Grandma.
> I sit all night, Grandma, upon a pile of wood,
> a whole load of timber, Grandma, that I’ve ached,
> and I think, Grandma, only of her.
>
> - You sit all night, my son, upon the wood,
> did you see, my son, who passed by?
> - I saw them, Grandma, I saw them on the path,
> passing by, Grandma, moving on,
> the carters from Zhernovo, Grandma.
>
> The youngest carter, Grandma,
> was singing my song, Grandma.
> And she wept for me, Grandma,
> he never tires of it, Grandma,
> filling a whole cup with tears, Grandma.
>
> She fills a cup with tears, Grandma,
> soaking her thin shirt, Grandma.
> I keep thinking, Grandma, only of her,
> all about her, Grandma, always for her.
