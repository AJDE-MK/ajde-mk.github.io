## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Ѓулави јаб'лка' (round apples) is likely a metaphor for breasts or for untouched purity; that is my reading. 'Да сториме здраво-живо' rendered as 'to say farewell' (my reading).
