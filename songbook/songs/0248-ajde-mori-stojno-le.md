*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Heavily dialectal and garbled text; 'урва мој воѓеало' (I render 'ox-cart overturned') is a guess, and 'мој' is a filler particle I mostly dropped. 'се фати ... волите' as 'got stuck' is also my reading.
