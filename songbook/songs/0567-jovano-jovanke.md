# Јовано, Јованке

*Jovano, Jovanke* · *Jovano, Jovanke*

Pesna.org #567: <https://pesna.org/song/567>

## Background

Two young lovers are kept apart by the girl's disapproving parents. She bleaches linen on the banks of the Vardar, the river that runs through the middle of Macedonia. It is sung across the Balkans, in North Macedonia, Bulgaria, Greek Macedonia and former Yugoslavia, and is probably the most-covered Macedonian song. *Mori* and *dušo* are endearments, roughly "hey girl" and "my soul". The song is short and plain, which is much of its power.

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Jovano, Jovanke
> you are sitting by the River Vardar,
> washing the white linens
> washing the white linens, dear
> and looking up all the time.
>
> Jovano, Jovanke
> I am waiting for you
> to come to my home
> but you are not coming, dear
> my heart, Jovano.
>
> Jovano, Jovanke
> your mother
> is not letting you
> to come to me, dear
> my heart, Jovano.
