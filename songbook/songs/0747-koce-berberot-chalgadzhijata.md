## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Koce the barber, the musician,
> Koce the barber, the passionate one.
> Koce, Koce does not eat salted meat (no!),
> Koce, Koce does not eat beans with preserved meat.
>
> (Oh, tasty beans!)
>
> Koce the barber, the musician,
> Koce the barber, the passionate one.
> Koce eats roasted goose, hey,
> goose roasted on a spit turned.
> Goose, goose roasted, on a spit turned
> with raisins the whole goose filled.
>
> (Koce with the large moustache, who likes only the good things!)
>
> Koce the barber, the musician,
> Koce the barber, the passionate one.
> Koce, Koce does not drink mild rakiya (no!),
> Koce, Koce does not drink sour wine.
>
> (Hey, Koce is choosy about what he eats,
> Koce is choosy about what he drinks,
> he is a real gourmet, hey, hey!)
>
> Koce the barber, the musician,
> Koce the barber, the passionate one.
> Koce, Koce drinks strong rakiya, hey,
> Koce, Koce drinks aged wine.
>
> If you want him to play for you,
> give Koce what he wants.
> Koce will play and sing for you,
> old and young will rejoice in the dance, hey!
