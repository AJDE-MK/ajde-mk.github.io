*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, Ottoman-era setting. 'Бегу' in line 4 I read as an address to the bey, my reading; 'дружина' probably means a haiduk band.
