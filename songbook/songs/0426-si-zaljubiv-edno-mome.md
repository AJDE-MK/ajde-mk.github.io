## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'понадица' is my reading (a treat or gift she asks for); I am not certain of the word. The last two lines are my reading: he offers money to the bearers to set her down.
