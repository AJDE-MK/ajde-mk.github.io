## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Mariovo song about a girl sold to Ali Pasha at Ioannina; the line 'Ти побело дојде' is my reading (you came out whiter than the brides). 'Тија ти зедова' I render 'Those we took' (my reading, unsure).
