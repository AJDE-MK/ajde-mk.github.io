## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> May you be damned, mother,
> why did you give birth to me poor, Mother?
> Why did you give birth to me poor, Mother,
> without money, and, mother, without luck?
>
> Then you sent me to Australia,
> to the famous city of Sydney, mother.
>
> I went out for a walk, Mother,
> through the streets of Sydney, mother.
> There I met two strangers,
> they were speaking Macedonian, mother.
>
> They were speaking Macedonian, mother,
> and bitterly cursing their mothers, oh my.
> Bitterly cursing their mothers, oh my,
> shedding bloody tears, mother.
>
> May you be damned, mother
> why did you send me to Australia?
> Why did you send me to Australia,
> to the famous city of Sydney, Mother?
