## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Folk song about Gotse Delchev; no author known to me. 'Бегче' is a diminutive of 'beg' (bey); 'Малеш' is the Maleshevo mountain region.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| развивај | unfurl, leaf out |
| горо | forest (vocative) |
| војвода | voivode, rebel chieftain |
| бегче | young bey (Ottoman noble) |
| сејмени | seymen, Ottoman guard soldiers |
| фатете | catch (pl. imperative) |
| погубвај | kill, destroy |
| комити | komitas, rebel fighters |
| замоли | begged |
