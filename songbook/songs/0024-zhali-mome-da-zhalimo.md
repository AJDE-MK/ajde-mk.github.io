## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Скендерија' is a place name (Skenderija, I take it as a foreign city). The line '(Друга верзија)' is translated as '(Another version)'; 'оти' in the second version means 'because' (dialect).
