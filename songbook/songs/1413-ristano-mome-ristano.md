## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> - Ristana, maiden Ristana,
> so you’ll know girl, so you’ll know
> that your mother engaged you off.
>
> To a big village,
> to a rich home,
> to a young lad.
>
> - Damned be that village,
> let a fire burn down the house,
> let a bullet strike the young lad.
