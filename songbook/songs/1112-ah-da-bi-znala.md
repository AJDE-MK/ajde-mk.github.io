## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Луди, млади' rendered as 'wild young ones' (my reading); 'рано што пејат' as 'that sing early' (my reading, since рано = early and ранам = feed are wordplay). The source has some Latin letters typed in the Cyrillic text (ќe, сo, Ax), and 'саз ќe то ранав' is read as 'јаз ќе го ранав'.
