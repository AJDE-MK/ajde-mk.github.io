## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** WWII partisan song, no author named, so translated. 'убај' is read as 'lovely, handsome' (my reading); 'балисти' refers to the Albanian nationalist Balli Kombetar.
