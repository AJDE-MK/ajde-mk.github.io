## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> When you laid down, Dana,
> why didn't you tell me, Dano?
>
> I offer to bring to you
> an apple from Tetovo.
>
> That was grown at the top of a branch,
> that was stolen by moonlight,
> carried into my bosom, Dana.
