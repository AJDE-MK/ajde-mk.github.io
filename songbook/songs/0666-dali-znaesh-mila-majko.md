## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, reads like a 20th-century composed song but I cannot name an author. 'проклета да си од либето' is my reading: 'cursed be you for the sweetheart (who parted us)'.
