## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Стројник' as matchmaker and 'зацрни' as 'make me wear black / blacken my life' are my reading. 'Станаа три години' taken as 'it has been three years'.
