## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> A girl walked off from harvesting
> To her brother she said:
> - My dear brother, dear brother Stojan
> Give me some cold spring-water
> My dear brother, dear brother Stojan
> Find me some dense shade.
>
> Stojan spoke and replied
> To his sister he said:
> - My dear sister, dear Elena
> There is no cold spring-water here
> My dear sister, dear sister Elena
> Nor is there any dense shade.
>
> Stojan spoke and replied
> To his sister he said:
> - Come to the green mountain forest
> Fetch some cold spring-water
> Come to the green mountain forest
> Over there, there is dense shade.
