## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Пусти ми биле ергени' is my reading ('the bachelors were left empty/forlorn'); 'нф' in the text is a typo for 'оф'.
