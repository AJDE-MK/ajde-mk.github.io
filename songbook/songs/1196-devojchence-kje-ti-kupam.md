## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Дуњата цреша не бива' is a proverb-like image (quince cannot turn into cherry); my reading is that it means she will not be changed by gifts.
