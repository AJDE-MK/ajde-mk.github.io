## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Host, good guests have come to you,
> ah, they have brought you good news.
>
> Host, your flock has lambed,
> ah, half of them are tweens.
>
> Host, your wife has given birth,
> ah, she has given birth to a son.
>
> Host, today we eat and drink,
> ah, today we have a wedding.
>
> If you are sleeping, host, wake up,
> ah, if you are drinking, host, cheer up,
> ah, if you are drinking, cheer up.
