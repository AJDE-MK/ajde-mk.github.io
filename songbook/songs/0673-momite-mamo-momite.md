## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Љубов да водат' is literally 'to lead love'; my reading is 'carry on a courtship'. 'На знајат' is taken as a dialect or typo form of 'не знаат'.

**Words to learn**
