## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Maria, pretty white Maria,
> Maria, pretty white Maria,
> why are you gently weaving, and crying,
> why are you gently weaving, and crying?
>
> Is your loom not working,
> is your loom not working,
> or are your threads broken,
> or is your thread holder broken?
>
> Neighter my loom not working,
> neighter my loom not working,
> nor my threads are broken,
> nor my threads are broken.
>
> Neighter my threads are broken,
> neighter my threads are broken,
> nor my thread holder is broken,
> but my darling is far away.
