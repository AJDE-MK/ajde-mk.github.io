## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional; Konopishte is a village in the Kavadarci area. 'риџа' and 'калеш' are dialect words I am unsure of; 'калеш' I rendered 'guest-welcoming' as a guess (it is a Turkish-origin word for an invitation, my reading).
