## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Ilinden-uprising song about Pitu Guli (Mečkin Kamen, Kruševo, 1903); treated as traditional. 'Горе Крушево' rendered as 'above Kruševo' in my reading.
