## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Криво магаре' translated as 'lame donkey' (my reading; literally 'crooked donkey'). 'Ќе ме раскости' is rendered as 'will tear me apart' (my reading, roughly 'will pull my bones apart').
