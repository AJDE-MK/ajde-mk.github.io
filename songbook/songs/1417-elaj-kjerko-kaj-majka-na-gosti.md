## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The last stanza is hard to follow (dialect: 'јет' = 'is', 'вркнит' = push or throw, 'желки' = grief). Speaker and meaning of the last stanza are my reading only; it seems to be a stepmother or wife speaking harshly. Origin unclear, treated as traditional.
