## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Commemorates the village of Buf (Aegean Macedonia); origin unclear, no named author. 'прекрстија', 'истепаја' and 'игроорки' (read as 'dancers') are my reading; 'клетва' may mean a vow or a curse.
