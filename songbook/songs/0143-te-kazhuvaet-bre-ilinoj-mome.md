## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> People praise you, Ilina girl
> that you can knit so thin
> and that you can weave so precise.
> I will give you, Ilina girl,
> a hundred drams of cotton,
> a hundred drams of cotton.
>
> You will weave for me, Ilina girl,
> three hundred cubits of canvas,
> three hundred cubits of canvas.
> With whatever is left, Ilina girl,
> weave for me one white towel,
> one white towel.
>
> People praise you, silversmith Peter (or George),
> that you are a great craftsman,
> that you are a great craftsman.
> I will give you, silversmith Peter
> one old coin,
> one old coin.
>
> You will craft for me, silversmith Peter,
> one silver loom,
> one silver loom.
> With whatever is left, silversmith Peter,
> one silver ring,
> one silver ring.
>
> * Weight units: 100 drams is about 177 grams. Length units: 300 cubits is about 144 meters.
