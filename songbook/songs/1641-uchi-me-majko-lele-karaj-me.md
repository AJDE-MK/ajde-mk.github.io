## English

*From Pesna.org*

> - Teach me, mother, oh, tell me,
> how to get her, oh, Bojana.
> - I'm teaching you, son, oh, telling you
> how to get her, oh, Bojana.
>
> Go to the city, oh, Bitola,
> buy yourself a plow, oh, oxen.
> Buy yourself a plow, oh, oxen,
> plow a garden, oh, in the middle of the village.
>
> Plow a garden, oh, in the middle of the village,
> plant flowers, oh, all sorts.
> Girls, brides, oh, will come,
> hopefully Bojana, oh, will come.
>
> - Girls, brides, oh, they all came
> white Bojana, oh, didn't come!
