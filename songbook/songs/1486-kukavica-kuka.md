## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The language mixes Serbian-style forms (је, њума, ко, кроз) with Macedonian dialect; 'њума' is a dialect dative 'her', my reading. 'Не је Тодор, тој си је Тодора' is my reading: the empress says the soldier is really a woman, Todora. Cigarettes (цигаре) are probably tobacco, my reading.
