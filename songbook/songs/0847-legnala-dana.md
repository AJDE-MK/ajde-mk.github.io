## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Alan Moore)*

> Dana lay down and fell asleep, God,
> in the uncle's garden,
> under an olive tree.
>
> A wind blowing from the sea, God,
> broke an olive branch,
> hit Dana on the forehead.
>
> Dana began to cry, God
> - Oh, lele, God, God,
> how can I go home?
>
> When I go home, God,
> how can I tell my mother?
> Mother will tell father.
