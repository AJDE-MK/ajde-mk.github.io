# Што ти текна, Јано, да побегнеш

*Što ti tekna, Jano, da pobegneš* · *What Got Into You, Jana, to Run Away*

Pesna.org #772: <https://pesna.org/song/772>

## Background

Another version of the cheeky wedding song about a bride who runs away at dawn; see #440 for the story.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Лешник' and 'два лешника' are my reading: hazelnut or a man's name/nickname (Leshnik); 'гламно' (head, dialect) and 'мангари' (coins) are guesses. Turla and Vranestica are place names.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| текна | occurred to, got into (one's head) |
| побегнеш | you run away, elope |
| натема | curse, damn |
| бож' уби те | may God strike you |
| многу пари | a lot of money |
| мангари | mangar (small copper coin) |
