## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional revolutionary (komita) song. 'Чифте пиштоле' as 'double-barreled pistols' is my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| като | when (dialect, = кога) |
| ќе ј'умреш | you will die (dialect, = ќе умреш) |
| носи | carries |
| тенка пушка | slender rifle |
| чифте пиштоле | pair of pistols |
| острата сабја | the sharp sabre |
| војвода | voivode, rebel band leader |
| браќа | brothers |
