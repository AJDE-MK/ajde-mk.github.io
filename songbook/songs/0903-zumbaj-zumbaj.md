## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Humorous song, 'zumbaj' is a refrain I leave untranslated. 'У петак си преседесмо' (sat it out) and 'кума прати' (sends off the godfather) are my reading; the lyrics mix Serbian-style forms (Leskovac, Nis).

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| замеса | to knead (dough) |
| бања | bath, spa |
| кум | godfather, best man |
| снашка / сна | bride, daughter-in-law |
| свекр | father-in-law |
| мераклија | bon vivant, someone who enjoys life's pleasures |
| се чуди | to wonder, be amazed |
| одма | right away |
