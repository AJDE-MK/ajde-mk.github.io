## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** About Goce Delchev's mother (Sultana); origin unclear. In stanza 3 the second 'гордеј се што си родила' is rendered as a fragment to keep the line count; the sense continues 'the greatest son'.
