## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional soldier's farewell song, origin unclear. 'је' in 'је реков' is dialect for 'ѝ' (to her), my reading; 'Ленче бре' is an address to a name (Lenche).
