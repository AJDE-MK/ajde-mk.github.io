## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear: a tribute-style song about Goce Delchev (born in Kukush), no author I can name. 'Никола срце разврзал' is my reading ('Nikola unties his heart', i.e. rejoices).
