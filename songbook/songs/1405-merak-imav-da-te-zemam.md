## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'стројник' is my reading of a wedding-related go-between; the verb forms in -ја (дадоја, даваја) are dialect. Origin unclear, looks traditional.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| мерак | longing, desire, keen wish |
| земам | I take (here: to marry) |
| пратив | I sent |
| стројник | wedding official/matchmaker who arranges the match (my reading) |
| дадоја | they gave (dialect form) |
| пројде | passed |
| зедов | I took |
