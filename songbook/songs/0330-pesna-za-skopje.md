# Песна за Скопје

*Pesna za Skopje* · *Song for Skopje*

Pesna.org #330: <https://pesna.org/song/330>

## Background

A modern song written as a folk lament after the 1963 earthquake that destroyed Skopje. The Vardar ran muddy with tears; one sorrowful morning the earth shook and took the child, Skopje, from the mother's lap. Macedonia lives and will bear a new Skopje. There is a second song with the same title under #131.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; it clearly refers to the 1963 Skopje earthquake. The third line, 'деца кој ги зема', is my reading: 'who took the children'.
