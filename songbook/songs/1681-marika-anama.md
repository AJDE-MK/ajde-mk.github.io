## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Alija desired
> Marika as his bride,
> oh, oh, oh, alas,
> for her to become his anama1.
>
> So he sent matchmakers,
> and a bag of gold coins,
> oh, oh, oh, alas,
> for her to become his anama.
>
> When her father heard,
> he loaded his Berdan rifle,
> oh, oh, oh, alas,
> to kill Alija.
>
> Her mother wept,
> Marika swore an oath,
> oh, oh, oh, alas,
> she would not become his anama,
> oh, oh, oh, alas,
> she would not change her faith.
