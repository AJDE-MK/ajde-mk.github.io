## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional revolutionary-style song. 'Ѓолот да сардисаат' is my reading (to crush/overwhelm the bandits); the word ѓол is unclear to me and may be a dialect term.
