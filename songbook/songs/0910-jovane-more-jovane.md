## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Коконо' (address to a lady) is my reading; 'ќе ти запалам дуќанот' lines are literal. 'терзивче' is a diminutive of тerzija (tailor).
