## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The song is a dialogue between the boy and the girl. 'Загин'вам' is my reading of a dialect form.

**Words to learn**

| девојче | girl |
