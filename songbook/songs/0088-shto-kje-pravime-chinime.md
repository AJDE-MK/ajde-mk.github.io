## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, origin unclear. 'вој мерак ш'о го имаме' and 'до два изгора' are my reading (the second perhaps 'two words burned like two coals'); 'ногу' is dialect for 'многу' (much).
