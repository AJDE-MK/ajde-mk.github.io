## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Фати за р'ка' is rendered 'took by the hand'; 'Македонче' as 'young Macedonian' (diminutive). 'Тино' is a girl's name in dialect (my reading).

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| се забави | was delayed, took long |
| пошчо | why (dialect) |
| опитваш | you question, ask |
| табур | battalion (Turkish loan) |
| аскер | army, soldiers (Turkish loan) |
| тргаше | was setting out, riding off |
| фрли | threw himself, leapt |
| меџидија | Ottoman silver coin |
