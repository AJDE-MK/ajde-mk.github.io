## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Please give me, my love, a glass of wine,
> to drink, my love, please let me drink.
>
> - Oh, the old father is standing at the gate
> and watching, my love, he is watching.
>
> - Both your father, and my grandfather,
> they don't see, my love, they don't see.
>
> - Oh, my mother is standing at the gate
> and watching, my love, she is watching.
>
> - Both your mother, and my grandmother,
> they don't see, my love, they don't see.
