## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I have been a tormented child,
> I have not cried a single tear,
> only one weight
> torn a man's heart:
> my great love
> was given for another.
>
> Crack, my heart like Shar Mountain,
> into a hundred pieces,
> what will my heart do when I have
> no one to love.
> God grant me at least to grow old,
> to forget.
> People say time heals everything,
> come on, make me old.
>
> I haven't seen much while I was a bachelor,
> living alone with an old mother, I haven't gone to the city,
> and her poor soul,
> didn't live long enough to see me with a bride.
> A dilapidated house remained
> like that, unpainted.
>
> Crack, my heart like Shar Mountain,
> into a hundred pieces,
> what will my heart do when I have
> no one to love.
> God grant me at least to grow old,
> to forget.
> People say time heals everything,
> come on, make me old.
