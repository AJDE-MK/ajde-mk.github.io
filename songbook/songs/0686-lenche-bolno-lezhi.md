## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Lenche lies sick
> for three years
> and nine months.
>
> Above her stands
> her old mother
> and speaks to Lenche:
>
> - Get up, Lenche, get up
> the matchmakers have come for you
> the matchmakers from Skopje.
>
> - As they have come, mother,
> so let them leave.
> I already have, mother
> a love from Strumica.
