## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Comic dialogue between the two families' in-laws. 'Ја гу пратив' is dialect for 'I sent her'; 'ич не ја бива' is 'she is no good at all'. 'Black in-law' (црни свате) is an insult, my reading. Traditional.

**Words to learn**

| --- | --- |
