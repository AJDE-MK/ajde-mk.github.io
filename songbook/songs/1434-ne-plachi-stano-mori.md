# Не плачи Стано мори

*Ne plači Stano mori* · *Don't cry, Stana, hey*

Pesna.org #1434: <https://pesna.org/song/1434>

**Notes.** Сновење е приготвување основа за ткаење. Саја е женска облека без ракави, вид елече со везови и украси.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding women's song. 'топар' I render as 'bundle' (my reading); 'Пусто да остане' is a curse-like phrase, my reading 'may it be left empty/damned'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| сајче | sayak, sleeveless embroidered woman's jacket |
| срмено | made with silver/gold thread |
