## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The 'Друга верзија' and 'Магла пада нане' lines are translated as ordinary lines. 'Или ме жени, или ме доми' is my reading: 'marry me off or send me to a husband's house'. The 'flower divides the cheeks' line is my reading. In the first version 'лудо' is read as 'young man'.
