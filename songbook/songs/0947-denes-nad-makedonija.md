## English

*From Pesna.org*

> (Anthem of the Republic of Macedonia)
>
> Today over Macedonia is born
> the new sun of liberty.
> Macedonians are fighting
> for their own rights.
>
> Now once again the flag flutters,
> (that) of the Krushevo Republic,
> of Goce Delchev, Pitu Guli,
> Dame Gruev, Sandanski.
>
> The Macedonian woods resoundly sing
> new songs and news:
> Macedonia liberated
> lives in liberty!
