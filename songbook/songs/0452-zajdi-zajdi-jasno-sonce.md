# Зајди, зајди јасно сонце

*Zajdi, zajdi jasno sonce* · *Set, set, clear sun*

Pesna.org #452: <https://pesna.org/song/452>

## Background

A lament for lost youth, not for a lover. The singer tells the sun and moon to go dark and calls the forest her sister. The forest's leaves will grow back each spring; her youth never will. It is one of the most performed slow songs in the repertoire, recorded by everyone from Aleksandar Sarievski to Toše Proeski. Qwen's draft adds that it is usually sung as a mother's lament, and that other versions give the reason as wanting her son not to be sent to war (unchecked).

*Source of these notes: your 6-song songbook, with a variant noted from Qwen's draft.*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Set, set, clear Sun,
> set to dim
> and you bright Moon,
> begone, drown yourself.
>
> Blacken forest, blacken sister,
> let's both blacken.
> You, for your leaves, forest,
> I, for my youth.
>
> Your leaves, forest, sister,
> will come back again.
> My youth, forest, sister
> will not come back.

Also on Pesna.org: Dutch, French, German, Turkish, Russian, Polish, Greek.
