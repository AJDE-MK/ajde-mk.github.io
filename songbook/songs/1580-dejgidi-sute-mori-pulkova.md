## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional; 'Пулкова' is probably a surname or 'daughter-in-law of the Pulko family' (my reading). 'полтата' and 'јанакија' are dialect words I am unsure of; I kept 'janakija' untranslated and 'coat' is a guess.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| дејгиди | oh, alas (exclamation) |
| севда | love, longing |
| арам да ти е | shame on you / may it be cursed for you |
| свекор | father-in-law |
| прелага | talks round, deceives, persuades |
| полта | a kind of coat or gown (dialect) |
