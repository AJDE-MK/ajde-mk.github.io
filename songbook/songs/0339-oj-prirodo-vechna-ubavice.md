# Ој природо вечна убавице

*Oj prirodo večna ubavice* · *Oh Eternal Beauty, Nature*

Pesna.org #339: <https://pesna.org/song/339>

## Background

A folk-philosopher's song. Nature made love and hatred, luck and joy; rich ladies with rich husbands keep selling their honour; the singer wants no palaces or riches, only a straw-roofed hut and sweet sleep. Sung slowly, with a half-chalgija feel. Whether it is traditional or composed is unclear.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft; an agent flagged the origin as unclear.*
