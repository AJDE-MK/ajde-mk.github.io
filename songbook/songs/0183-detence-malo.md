## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Contrasts a carefree child with the hardship of servants and prisoners in Ottoman times; origin unclear, I cannot name an author. 'јаска' (probably 'јас', I), 'ливаѓе' (meadows) and 'Прости ми' are my reading.
