# Вирум вода, вирум вировита

*Virum voda, virum virovita* · *The water whirls, a whirlpool*

Pesna.org #1024: <https://pesna.org/song/1024>

**Notes.** Песната е дел од народните обичаи во Струшка Малесија.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Вирум вода, вирум вировита' is my reading (verb form unclear); the image of the whirlpool stands for the crowd gathering at the groom's yard. Struga Malesija wedding custom song, dialect forms (-ит, -ват).
