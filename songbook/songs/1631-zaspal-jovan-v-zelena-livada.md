## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Jovan fell asleep in a green meadow,
> and a girl was a dewy clover.
>
> Whoever passes by, wakes up the girl,
> and no one will wake up John.
>
> A falcon chick flew in,
> and perched on his right shoulder.
>
> With its wings fluttered, it spoke with its throat:
> - Are you married, John, are you betrothed?
>
> John answered, a good hero:
> - Oh, you chicken, falcon chick.
>
> I am not married, nor will I marry,
> until I marry Dimka with nine brothers.
>
> When Dimka's brothers heard,
> they built a tower of lead.
>
> They built a tower of lead,
> and put Dimka in the tower.
>
> Dimka prayed to God:
> - God, give me spring rains.
>
> To knead a tower of lead,
> to see Jovan, a good hero.
