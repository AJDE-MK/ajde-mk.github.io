## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The first version reads as old folk song; the second version (after the marker) sounds like a later addition naming Gotse Delchev, Yane Sandanski, Dame Gruev and Todor Aleksandrov. Guesses: 'ан'мки' as hanim ladies, 'кавурче' (unknown, perhaps 'little unbeliever'), 'Omile mi' as 'grows dear'; the marker line is copied as given in the source.
