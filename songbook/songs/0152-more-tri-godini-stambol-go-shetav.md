# Море три години Стамбол го шетав

*More tri godini Stambol go šetav* · *For Three Years I Roamed Istanbul*

Pesna.org #152: <https://pesna.org/song/152>

## Background

A pechalba song with a sad twist. Three years working in Istanbul and three thousand groats saved. The first thousand bought a horse, the second went on the horse, the third was meant to marry Temjano; just as he sets out for home, the horse dies in the middle of the stream. Istanbul (*Stambol*) was the economic magnet before America was.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Temjano is a woman's name used as an address. 'такам за коњче' and 'коњче ми пукна' (literally 'burst', my reading: the horse gave out or died) are my reading.
