## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Kate, Kate, Kate, Katerina hey,
> when you get married Kate, let me know hey.
>
> Don't marry the teacher hey,
> the teacher does not have a house hey.
>
> He will take you from one village to another hey,
> the dogs will bark after you Kate hey.
