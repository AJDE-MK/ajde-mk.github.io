## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> A sugar Moon rose.
> Dear, it is sweet, like honey.
>
> It wasn't a sugar Moon.
> Dear, it is sweet, like honey.
>
> It was a little beautiful girl.
> Dear, it is sweet, like honey.
>
> She rose early to fetch cold water.
> Dear, it is sweet, like honey.
