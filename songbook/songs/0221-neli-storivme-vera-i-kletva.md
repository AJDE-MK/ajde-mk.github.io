## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Didn't we, hey Sevda,
> promise and swear,
> that you will not marry, hey Sevda,
> and I will not marry?
> Hey, pretty, I will not marry.
>
> You were supposed to wait for me, hey Sevda,
> up to three years,
> but you didn't wait for me, hey Sevda,
> not even for three weeks.
> Hey, pretty, not for three weeks.
>
> I went to Solun, hey Sevda,
> and I received some news,
> that you are engaged to be married, hey Sevda,
> to my little godfather.
> Hey, pretty, to my little godfather.
>
> I am invited, hey Sevda,
> to be your godfather at the wedding,
> but my hands, hey Sevda,
> can't hold the flowers.
> Hey, pretty, can't hold the flowers.
>
> I said with my mouth, hey Sevda,
> "may you be happy",
> I am saying with my heart, hey Sevda,
> "may you be mine again".
> Hey, pretty, may you be mine again.
