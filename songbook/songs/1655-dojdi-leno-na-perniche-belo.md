## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Winter has come, the snow is falling,
> you can't lie in a cold bed.
> Come to me Leno, who will warm me,
> without you this night will be damned.
>
> Come to me Leno on a white pillow
> so that your warm body can warm me,
> so that gentle hands can warm you
> on long winter, snowy nights.
>
> White blanket, the day is fading,
> come to me Leno, save me from the cold.
> Your lips are flames, warm
> may they melt the white snowflakes in my heart.
>
> Come to me Leno on a white pillow
> so that a warm body can warm me,
> so that gentle hands can warm you
> on long winter, snowy nights.
