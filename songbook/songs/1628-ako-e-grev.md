## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; sounds like a modern composed song but I cannot name an author. The third-stanza refrain line 'пратил јас' is a variant of the earlier 'прати ја' and I translated it as written ('I sent').
