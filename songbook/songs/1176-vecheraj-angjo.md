## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding. The last two lines are my reading: the magic in the rakija makes a girl fall for the man while ruining the singer.
