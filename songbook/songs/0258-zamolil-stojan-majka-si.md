*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Дејгиди' is a refrain I am not sure of; treated as an interjection. The last stanza reads as the mother's answer ('green' meaning young and inexperienced), my reading.
