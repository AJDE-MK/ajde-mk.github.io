## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Пијана без ракија' is rendered literally ('drunk without rakija', likely meaning drunk on love or just carefree); that interpretation is my reading. 'Налани' as wooden clogs is my reading.
