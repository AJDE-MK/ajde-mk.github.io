## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Fire burns Brezovo,
> Macedonia, Macedonia.
> Ordan enters with his company, Macedonia,.
> into the enslaved Brezovo.
>
> He speaks to his company, Macedonia:
> - "Get up brothers, don't sleep.
> Take your bombs and ammunition,
> we will fight the enemy".
>
> Ordan says to his company,
> Macedonia, Macedonia:
> - "Take your bombs and ammunition,
> destroy the tyrants,
> let's win our freedom".
>
> Bombs explode, bullets, Macedonia,
> a bloody fight starts.
> And Ordan shouted:
> - "We have conquered Brezovo"!
>
> Note: Brezovo is a small village in Macedonia near Demir Hisar (map). Ordan (or Jordan, Yordan) is the Macedonian voivoda Јordan Piperkov (1870-1903) also known as Jordan Piperkata (Macedonian: Јордан Пиперката).

Also on Pesna.org: Turkish, Greek.
