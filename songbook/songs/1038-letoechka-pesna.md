## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional song (title says Летоечка, a seasonal song). 'Мие' in line 4 and 'се подвирило' as 'been flooded' are my reading; 'зборвит', 'држит' are dialect -it forms.
