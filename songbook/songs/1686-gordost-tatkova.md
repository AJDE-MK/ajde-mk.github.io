## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Tell me, tell me, dear son, will you look after
> your father once he grows old?
> - I will look after you, dear father, yes,
> just as Delchev looked after Macedonia.
>
> You gave me life, raised me, and saw me wed,
> I will look after you, dear father, may you live to grow old.
>
> Life flows on, passing by so very fast,
> I wish for you to caress your grandchildren,
> offspring are a source of great joy,
> a blessing everyone hopes to see.
> A mighty hero, a grandfather’s pride,
> to stand guard over Macedonia.
>
> You gave me life, raised me, and saw me wed,
> I will look after you, dear father, may you live to grow old.
