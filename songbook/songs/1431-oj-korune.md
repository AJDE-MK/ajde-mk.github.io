# Ој Коруне

*Oj Korune* · *Oh Korun*

Pesna.org #1431: <https://pesna.org/song/1431>

**Notes.** Ќесеџија е разбојник, човек кој краде сечејќи ги ќесињата на жртвите. Бинекчија е коњаник, добар јавач. Арамија е крадец, разбојник.

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Word meanings for the three bandit terms come from the source notes. The last stanza is unclear to me; my reading is that the boy burns to catch the speaker and collect a blood debt, with his father and uncles behind him.
