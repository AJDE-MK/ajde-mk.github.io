## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I would like so much
> to have a store in Struga.
> Oh hey, my dear love,
> you make my heart burn.
>
> To have a store in Struga,
> to sit at the entrance.
> Oh hey, my dear love,
> you make my heart burn.
>
> To site at the entrance,
> to watch the girls from Struga.
> Oh hey, my dear love,
> you make my heart burn.
>
> When they go to get water
> with those colorful clay jugs.
> Oh hey, my dear love,
> you make my heart burn.
>
> When they stomp with their cloggs,
> they rip my heart out.
> Oh hey, my dear love,
> you make my heart burn.
