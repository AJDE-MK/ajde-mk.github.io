## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding song. 'Дружба' in 'писмо од дружба' is my reading: a letter from a companion/friend (possibly the sweetheart).
