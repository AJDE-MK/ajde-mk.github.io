## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> - Stojna, maiden from Kochani,
> where were you, strolling?
>
> All day you weren't at home,
> a drop of water you haven't even had.
>
> A drop of water, you haven't even had,
> a crumb of bread, you haven't even eaten.
>
> - Oh, you crazy lad,
> don't you worry about me.
>
> I got engaged, you crazy lad,
> with, you crazy lad, a boy from the neighbourhood.
>
> He rides a bay horse,
> he carries a watch in his bosom.
