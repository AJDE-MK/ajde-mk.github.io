# Болка за Нерет

*Bolka za Neret* · *Pain for Neret*

Pesna.org #1344: <https://pesna.org/song/1344>

**Notes.** Нерет (грчки: Πολυπόταμο, Полипотамо) е село во Република Грција, во околината на Лерин, Западен регион.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, it reads like a modern song about Aegean Macedonian exile; no author known to me. 'Пеесет' I read as 'петесет' (fifty), and 'Уште ни зборот не докажа' as 'had not yet finished the word' (my reading).
