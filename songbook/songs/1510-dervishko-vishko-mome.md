## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional hoeing song. 'Дервишко-вишко' is a rhyming play on 'дервишко' (dervish-like); the sense of 'dervish-like' here is my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| моме | girl, maiden (vocative) |
| душо | soul, darling (vocative) |
| роб | slave, servant |
| лико | face |
| лудо | silly one, fool (here teasing) |
| фајда | benefit, use |
| копачка | hoeing/digging song |
