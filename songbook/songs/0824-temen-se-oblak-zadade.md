## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Ilinden-era revolutionary song naming Dame Gruev, Alekso Turundzhov and Gjurchin Slanchanec; no named author, so translated. 'четничката рамнина', 'кумити' as best men and 'малихер' as Mannlicher are my reading; 'ни еден не се издаде' is also my reading.
