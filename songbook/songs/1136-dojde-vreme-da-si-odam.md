## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, no author known to me; translated as an emigrant (gurbet) song. 'Ќе горам' (I will burn) means to yearn for.
