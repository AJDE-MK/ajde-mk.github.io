## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Patriotic song, origin unclear. 'Бело Море' is the Macedonian name for the Aegean Sea; 'тебе ми те прават' (stanza 3) is my reading: 'they are making you (into a beauty of sorrow)'. Goce is Goce Delchev; 'Jane' probably Jane Sandanski (my reading).

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| земјо | land, earth (vocative) |
| убавица | beauty |
| тирани | tyrants |
| душмани | enemies |
