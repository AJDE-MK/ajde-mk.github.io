## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I walked as unmarried man, walked around, mother,
> I walked as unmarried man, walked around,
> I walked as unmarried man, walked around, mother,
> I roamed nine cities.
>
> I roamed nine cities, mother,
> I fell in love with nine girls,
> I fell in love with nine girls, mother,
> but none of them rests in my heart.
>
> Now I fell in love with one, mother,
> she is thin and tall,
> she is thin and tall,
> with face that is white and red.
> With face that is white and red, mother,
> she rests in my heart.
