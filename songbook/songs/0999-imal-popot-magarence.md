## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional humorous song, no author known. 'се сопало' is a variant of 'се сопнало'; the 'Hush, priest, do not weep for me' line is literal, the wife is speaking.
