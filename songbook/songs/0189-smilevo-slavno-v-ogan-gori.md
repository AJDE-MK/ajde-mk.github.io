# Смилево славно в' оган гори

*Smilevo slavno v' ogan gori* · *Glorious Smilevo burns in fire*

Pesna.org #189: <https://pesna.org/song/189>

## Background

One of the further Ilinden songs your songbook listed to explore. Its title means "Glorious Smilevo Burns". Not checked whether it is traditional or a later composition.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Song about the 1903 Smilevo Congress/Ilinden uprising; second version has dialect forms (горит, командувет, паднит). The line 'И на ова царство ќе достоеме' is my reading ('we will outlast this empire').
