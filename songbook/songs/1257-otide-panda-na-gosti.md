## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, humorous song about a corn-shucking gathering. 'жив ти теткина' is my reading (an oath-like exclamation, roughly 'may your aunt live').
