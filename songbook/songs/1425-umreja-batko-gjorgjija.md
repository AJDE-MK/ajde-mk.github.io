## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Big brother George died, grandma,
> for one glass of rakija*.
>
> He did not die of a disease, grandma,
> but died of madness.
>
> Everything beautiful that he saw, grandma,
> he wanted it to be his.
>
> He saw nice oxen, grandma,
> so he wanted them to be his.
>
> He saw a young bride, grandma,
> so he wanted her to be his.
>
> He did not die of a disease, grandma,
> but died of madness.
