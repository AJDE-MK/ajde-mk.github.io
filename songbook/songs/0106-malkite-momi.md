## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The song uses a dated term for a dark-skinned boy; translated as 'Arab boy' literally. 'Делба делиле' is my reading as 'were dividing shares/lots'.
