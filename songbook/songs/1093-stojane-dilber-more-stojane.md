## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional love song. 'Рудо јагне' as 'ruddy lamb' (рудо = reddish, fleecy) and 'лудо' as an affectionate 'silly' are my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |

| џанам | my dear, my soul (Turkish loan) |
