## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional ballad. 'Печали' I took as 'gained/acquired' (my reading); 'през' = through (dialect). Stojan seems to be Mara's husband, so she returns unrecognized (my reading).
