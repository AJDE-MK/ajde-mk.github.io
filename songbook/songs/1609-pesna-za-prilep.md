## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear: sounds like a modern town song, no author named. The saat (clock tower) is Prilep's well-known landmark.
