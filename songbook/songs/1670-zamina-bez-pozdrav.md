## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> You left without saying goodbye, my dear,
> and didn't call back.
> How can you forget the happy days?
>
> Oh, you swore to me
> that for the first time you were happy
> only with me.
>
> Like snow in the sun, my dear,
> you were melting.
> And with your sweet lips
> you whispered to me.
>
> Oh, you swore to me
> that for the first time you were happy
> only with me.
>
> I know you're burning even now, my dear,
> you're tormenting yourself.
> I won't have to wait long,
> you'll look for me.
>
> Oh, you swore to me
> that for the first time you were happy
> only with me.
