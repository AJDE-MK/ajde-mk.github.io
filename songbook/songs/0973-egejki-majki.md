## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (patriotic song about Aegean Macedonia, no author I can name). 'нашински' I read as 'in our own way, our own'; 'Елини' means Hellenes (Greeks).
