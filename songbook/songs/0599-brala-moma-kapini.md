## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'падарот' is my reading as 'field guard' (a dialect/Turkish-derived word, not certain). The joke is that the guard asks her to shorten her dress and hair so she does not damage the crops.
