## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Рудото' as 'ruddy' and 'була' as 'married woman' are my reading. 'Сос' мен'' is dialect for 'with me'; 'вази' is a dialect form, meaning unsure.
