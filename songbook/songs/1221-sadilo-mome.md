## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> A girl planted, girl, hey girl,
> early white basil, girl, hey girl,
> early white basil.
>
> When she planted it, girl, hey girl,
> she didn't look into it, girl, hey girl,
> she didn't look into it.
>
> When she saw it, girl, hey girl,
> it grew into a forest, girl, hey girl,
> enough for a horse to rest in it.
>
> A horse to rest, girl, hey girl,
> and a crazy lad, hey girl,
> who is also not married.
>
> A big dance started, big,
> in front of priest's entrance.
>
> All the girls came, they came,
> only Stojna Popova didn't come.
>
> Stojna Popova wasn't there, wasn't there,
> her mother was making a pearl necklace.
>
> Her mother was making a pearl necklace,
> and taught Stojna, she scolded her:
>
> - Stojna dear, my daughter, my daughter,
> when you go to see the oro, the oro,
> don't go and start to dance, to dance,
> because the crazy lad will be dancing.
>
> He will stumble you with his leg, trip you up,
> he will push you with his arm, push you.
