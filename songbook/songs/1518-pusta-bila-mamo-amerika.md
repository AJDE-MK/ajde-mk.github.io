## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional emigration song. 'Пуста била' is rendered 'was desolate' (the 'била' is a reported-past form, roughly 'it turned out to be').

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| пуста | desolate, wretched, cursed |
| печалба | work abroad, migrant earnings |
| печалбарски | of migrant work |
| се врати | returned |
| се сакати | got crippled, maimed |
| либе | sweetheart |
| рака | arm, hand |
| нога | leg |
