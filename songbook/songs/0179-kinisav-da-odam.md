## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I was preparing to go
> to Kostur on a visit.
> My borther-in-law said:
> - "Come back, bride-to-be".
>
> Come back, bride-to-be,
> your boyfriend came.
> Your boyfriend came
> he didn't find you at home.
>
> He wants you to give him clothes,
> the white shirt.
> The white shirt
> that is brand new.
>
> He wants a dinner from you
> a roasted chicken.
> A roasted chicken
> that is still young.
