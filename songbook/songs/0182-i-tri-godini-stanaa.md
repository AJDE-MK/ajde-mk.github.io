## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Verbs ending in -ат/-ит for third person singular (дојдит, напуштат) are dialect forms. This may be a later composed song; I treated it as traditional but am not certain.
