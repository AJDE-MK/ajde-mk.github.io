## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Јас ќе се либе посвршам' is my reading as 'I will die/finish myself'; 'робинка за црна земја' read as 'slave of the black earth' (buried in the ground).
