## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Jutta Malzbender)*

> Come out, Vida, to look at something marvellous,
> at something marvellous, a huge miracle.
> A huge miracle, a hare with a turban,
> a hare with a turban, with his head swathed.
>
> Come out, Vida, to look at something marvellous,
> at something marvellous, a huge miracle.
> A huge miracle, a wolf in a Turkish cloak,
> a wolf in a Turkish cloak, a hare with a turban,
> a hare with a turban, with his head swathed.
>
> Come out, Vida, to look at something marvellous,
> at something marvellous, a huge miracle.
> A huge miracle, a fox with a wreath on his head,
> a fox with a wreath on his head, a wolf in a Turkish cloak,
> a wolf in a Turkish cloak, a hare with a turban,
> a hare with a turban, with his head swathed.
>
> Vida came out to look at something marvellous,
> at something marvellous, a huge miracle.
> A huge miracle, an unmarried young man.

Also on Pesna.org: German, Turkish.
