## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Мице ора в' градина' is my reading (Mica is digging in the garden). Para and Mica are names (Mica is likely a male nickname, Para a girl's name).

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| киселец | sorrel (sour leafy plant) |
| бери | picks, gathers |
| ора | plows, digs (dialect) |
| градина | garden |
| севда | love, longing |
| усте | mouth (dialect form of уста) |
| оладам | I cool down |
