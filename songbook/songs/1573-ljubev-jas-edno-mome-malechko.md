*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'непосвршено' and 'посврши' are my reading: not yet promised or settled in marriage, and promised to another; I am not certain of this sense.
