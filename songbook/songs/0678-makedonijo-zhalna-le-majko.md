## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Горо ле мајко' (vocative to the forest) is my reading of line 5; the mother is both Macedonia and the forest here. Appears to be an old revolutionary-era (komitadji) song, but I am not certain of its origin.
