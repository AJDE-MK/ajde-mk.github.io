*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Усојница' is a guess ('poisonous' / 'sun-lover'). In the fourth stanza the text addresses the love but still says 'мале' (mother), kept literally. 'Поголема' rendered 'elder', my reading. Traditional.
