## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I was in love in one crazy young girl,
> it was not engaged to be married.
> For nine years it laid on my heart,
> and left me with a wound in my heart.
>
> It is so hard faithfully to love,
> faithfully to love for nine years.
> It is so hard faithfully to love,
> and be left with sorrow in my heart.
>
> Our love was not faithful,
> and it wasn't real, true.
> I loved you, some other had you,
> I didn't know where I am.
>
> It is so hard faithfully to love,
> faithfully to love for nine years.
> It is so hard faithfully to love,
> and be left with sorrow in my heart.
>
> If I die, please,
> you come to my grave.
> Shed tears and mourn me,
> in honor of our past love.
>
> It is so hard faithfully to love,
> faithfully to love for nine years.
> It is so hard faithfully to love,
> and be left with sorrow in my heart.
