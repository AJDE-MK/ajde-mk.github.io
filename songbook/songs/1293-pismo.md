## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; reads like a modern composed song but I cannot name an author. 'јанѕа имаш' is my reading: roughly 'are you troubled/anxious'. 'Си го прости господ Стојана' means the letter announces Stojan's death.
