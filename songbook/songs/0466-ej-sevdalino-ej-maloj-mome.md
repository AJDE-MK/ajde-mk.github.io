## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Line 'со тебе да се ќердосам' is my reading (ќердосам = enjoy myself); the stanza 3 line 'со мерак сакам, со тебе душо' is also loosely rendered.
