# Македонки билбилјанки

*Makedonki bilbiljanki* · *Macedonian Women, Basil-Pot Girls*

Pesna.org #677: <https://pesna.org/song/677>

## Background

A flirtatious roll-call of Macedonian towns and their girls: Veles the potters, Prilep the tobacco-rollers, Bitola the sweethearts, Ohrid the heart-scarers, Struga the white-and-red. The chorus is the same each time: climb up behind me on the horse and come with me. In each verse the town changes; the verse for your family's town is the one dad pointed at.
