## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Modern WWII partisan song; origin unclear, no author named. The last line ('на место дваесет партизани') is my reading as twenty partisans being saved because of her; 'зрно кибрит' I rendered as a lit match.
