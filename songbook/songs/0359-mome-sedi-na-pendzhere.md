## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The first line in parentheses is an alternate opening line, translated as given. 'Сараф' means banker/money-changer per the source note; the lad's reply plays on it ('a banker of girls'), which is my reading.
