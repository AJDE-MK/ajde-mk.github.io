## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Donka got lost in the mountains, oh
> there he found a young shepherd.
> Shepherd says, speaks, oh:
> - Donka, let me kiss you.
>
> - Come on, little shepherd, you fool, oh,
> this is no place to kiss a girl.
> Let's go down to the carriage, oh,
> there I will let you kiss me.
>
> They went down in the carriage, oh,
> Donka tells him, speaks:
> - Let's go home, in our courtyard, oh,
> I will let you kiss me there.
>
> They went home to the courtyards, oh,
> Donka shouted, shouted:
> - Come out and see, oh,
> what a fool I am bringing with me!
