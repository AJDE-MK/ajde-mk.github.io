## English

*From Pesna.org*

> The nightingale in the cage, in the room,
> Mournfully sings a sad song:
> - Hey, get up, get up, old Eftimica,
> To get your son, Stojan, into gurbet*
> Into gurbet, in those damn foreign lands!
>
> He will go into gurbet, into foreign countries,
> Foreign countries, most of all America,
> He will earn some money, and then will go back,
> He will hug his mother and organize a wedding.
>
> One day Eftimica woke up,
> to get her son Stojan into gurbet.
> His sweetheart is in tears, mourning and begging him:
> - No, Stojan, don't listen to your mother, don't go to damn America!
>
> Three years have passed,
> And poor old Eftimica already gets a letter.
> In the letter Stojan wrote: - Don't mourn, dear mother.
> Don't mourn, dear mother, don't cry, dear sweetheart,
> I have not earned any money, and I've left my youth behind!
>
> Eftimica cursed and swore:
> Desolate be, you damn foreign land!
> Desolate be, you damn America!
> You took my only child, my son Stojan.
