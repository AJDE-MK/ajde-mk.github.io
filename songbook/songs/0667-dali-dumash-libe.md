## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Дали думаш' is my reading as 'do you recall' (the alternate title says 'знаеш', do you know). 'Два' in 'двата' is dialectal for 'the two of us'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| думаш | you say/mention (dialect; here 'do you recall') |
| паметуваш | you remember |
| прошетка | a stroll |
| се колнеше | you swore |
| распарај | rip open |
| мачно | hard, painful |
| жално | sad, sorrowful |
