## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Dialect with verb forms like 'ми го паслo', 'му утепале'. 'Везден' (embroidered) is my reading, perhaps 'everywhere/all day'; 'сардисале' and 'посилеле' are guesses.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| невен | marigold; here an epithet for a lad (my reading) |
| кавал | kaval, shepherd's flute |
| трло | sheep fold |
| колиба | hut, shepherd's cabin |
| арамии | bandits, outlaws |
| џанам | dear, my soul (Turkish-origin endearment) |
| потера | chase, pursuit |
