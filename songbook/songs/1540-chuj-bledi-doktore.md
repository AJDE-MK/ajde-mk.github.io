## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; it reads like a later urban song, but I cannot name an author. The speaker is a woman ('која сум'). 'Кола' is rendered 'carriage' (my reading, possibly a hearse).
