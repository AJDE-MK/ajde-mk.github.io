## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Get up, let's go,
> father's Sofka,
> to the city of Gumendzha.
> Oh, dear,
> father's Sofka,
> to the city of Gumendzha.
>
> To the city of Gumendzha,
> father's Sofka,
> to see the doctor.
> Oh, dear,
> father's Sofka,
> to see Gono Rusino.
>
> He may tell us,
> father's Sofka,
> what is your ache.
> Oh, dear,
> father's Sofka,
> what is your ache.
>
> - Tell us, tell us,
> Gono tell your uncle,
> what is Sofka's ache?
> Oh, dear,
> Gono, my dear,
> what is Sofka's ache?
>
> - Sofka's ache,

> the girl is carrying a child.
> Oh, dear,
> dear uncle Dean,
