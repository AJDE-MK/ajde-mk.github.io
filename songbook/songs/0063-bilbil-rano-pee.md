## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Cumulative counting song. 'Пирустија' as a three-legged pot stand is my reading; 'на крст' (crosswise) is literal.
