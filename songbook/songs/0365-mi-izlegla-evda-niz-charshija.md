*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Голај бива' is rendered 'it is easy' and 'џанам аџамија' as 'my dear rascal' (aџамија = novice or rogue), both my reading. 'Ми заличи' is rendered 'you suit'.
