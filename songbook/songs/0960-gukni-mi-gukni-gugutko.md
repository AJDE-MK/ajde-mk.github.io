# Гукни ми, гукни гугутко

*Gukni mi, gukni gugutko* · *Coo for Me, Coo, Little Dove*

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional bride's song, as far as I can tell. 'Сум било' (I was) is a dialect/narrative form; 'ц'втеше/цавтеше' = bloomed.
