## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Come Lena, let's go
> down, to the center of the village,
> down, to the center of the village,
> there is a gathering.
>
> There have gathered
> old and young,
> they dance oro,
> they sing songs.
>
> In the middle of the oro,
> there is our shepherd,
> he is blowing
> his damn gajda (bagpipe).
>
> All the girls
> are dancing and singing,
> the pretty Cena
> neighter dances, nor sings.
