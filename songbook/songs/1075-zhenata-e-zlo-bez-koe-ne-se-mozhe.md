## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, humorous song of unknown authorship. 'Нејќе пред очи да види' is my reading as 'will not bear to see'; 'подмавнува' I guess as 'provokes, needles'; 'надрапав' is 'I stumbled upon'.
