## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Folk-style ballad; origin unclear, I cannot name an author. The second stanza's 'ете ти го сега него' is my reading as 'here is him now, in my place'. It is the dead daughter who speaks in the second stanza.
