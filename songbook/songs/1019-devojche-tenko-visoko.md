## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Thin and tall girl,
> don't walk with short steps in front of me.
>
> Don't walk with short steps in front of me,
> don't make mist and dust.
>
> Don't make mist and dust,
> don't wake the grief inside me.
>
> Don't wake the grief inside me,
> when I am already alone, grieving.
