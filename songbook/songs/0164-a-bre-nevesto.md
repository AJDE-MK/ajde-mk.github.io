## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Око калешово' is unclear to me (possibly 'dear/invited one'), left untranslated. 'Лудо' rendered 'dear one' and the last stanza (the soldier is likely her own husband, unrecognized) is my reading.
