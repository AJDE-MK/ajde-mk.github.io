## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Stanza 2 line 1 ('дибек би те кало') is unclear to me; my reading is that a dibek stone would be set for her. Treat that line as a guess.
