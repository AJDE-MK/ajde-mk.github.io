## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding lament. Speaker changes are not marked in the original in every stanza; I read the dialogue as son and mother alternating, with the last stanza the son again.
