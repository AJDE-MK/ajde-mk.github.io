## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Нешо' and 'лудо' are address words; I render 'лудо' as 'my dear' (my reading), though it literally means 'crazy/young lad'. 'Таксај му таксај... што ќе му таксаш' is my reading of a bargaining line.
