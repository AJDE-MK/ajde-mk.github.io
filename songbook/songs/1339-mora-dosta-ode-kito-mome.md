## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional song from the Debar area. 'Мора доста' is a guess (my reading: 'a lot, plenty'); 'не ги гори' I render 'do not burn them' as in 'do not make them pine', which is uncertain.
