## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Виши' is my reading as an imperative 'look up' (raise your eyes). The 'one fool' may be an affectionate 'lover' (лудо) rather than insulting, my reading.
