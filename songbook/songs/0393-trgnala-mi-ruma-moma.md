## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Shepherd-and-girl dialogue in folk style; origin unclear, no author I can name. 'Зело би те, зело лудо' is my reading ('I would take you, take you madly'); 'Рума' is taken as a girl's name.
