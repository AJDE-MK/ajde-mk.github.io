## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Анѓе је се нешто учинило' is my reading: 'something has come over Anga'. 'Влегло лудо' could mean 'the foolish/young one went into' rather than 'went crazy'; I am not sure.
