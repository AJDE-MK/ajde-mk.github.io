## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-style. 'тељојте' is a dialect word I am unsure of (my reading: headscarf/headdress); 'ни во турско, ни каурско' means neither among Turks nor Christians.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| џанам | my dear (Turkish-origin endearment) |
| момче | boy, lad |

| ќерка | daughter |
| поубо | better (dialect for 'подобро') |
