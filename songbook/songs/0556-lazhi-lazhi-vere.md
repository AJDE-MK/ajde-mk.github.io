## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Lie, lie, Vere,
> lie whoever you want to lie,
> just please Vere,
> don't lie to me.
>
> I have, Vere,
> oh, I have
> I have, Vere,
> three sicknesses.
>
> The first sickness, Vere,
> oh, the first sickness,
> the first sickness, Vere:
> the taverns in Strumica.
>
> The second sickness, Vera,
> oh, the second sickness,
> the second sickness, Vera:
> I'm going abroad.
>
> The third sickness, Vera,
> oh, the third sickness,
> the third sickness, Vera:
> I'm dying for you.
