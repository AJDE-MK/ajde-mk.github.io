## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> We, the people of Kicehvo,
> are not afraid of anything,
> we were born here,
> we love Kichevo.
>
> Wherever we go,
> we return back
> to our native Kichevo
> we love it from the bottom of our hearts.
>
> There is nothing more precious
> and joyful in the soul
> when we return
> to our native Kichevo.
>
> Up there, high
> below Bistra and Stogovo
> there is one native village,
> our heavenly beauty.
>
> In our village of Ehloec
> we celebrate Vodici.
> People come from all over the world,
> they open their native houses.
>
> With flutes and drums
> to welcome the godfather,
> in the middle of the village,
> they sing and are merry.
