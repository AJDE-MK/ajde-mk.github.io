## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Wouter Swets)*

> Do you remember
> when we were young?
>
> We sat in the rose garden
> under a rosebush.
>
> The rosebush dripped
> on the both of us.
>
> We caught the drops and
> rubbed our faces with them.
