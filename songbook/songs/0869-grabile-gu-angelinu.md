## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional bride-abduction song. 'гу' and 'вадив' are dialect forms; 'Идете си' I read as 'go away'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| грабиле | they seized / abducted |
| ајде џанам | come on, my dear (refrain) |
| собори | village gathering / fair |
| велигденски | of Easter |
| секира | axe |
| вадив | to get out / rescue (dialect infinitive) |
| силом | by force |
| милом | willingly |
| гу | her (dialect 'го') |
