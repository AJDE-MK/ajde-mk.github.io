## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Jutta Malzbender)*

> The sun is setting
> between two mountains, oh,
> the sun is setting,
> between two mountains.
>
> In the mountains,
> there are two white tents, oh,
> in the mountains,
> there are two white tents.
>
> Under the tents,
> a girl is lying ill, oh,
> under the tents,
> a girl is lying ill.
>
> She is afflicted
> with her silvery teeth, oh,
> she is afflicted
> with her silvery teeth.

Also on Pesna.org: German.
