## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional revolutionary song about the 1903 Rusinovo fight (Damjan Gruev, Atanas Karchev named in the text). Dialect forms are heavy (ѝ = them, у = in, они/ни, куга); 'ка прирапаја' as 'when they rushed out' and 'преслав' as 'pass' (probably преслап) are my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| војвода | voivode, band leader |
| куга | when (dialect) |
| ка | when, as (dialect) |
| чорбаџија | rich man, local notable |
| четник | fighter of a rebel band (чета) |
| стража | guard, lookout |
| опсадено | besieged |
| ишпиони | spies |
| жртва | victim, casualty |
| сборен пункт | assembly point |
