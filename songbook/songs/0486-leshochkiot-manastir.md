## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Сардисале' is my reading (besieged); 'кузум' is Turkish-derived address, meaning guessed. Last line has the mosque burned by Gotse Delchev as written.
