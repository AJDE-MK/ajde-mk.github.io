## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Тумбе Кафе' is a place name (a cafe in Bitola, as I understand it). 'бели, црвени' for girls is my reading: fair and rosy-cheeked.
