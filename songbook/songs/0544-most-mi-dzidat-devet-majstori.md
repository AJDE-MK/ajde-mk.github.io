## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Jutta Malzbender)*

> Nine builders built a bridge,
> nine builders, nine brothers,
> they built a bridge near Kratovo
> across the Manceva river.
>
> By day they built the bridge, but at night it broke down.
> The brothers made a commitment
> that the bride who would come the next day
> to bring a meal to the builders
> would be built into the base of the bridge
> to fasten it to the river.
>
> When they came home that night
> they all informed there wives,
> only the youngest one did not,
> he did not speak to his bride Rada.
>
> Rada stood up to prepare a meal
> and brought the lunch to the builders.
> When the young brother saw Rada,
> he cried out with lamenting voice.
>
> When the brothers grabbed Rada
> and built her into the base,
> Rada begged her brothers-in-law,
> to let at least her right breast in the open.
>
> At least leave my right breast in the open
> so I can give it to my little son.
> And they let it in the open
> so she could nourish the little boy.
