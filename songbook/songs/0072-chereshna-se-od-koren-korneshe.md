## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'проштевај' can mean both forgive and farewell; I rendered it as 'farewell' (my reading). 'ни' in the last line is rendered 'our'.
