## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Adapted from a poem by Trajko Kitanchev (died before 1930), so translated in full. 'завижџет' is a dialect form that I read as 'they envy'.
