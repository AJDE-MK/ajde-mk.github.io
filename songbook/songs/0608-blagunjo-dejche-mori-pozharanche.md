## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Blagunya girl from Pozharane,
> Open the glass window for me,
> I want to see you.
>
> Oh, Ace, you naive boy,
> Why should I open the window,
> When I don’t love you?
>
> Blaguna girl from Pozharane,
> Let down your blonde hair,
> I want to play with it.
>
> Oh, Ace, you naive boy,
> Why should I let down my blonde hair,
> When I don’t love you?
>
> Blagunya girl from Pozharane,
> I will sell my mansion,
> Just to marry you.
>
> Oh, Ace, you naive boy,
> Don't sell your mansion,
> I don't love you.
> I have another love,
> And I love him.
