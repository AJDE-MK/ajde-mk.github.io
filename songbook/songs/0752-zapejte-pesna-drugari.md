## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Tavern song, origin unclear, no author I can name. 'Затворете ладна меана / свири ја само нашата' is my reading: close the cold tavern (to outsiders) and play only our own song.
