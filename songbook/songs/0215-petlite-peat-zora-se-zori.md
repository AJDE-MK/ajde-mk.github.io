## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> The roosters are crowing, the dawn is breaking,
> let me, dear Mitra, get up,
> let me, dear Mitra, go.
>
> Our roosters are crowing at night,
> continue to sleep, dear, sleep,
> kiss Mitra's white face.
>
> Our lumberjacks are going to gather wood,
> let me, dear Mitra, get up,
> let me, dear Mitra, go.
>
> Our lumberjacks are going at night,
> continue to sleep, dear, sleep,
> hold Mitra's pretty body.
