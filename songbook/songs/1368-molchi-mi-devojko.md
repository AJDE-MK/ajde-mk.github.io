## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> Hush, hush my maiden,
> don’t let your black eyes go to waste.
>
> Don’t let your white face wither away,
> I will go to the city of Solun.
>
> I will bring back two slaves,
> one slave will serve you.
>
> One slave will serve you,
> the other will light my tobacco pipe.
