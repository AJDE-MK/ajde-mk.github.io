## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Guessed: 'Коњ ми вода' (my reading: I water my horse), and the last stanza 'Ушче да бев... црно око' (my reading: even if I were dark-eyed, I would marry the most beautiful girl of the village). 'Пуста таго' is a refrain I rendered loosely.
