*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-style love song, origin unclear. Kalja is a pet form of Kalina; the -м ending in 'спијам, заспијам' is a dialect/rhyming form.
