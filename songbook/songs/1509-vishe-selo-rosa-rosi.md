# Више село роса роси

*Više selo rosa rosi* · *Above the village the dew falls*

Pesna.org #1509: <https://pesna.org/song/1509>

**Notes.** Кросно е вратило на разбој за ткаење.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The note says кросно is the beam of a loom. Last stanza is partly my reading: 'да не зему' is a dialect negative purpose clause, and the Plague is personified as a visitor who is chased off.
