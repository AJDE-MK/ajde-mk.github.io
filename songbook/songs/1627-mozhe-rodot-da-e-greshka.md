## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> With pure love I leave you,
> with a lump in my heart
> I will travel.
> Without greetings, dear,
> with my family,
> without aim,
> to write down two or three words
> for you.
>
> - Don't be ashamed, son.
> - Don't curse me, mother.
> Don't you sing, my song,
> I am always yours.
> Maybe it's injustice,
> maybe the lineage is wrong,
> give me another tribe,
> just as long as it's Macedonian.
>
> Guard them, guard our angels,
> with faith that God
> will have mercy.
> And I wish the sun warms me,
> for the first time, the ray
> gently caresses me.
>
> - Don't be ashamed, son.
> - Don't curse me, mother.
> Don't you sing, my song,
> I am always yours.
> Maybe it's injustice,
> maybe the lineage is wrong,
> give me another tribe,
> just as long as it's Macedonian.
