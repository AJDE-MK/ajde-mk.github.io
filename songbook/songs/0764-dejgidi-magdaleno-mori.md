## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Дејгиди' in the title is an unclear word I left untranslated (my reading: a filler exclamation). 'Сам не се јаде' is rendered literally as 'it does not eat by itself', meaning it is no pleasure alone.
