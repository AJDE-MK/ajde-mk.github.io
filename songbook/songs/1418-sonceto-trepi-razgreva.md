## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** A partisan/war song; the 'bride' dark and of the earth and the ravens as guests read as an image of death (my reading). Origin unclear, treated as traditional.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |

| разгрева | warms up (rises and warms) |

| оженивме | we married off (a man) |
| земнена | earthen, of the earth |
| девери | brothers-in-law (here, escorts of the bride) |
