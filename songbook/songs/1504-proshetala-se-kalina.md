## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional ballad, origin unclear. The line 'Да помогнеш ели мене, ели мене, ели нему' is my reading: 'ели' is a dialect form of 'или' (or).
