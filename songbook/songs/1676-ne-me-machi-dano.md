## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I frequent the taverns,
> drinking deep red wine
> all because of you,
> my lovely Dana.
>
> When I see you
> smiling so sweetly,
> my soul is on fire,
> I want you to be mine.
>
> When I see
> your lovely eyes,
> my soul is on fire,
> I want them to be mine.
>
> Do not torment me, Dana,
> I burn with intense love,
> If I do not have your love,
> I shall die a drunkard.
