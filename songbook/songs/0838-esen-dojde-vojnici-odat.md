## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Autumn came to our city,
> to the army the soldiers left.
> My love went with them,
> far away, into an unknown city.
>
> I stayed alone to sit
> and wait for him until next year.
> Tear by tear, a stream started to flow,
> breath by breath, wind started to blow.
>
> Autumn came to our city,
> from the army soldiers were coming.
> My love came with them,
> from far away, from an unknown city.
>
> I went to welcome him,
> I welcomed him, I embraced him.
> Smile by smile, the Sun started to shine,
> kiss by kiss, a song started to flow.
