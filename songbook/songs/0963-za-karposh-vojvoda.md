## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** About Karpos, the leader of the 1689 uprising, said to have been impaled; the song says hanged. No named author, so translated. 'клета' (cursed) applied to the mother is my reading.
