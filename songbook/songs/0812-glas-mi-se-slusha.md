*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The speaker of the second-to-last stanza is presumably the Turks interrogating Jana (my reading); 'кучко' is an insult.
