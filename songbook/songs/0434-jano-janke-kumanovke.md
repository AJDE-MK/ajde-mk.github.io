# Јано, Јанке Кумановке

*Jano, Janke Kumanovke* · *Jana, little Jana from Kumanovo*

Pesna.org #434: <https://pesna.org/song/434>

## Background

A fire-song from Kumanovo. "You burned me, you set me alight" with her black eyes and her eyebrows braided like *gajtan* cord. She kissed him but warned him not to go mad over her; he did, and now he is "a dry tree, a dry maple". The *их!* whoops between verses are where everyone joins in.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Jutta Weber-Karn)*

> Jana, little Jana from Kumanovo,
> ignite me, burn me.
> Jana, little Jana from Kumanovo,
> Jana, little Jana, you enemy.
>
> You turn me into a dry tree,
> а dry maple tree.
> Jana, little Jana from Kumanovo,
> Jana, little Jana, you enemy.
>
> Ignite me, burn me
> with your black eyes.
> Jana, little Jana from Kumanovo,
> Jana, little Jana, you enemy.
>
> With your black eyes,
> with your eyebrows like ribbons.
> Jana, little Jana from Kumanovo,
> Jana, little Jana, you enemy.
