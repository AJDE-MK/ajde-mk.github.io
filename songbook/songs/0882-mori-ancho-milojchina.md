## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. 'Илиџе' is my reading of Ilidzha (a spa town near Sarajevo); the line 'te испрати' is read as 'and was sent off', which is a guess about who sends whom.
