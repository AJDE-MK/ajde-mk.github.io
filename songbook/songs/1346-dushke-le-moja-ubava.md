## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Душке' is a pet form of the name Душка, also pun-like with 'душа' (soul). 'убост' in line 2 is my reading as 'beauty' (from убав). Origin unclear, treated as traditional.
