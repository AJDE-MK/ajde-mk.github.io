## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Hey girl, white and red,
> like you, girl,
> I haven't found anywhere.
>
> Who was the mother
> that gave birth to you,
> that gave birth to you
> so beautiful?
>
> Are you Greek
> or Macedonian,
> the whole wolrd is wondering
> about your beauty?
>
> Three years have passed now
> since it started,
> since I am tormented
> by my love to you.
>
> Here, get the little knife,
> tear my heart apart,
> there you will find
> a poisonous love.
