## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Several lines are my reading: 'Ј' отруј се' (I have poisoned myself, possibly figurative for being worn out by grief), 'уки вруќини' (unclear dialect, I read it as the hands feeling the heat) and 'Ручок над глава' (lunch overhead, possibly the midday sun).
