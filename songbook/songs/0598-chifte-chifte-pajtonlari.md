## English

*From Pesna.org*

> I serve you two, and two phaetons.
> What I say to you, aman!
> Don't be angry with me! Hoppa!
> What I say to you maykem,
> don't be angry with me.
>
> I serve you two, and two pounds of lokum.
> What I say to you, aman!
> Don't be angry with me! Hoppa!
> What I say to you maykem,
> don't be angry with me.
>
> I serve you two, and two bottles of cognac.
> What I say to you, aman!
> Don't be angry with me! Hoppa!
> What I say to you maykem,
> don't be angry with me.
>
> Once upon a time, because we were very close,
> you had to be angry with me.
> What I say to you, aman!
> Don't be angry with me! Hoppa!
> What I say to you maykem,
> don't be angry with me.
