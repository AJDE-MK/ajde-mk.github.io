## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> Velja is going for water:
> - Hey you Velja, Veljusha,
> Velje let go of the horses,
> They broke the pitchers.
>
> Velja sat down and cried,
> Velje consoled her:
> - Calm down Velja, don’t cry,
> I will buy you pitchers,
> today is Saturday,
> tomorrow is Sunday.
>
> Tomorrow is Sunday,
> I will go to the bazaar,
> I will buy you two pitchers.
> If they are all taken
> I will buy you silver ones!
