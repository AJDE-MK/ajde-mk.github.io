## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (sounds like a modern emigrant song). 'Слушај сине мајка си' is my reading: 'listen, son, to your mother'; 'затрај се' is a dialect imperative, my reading 'hold on'.
