## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; sounds like a modern drinking song but I cannot name an author. 'Кој ја прави да се слади цела Струмица' is my reading: 'whoever makes it, all Strumica enjoys it'.
