## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Пусија' is rendered 'ambush' (from Turkish pusu), my reading. 'Македонче' is a diminutive, 'little Macedonian'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| а бре | hey (address particle) |
| пиле | bird, chick (also endearment) |
| поноќно време | midnight time |
| аман | mercy, alas (Turkish loan) |
| пусија | ambush |
| Солун | Salonica (Thessaloniki) |
| немој | do not |
| чекат | they wait |
