## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-style outlaw song. 'Свират' as 'sounding the alarm' and 'нè сардиса' as 'has surprised us' are my reading (сардиса is dialect, unsure).
