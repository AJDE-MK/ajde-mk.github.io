## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Не ми чинат' means 'are worth nothing to me'. 'Вез' / 'везани' in stanza three is probably the same as 'везени' (embroidered), my reading. Traditional city song, origin unclear.
