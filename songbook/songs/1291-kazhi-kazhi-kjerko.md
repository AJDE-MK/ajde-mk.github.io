## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Stanza 6 (Арам да му се... чурапе... да не ги ќердосе) is unclear; my reading is a curse on his socks, 'арам' = forbidden/cursed, 'ќердосе' possibly 'wear out/enjoy'. Dialect forms like к'лни and 'ќа' are as written; 'Дилми' in line 5 is my reading as 'Do you (ask) me'.
