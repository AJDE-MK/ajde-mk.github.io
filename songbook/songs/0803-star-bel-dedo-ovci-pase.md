## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (patriotic song, no author I can name). Gotse and Jane are, in my reading, Goce Delchev and Jane Sandanski; 'нашиот цар пирински' (our Pirin tsar) is probably Sandanski.
