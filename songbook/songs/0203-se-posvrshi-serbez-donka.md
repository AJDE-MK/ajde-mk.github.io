# Се посврши сербез Донка

*Se posvrši serbez Donka* · *Brave Donka got engaged*

Pesna.org #203: <https://pesna.org/song/203>

**Notes.** „Сербез“ значи слободен, безгрижен, смел. Приказната за Донка Јаричкова од Велес ја има на следниот линк.

## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Brave Donka got engaged
> tra, la, la, la, la, la, la, la, la
> Brave Donka of the Jarichkov family.
>
> The neighbors gathered
> tra, la, la, la, la, la, la, la, la
> the neighbors, her friends.
>
> Congratulations, brave Donka
> tra, la, la, la, la, la, la, la, la
> congratulations on the engagement.
>
> Don’t say congratulations
> tra, la, la, la, la, la, la, la, la
> but tell my mother.
>

> tra, la, la, la, la, la, la, la, la
