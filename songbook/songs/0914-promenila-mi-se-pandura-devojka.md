*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The meaning of 'променила се' (changed, possibly her mood or status) and 'Пандура' as a name are my reading. 'Во 'изба' is read as 'in the cellar'.
