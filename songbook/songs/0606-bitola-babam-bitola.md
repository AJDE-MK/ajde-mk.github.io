# Битола, бабам Битола

*Bitola, babam Bitola* · *Bitola, My Dear Bitola*

Pesna.org #606: <https://pesna.org/song/606>

## Background

A love letter and a roast in one. Bitola is where life is actually lived: eating, drinking and beautiful girls. Then the curse: who hexed you, Bitola, to keep bearing girls this beautiful and driving every bachelor mad?

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*
