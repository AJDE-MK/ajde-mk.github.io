## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; sounds like a partisan or 1944 liberation song but I cannot name an author. Slavej is a mountain in western Macedonia; Debarca is a region. 'Крилна' I took as 'winged' (my reading).
