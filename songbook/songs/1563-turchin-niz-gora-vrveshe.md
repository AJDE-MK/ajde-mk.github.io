## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional ballad of captivity under Ottoman rule. The first line in brackets is an alternate opening line, translated as given. 'Мирјанчо' is the baby's name (diminutive).

**Words to learn**
