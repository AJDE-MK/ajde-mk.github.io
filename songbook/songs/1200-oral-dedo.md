*Oral dedo* · *The Ploughing Grandfather*

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Humorous traditional-style song. 'гурелаво' (mangy/scruffy) and 'прле' (piglet) are my reading of dialect words; 'влегнал во два дола' I took as 'drove into two valleys'.
