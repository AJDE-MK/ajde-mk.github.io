## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> If someone asks: - Where are you from,
> why are you so proud?
> Immediately tell him: - I'm from Ohrid,
> from the most beautiful place in Macedonia.
>
> Whoever hasn't been to Ohrid,
> should take a walk around the city,
> and see what beauty it is.
> Ohrid, our Macedonian pride,
> there is no such beauty anywhere.
>
> If someone asks: - Where are you from,
> why are you so beautiful?
> Immediately tell him: - I'm from Bitola,
> from the most beautiful place in Macedonia.
>
> Whoever hasn't been to Bitola,
> should take a walk around the city,
> and see what beauty it is.
> Bitola is one and only, very beautiful,
> and it has the most beautiful women.
>
> If someone asks: - Where are you from,
> why are you so much of a joker?
> Immediately tell him: - I'm from Prilep,
> from the most beautiful place in Macedonia.
>
> Whoever hasn't been to Prilep,
> should take a walk around the city,
> and see what beauty is.
> Is it from the warm sun, or is it from the water,
> old, young, everyone is joking.
>
> If someone asks: - Where are you from,
> why are you so proud?
> Immediately tell him: - I'm from Skopje,
> from the most beautiful place in Macedonia.
>
> Whoever hasn't been to Skopje, the city, should take a walk,
> and see what beauty is.
> Any mother who has lost a child,
> will find him carousing in Skopje.
