## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Драги Спасовски)*

> Your eyes, Lena,
> are like cherries from Voden.
> Give them to me,
> so I can eat them up.
> So that my desire goes away,
> So my passion for you can get out of me.
>
> Your face, Lena,
> is a sweet chickpea bun from Lerin.
> Give it to me,
> so I can eat it up.
>
> Your mouth, Lena,
> is a little jewelry box from Kostur.
> Give it to me,
> so I can open it up.
>
> Your body, Lena,
> is both slender and tall.
> Give it to me,
> so I can embrace it.

Also on Pesna.org: Dutch, German, Greek.
