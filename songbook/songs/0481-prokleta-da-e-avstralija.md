## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Emigrant song. 'Си ја оставив' is a reflexive past ('I left'); 'абре' is an interjection I render as 'hey'.
