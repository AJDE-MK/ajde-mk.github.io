## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> My mother, my dear mother,
> when I walked, mother, by the upper quarters,
> there I saw, mother, the maiden Dosta,
> Dosta was, my mother, just like me.
>
> My mother, my dear mother,
> she’s like me, dear mother, she’s my kind.
> Go to her, mom, dear mother, and ask for her,
> if they’ll give her to me, mother, then stay to celebrate.
>
> My mother, my dear mother,
> if they won’t give her, come back home quickly.
> I’ll knock down the house, mother, and I’ll build a monastery,
> I’ll build a monastery, mother, and become a monk.
