## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Колку ти чини ... толку ми чини грлото' is my reading: the girl says her throat (her life or voice) is worth as much as his estate. Name 'Ѓорѓија' kept as 'Gjorgija'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| чифлик | estate, large farm (Ottoman-era) |
| аман | oh my (cry of lament or entreaty, Turkish loan) |
| продадам | I will sell |
| нива | field |
| веѓи | eyebrows |
| грло | throat |
| земам | to take (here: to marry) |
| чини | is worth, costs |
