# Оро се вие крај манастирот

*Oro se vie kraj manastirot* · *They are dancing by the monastery*

Pesna.org #334: <https://pesna.org/song/334>

## Background

A generic dance caller. By the monastery an oro winds: young girls in the first, young men in the second, young brides in the third. The singer keeps asking to go and see who is dancing.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Alan Moore)*

> They are dancing by the monastery,
> They are dancing by the monastery.
> I'm going, my Gana, to see them,
> I'm going, Gana dear, to dance with them.
>
> First in the dance are the boys,
> First in the dance are the boys.
> I'm going, my Gana, to see them,
