*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Line 'Уста да ти капне' is my reading (a mild curse or wish, 'may your mouth drip'); 'Крши глава' is an idiomatic exhortation, rendered literally. Origin unclear, looks traditional.
