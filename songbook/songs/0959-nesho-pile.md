## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. Not sure of 'калешо' (my reading: a messenger/inviting one, as in a wedding-style address) and 'бабки те изеле' (my reading: grubs/lice, a curse). The last stanza's curses are folk-style insults.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| калеша | messenger, caller (endearment for a daughter, my reading) |
| поли | skirt hems, coat-tails |
| препиња | hinders, trips up |
| месам | I knead |
| кравајче | small loaf (ritual bread) |
