## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Revolutionary-era song about the band of Gotse Delchev. 'Буковичко' as 'Bukovik' is my reading of a place name.
