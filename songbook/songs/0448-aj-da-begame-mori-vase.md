## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'од мерак лежи' is my reading as lovesickness or longing; 'ќе ја земам' I read as 'marry her'. 'Васе' is a nickname form (Vasko/Vasil).
