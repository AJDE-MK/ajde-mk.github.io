## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> - I have fallen sick, I will die
> From a grave illness, from a high fever.
> From a high fever, a terrible headache,
> a terrible headache.
>
> - Don't fret, my son, from such illness,
> From such illness, from that kind of fever.
> Why didn’t you tell me three days ago,
> three days ago!
>
> So I could prescribe you three remedies:
> The first for your head, the second for your fever.
> So I could prescribe you three remedies,
> The second for your fever, the third for your heart.
