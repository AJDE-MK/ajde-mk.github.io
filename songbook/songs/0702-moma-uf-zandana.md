## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, origin unclear. 'руси коси посмолеле' is my reading (hair turned dark like pitch); 'уште душа стои во тебе' I read as 'the soul still stays in you'. The title uses 'уф', probably a variant of 'во' (in).
