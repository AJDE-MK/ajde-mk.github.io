## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Oh fair maiden, my pretty fair maiden,
> come marry me, come be my wife.
> I'll not be wedded to any man,
> my mother is lost, gone forever.
> Barely six months have passed now,
> and I must mourn her a year.
