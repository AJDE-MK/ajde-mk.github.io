# Коледица

*Koledica* · *Koledica*

Pesna.org #472: <https://pesna.org/song/472>

## Background

A children's carol from the Bitola region. The names Koleda, Meda and Vasilica are the calendar itself dressed as people: Koleda is Christmas, Vasilica is Old New Year (St. Basil, 14 January). In the second part a child asks for a chestnut, one for me and one for my friend.
