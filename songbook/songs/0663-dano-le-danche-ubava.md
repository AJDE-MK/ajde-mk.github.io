## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Дано ле Данче' is an affectionate address (Dana, diminutive Danche). 'Преваре' as 'deceived' and 'се армаса' as 'got engaged' are my reading.
