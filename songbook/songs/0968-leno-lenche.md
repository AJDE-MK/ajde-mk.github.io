## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. 'Изгор' is a guess: I read it as 'burning, torment' used as an endearment. 'Лено, Ленче' are forms of the name Lena.
