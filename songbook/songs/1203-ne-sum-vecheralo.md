## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. The text is dialectal and partly garbled; lines 1-2 of stanza 1, 'мене ме е мама посвршила' (my reading: mother married me off) and 'пресно ле с' месно' are my reading.
