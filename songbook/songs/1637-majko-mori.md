## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Oh, mother, old mother,
> you have an only son,
> mother, please, please.
> A son who loves a poor girl,
> mother, please, please.
>
> They tied their hearts,
> under the moon they swore silently,
> mother, please, please.
> Mother, you did not let them,
> mother, please, please.
>
> They left with bad words,
> like a knife in a living wound,
> mother, please, please.
> Mothers separated them,
> mother, please, please.
>
> The son went far,
> watered the road with tears,
> mother, please, please.
> And the girl stayed at home,
> with a black thought of growing old,
> mother, please, please.
>
> The son fell young,
> under a foreign sky, without forgiveness,
> mother, please, please.
> The girl put on a wreath,
> with love she lay in the grave,
> mother, please, please.
