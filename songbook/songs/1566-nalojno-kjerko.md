# Налојно ќерко

*Nalojno ḱerko* · *Naloјno, daughter*

Pesna.org #1566: <https://pesna.org/song/1566>

**Notes.** Шилегар е овчар. Шилеже е младо јагне, доцна ојагнето.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Налојно/Налојна' is probably a name or epithet for the daughter; my reading, I could not identify the word. The '(Друга верзија)' line is translated as '(Another version)'.
