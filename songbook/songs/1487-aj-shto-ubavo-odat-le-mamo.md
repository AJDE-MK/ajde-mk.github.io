# Ај што убаво одат ле мамо

*Aj što ubavo odat le mamo* · *How beautifully they walk, oh mother*

Pesna.org #1487: <https://pesna.org/song/1487>

**Notes.** Ајта (ајтиче) е широк, бел празничен фустан.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. 'тувенчиња' is a guess (my reading: kerchiefs), and 'сите ми се блеат' is my reading (they all bleat/coo, i.e. speak sweetly). Ајта is a wide white festive dress.
