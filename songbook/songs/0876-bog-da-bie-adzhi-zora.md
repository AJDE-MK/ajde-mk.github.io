## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, reads as traditional. 'На чучета' is my reading ('to a pup'), and 'алт'н до два' as 'two gold coins' is also my reading; dialect forms like додоја/посакаја are 3rd person plural past.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| аџи | hadji (title of one who made the pilgrimage) |
| посакаја | they asked for (in marriage) |
| појас | belt, sash |
| алтън | gold (Turkish loan, gold coin) |
| чест | honor |
| тука/туку | but (dialect 'туку') |
| расипеш | you will ruin |
