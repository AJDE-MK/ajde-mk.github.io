## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> The day has dawned, the sun has risen high,
> come over Mariovo,
> all over flat Pelagonija.
> Oh, it is joy to see
> the mountains, Macedonian villages.
> Over Kruševo and Prilep
> and over Bitola.
>
> The day has dawned, the sun has risen high,
> over Pelister Mountain,
> all over the Prespa district.
> Oh, it is joy to see
> the whole Prespa district.
> Lake Prespa,
> a beautiful pearl.
>
> The day has dawned, the sun has risen high,
> come over Galicica,
> all over the Ohrid district.
> Oh, it is joy to see
> the ancient Ohrid and beautiful Struga.
> Lake Ohrid,
> a pearl of pearls.
