## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Како да кандисам' as 'I felt like' is my reading (кандисам: to be persuaded / feel like); 'мале' is rendered 'mother' as a vocative filler.
