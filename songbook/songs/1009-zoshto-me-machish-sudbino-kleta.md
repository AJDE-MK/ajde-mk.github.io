## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear: sounds like a modern composed song about childlessness, but I cannot name an author. Line 'чедото мое ми го одзема' is read as fate keeping a child from her, my reading.
