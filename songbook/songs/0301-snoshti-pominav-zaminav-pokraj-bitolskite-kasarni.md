## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The refrain 'ben seni severim, chok seni severim' is Turkish for 'I love you, I love you very much' (my reading of the transliteration). Given note: some versions say Prilep barracks instead of Bitola.
