## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Дувло' is a dialect word I am unsure of; 'shop' is my reading (maybe 'hovel/den'). 'Проваѓа' rendered 'passes through' is my reading. 'Затрепери' in the title is the perfective 'began to tremble'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| планина | mountain |
| војвода | voivode, rebel chieftain |
| чета | armed band |
| комит | komit, rebel fighter (komitadji) |
| предај се | surrender |
| заробимо | we take prisoner |
| љута | fierce, fiery |
| дувло | (my reading) shop, hollow or lair |
