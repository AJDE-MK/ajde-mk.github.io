*Golemata družina* · *Great company*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> What a great company we were,
> a great company, all patriots.
> Come on, the time has come to go to war,
> to go to war, to defend our country.
>
> To defend our country from the wretched enemies,
> who have invaded our Macedonian land,
> Who have invaded our Macedonian land,
> to burn our houses, to kill our brothers.
>
> May God grant that we may return alive,
> to return alive, to our mothers.
> Let us go to cool taverns,
> to drink wine, to love our women.
