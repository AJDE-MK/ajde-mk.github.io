# Ситна ми роса зароси

*Sitna mi rosa zarosi* · *Fine dew has settled*

Pesna.org #1515: <https://pesna.org/song/1515>

## Lyrics

| --- | --- | --- |
| Ситна ми роса зароси | Sitna mi rosa zarosi | Fine dew has settled |
| горе Селечка Планина | gore Selečka Planina | up on Selechka Mountain, |
| падна ми јунак прободен | padna mi junak proboden | a hero has fallen, pierced |

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (partisan or revolutionary folk-style). Selechka Planina is a mountain in the Prespa/Kozhuf area (my understanding, unsure).
