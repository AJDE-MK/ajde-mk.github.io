## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Humorous dance song from Veles; origin unclear, I cannot name an author. 'ми седнал' and 'ми гледа' use a dative 'ми' in dialect style, 'гајтан веѓи' is my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |

| момите | the girls |
