## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Гори Балкан' could be 'the Balkan burns' or 'the Balkan forests'; I chose burns (my reading). 'Wedding with beasts' presumably refers to a battle, which is my reading.
