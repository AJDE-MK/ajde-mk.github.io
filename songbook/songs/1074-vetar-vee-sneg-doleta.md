## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional pechalbar (migrant labourer) style song. 'Чук мој верен' and 'камак црн' are my reading (tool and stone being worked); source spelling has a stray 'j' for 'ј' which I read normally.

| сиромаштија | poverty |
