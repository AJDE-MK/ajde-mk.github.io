## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'сè си апнува' is my reading (everything gets hooked/captivated); 'апнува' is a dialect word I am unsure about. 'Костурски' refers to Kostur (Kastoria).
