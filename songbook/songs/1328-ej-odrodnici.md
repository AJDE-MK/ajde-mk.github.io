## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** About the 1948 Aegean Macedonian child refugees; origin unclear and clearly modern, but I cannot name an author. 'Беломорје' (Aegean) and the verbs 'разнебитат/однародат' are my reading (break up / denationalise).

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| одродник | renegade, traitor to one's people |
| напатени | tormented, suffered |
| распадени | scattered |
| огниште | hearth, family home |
| растуривте | you scattered, destroyed |
| род | kin, clan |
| Беломорје | the Aegean coast (White Sea) |
| клетва | curse |
| раздени | to dawn |
