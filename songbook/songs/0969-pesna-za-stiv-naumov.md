## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Commemorates Stefan (Stiv) Naumov, a people's hero of the WWII liberation war (per the source notes); origin of the song unclear, I cannot name an author. 'Одови' in the last line is my reading as 'from now on / for these times'; I rendered it loosely as 'for us'.
