## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Maiden Filka prays to God
> to make her into a bird,
> to fly over the wide sea
> to go into Solun.
>
> To go into Solun
> in the bazaar, to the booths,
> to buy some textile
> to tailor a gown.
>
> To tailor a gown
> long, long and wide,
> waving all the way down to the ground,
> caressing the cobble.
>
> Filka will then dance the oro,
> the oro of Kukush,
> where only unmarried men dance,
> only unmarried men, from Kukush.
>
> So that the unmarried men see her,
> the unmarried men from Kukush,
> so that the neighbors will envy her,
> her neighbors, her enemies.
>
> Note: Kukush is a town in Aegean Macedonia (Northern Greece) which was primarily populated with Macedonians and Bulgarians under the Ottoman rule.
