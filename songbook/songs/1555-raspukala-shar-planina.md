# Распукала Шар Планина

*Raspukala Šar Planina* · *Shar Mountain Burst Open*

Pesna.org #1555: <https://pesna.org/song/1555>

**Notes.** Ова е авторска песна од Ленче и Горан Кукиќ, премиерно изведена во рамки на музичкиот фестивал Тетовски филиграни во 1996 година. Кликнете на следниот линк за да ја отворите традиционалната песна „Се навали Шар Планина“.

## Background

An authored song by Lenče and Goran Kukić, first performed at the Tetovo Filigrani music festival in 1996. Pesna.org points to a separate traditional song, *Се навали Шар Планина* (#516). Mood: proud, rousing, a celebration of song and dance as a force that carries a people through the centuries.

*Source of these notes: the note on Pesna.org.*

## English

*A composed song with a known author, so there is no line-by-line English here: a summary and a word list instead, so you can follow along.*

**Your translation.** Written by you, not by me. I checked it line by line against the Cyrillic.

> Verse 1:
> Shar stretches out her hand to build a bridge,
> so that a day may dawn, hey, higher
> than all the ages.
> Voices from lush hills whirl the kolo dance,
> pearly songs, to build a bridge,
> so that a day may dawn.
>
> Refrain:
> From a heavy dance, from a mighty song,
> Shar Mountain has burst open.
>
> Verse 2:
> Like a white galley in the high sky
> she has set sail, like the songs
> through the centuries.
> And with our song we lived out our age,
> we lived, and even the hardest
> we got through.
>
> Refrain
>
> Verse 3:
> Let the maidens' dance whirl now, the kolo,
> the brides', hey, let everything
> Macedonian dance.
> Come, all together, let it thunder,
> the bachelors' dance, let everything
> Macedonian shake.
>
> Refrain

**What it is about.** Author song (per the source note, by Lenche and Goran Kukikj, first performed at the Tetovo Filigrani festival in 1996), so no line-by-line version. Mood: proud, rousing, patriotic celebration of song and dance as a force that carries a people through the centuries. Verse 1: Shar Mountain stretches out a hand to build a bridge so that a day can dawn higher than all the ages, while voices from the lush hills whirl the dance and pearly songs build that bridge. Refrain: out of heavy dance and strong song, Shar Mountain has burst open (my reading: also suggests blossoming). Verse 2: song sails across the sky like a white ship, just as songs travel through the centuries, and by song the people lived their age and got through even the hardest times. Verse 3: let the maidens' and brides' dance swirl and the bachelors' dance thunder until everything Macedonian shakes. Grammar: 'в'' is a contraction of 'во' (in), 'сè' means 'everything' here, and 'распукала' is a feminine l-participle (the mountain is feminine) used without an auxiliary, as is common in songs.
