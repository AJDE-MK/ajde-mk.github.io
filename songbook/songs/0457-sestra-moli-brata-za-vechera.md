## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The verb forms in -ло (јало, пило, заклало) are dialect past forms with the speaker as 'I'. 'Сестра има брате девет сина' is my reading: the sister has nine sons and the mother gives one up.
