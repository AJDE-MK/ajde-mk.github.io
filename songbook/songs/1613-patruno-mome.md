## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Patruna, beautiful girl,
> Patruna, thin and tall,
> Why do you pass by my gates?
> Why is my heart burning for you?
>
> Patruna, beautiful girl,
> Oh, go ask your mother,
> If she will give you to me,
> To me, my love, my soul.
>
> Oh come on, you are a crazy fool,
> How can I ask my mother?
> I am already engaged,
> With Koljo zengija1.
>
> Patruna, beautiful girl,
> Oh, I will elope with you,
> I have wealth of my own:
> The wide fields of Meglen.
