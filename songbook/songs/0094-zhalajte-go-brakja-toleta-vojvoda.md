# Жалајте го браќа Толета војвода

*Žalajte go braḱa Toleta vojvoda* · *Mourn him, brothers, Voivode Tole*

Pesna.org #94: <https://pesna.org/song/94>

## Background

A lament for the voivoda Tole, who died for his people and for freedom. Qwen links him to the Kruševo region and the Ilinden generation; I have not checked who he is.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Commemorative song about a voivode named Tole; I do not know if it is anonymous or composed, so it is treated as folk-style.
