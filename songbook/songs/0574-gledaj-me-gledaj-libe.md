## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Partisan-era song. 'ќ' одам' is a contracted 'ќе одам'; 'ѕверој фашисти' read as 'beastly fascists', my reading. Славеј Планина and Караорман are place names as written.
