*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, origin unclear. 'бабо мори' is an address to the father/grandfather (бабо can mean father in dialect); I render it 'grandfather hey', which is my reading.
