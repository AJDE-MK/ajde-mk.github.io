## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Абер стори' is my reading 'sent word' (Turkish haber); 'му врзаа' dialectal 'they bound his...'. 'Горски змија' is as written, likely 'змии'.
