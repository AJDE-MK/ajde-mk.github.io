## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** First lines read 'why are you washing white things and wearing black' (my reading of 'а што бело переш'). Implied meaning is that the soldier died.

| либе | sweetheart, beloved |
| војник | soldier |
