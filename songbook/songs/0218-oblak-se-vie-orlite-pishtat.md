## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Note says this is a version of 'Песна за Караџата'. 'Спици се прават' read as skewers/spikes used for torture, my reading; 'усти затворат' read as 'mouths are shut' (dialect).
