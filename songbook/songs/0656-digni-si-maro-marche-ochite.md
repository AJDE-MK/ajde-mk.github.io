## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Lines 6 and 7 ('свршија ли ергените / како за тебе момите') are my reading: roughly 'have the bachelors run out, the way girls have for you?'. 'Свршија' may also be dialectal for 'finished/run out'.
