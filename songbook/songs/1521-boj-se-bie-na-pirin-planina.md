## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-style song, origin unclear. 'Го мие' and 'го реди' refer to the head of Stojan (my reading); 'реди' could also mean 'arranges' as in preparing the dead.
