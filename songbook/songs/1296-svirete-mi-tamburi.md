## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; sounds like a newer tavern-style (kafana) song, but I cannot name an author. 'Тури' in 'две-три тури' is my reading as 'rounds/pours'; 'Шоиќ' looks like a surname, kept as Šoić.
