## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Pray to God, my son Stojan,
> that the beys may perish.
> That the beys may perish,
> and the hanums1 remain.
>
> Yashar Bey, Osman Bey,
> the whole lot, one by one.
>
> That the hanums remain,
> and to caress the giaours2.
> To caress the giaours,
> and pay the beys back in kind.
>
> Yashar Bey, Osman Bey,
> the whole lot, one by one.
>
> Do not, Mother, do not curse them,
> let them live, Mother, let them live.
> Let them see with their own eyes,
> yet die within their souls.
>
> Yashar Bey, Osman Bey,
> the whole lot, one by one.
