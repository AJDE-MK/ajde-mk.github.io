## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Hey Ance, white and red,
> you don't love me, white Ance,
> you don't love me, hey.
>
> I both love you, dear Dimco,
> and I am sweeping the yard, dear Dimco,
> and I am sweeping the yard, hey.
>
> I had a bad dream, dear Dimco,
> a night star, dear Dimco,
> a night star, hey.
>
> A night star, dear Dimco,
> fell into my lap, dear Dimco,
> and burned my lap, hey.
>
> Give me permission Ance, white Ance,
> to go to work abroad, white Ance,
> to go to work abroad, hey.
>
> To go to work abroad, white Ance,
> to earn some money, white Ance,
> to marry you, hey.
