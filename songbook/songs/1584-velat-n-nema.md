## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> We have been here for centuries,
> in this beautiful Aegean Sea.
> Here are our holy roots
> from the same mothers born here.
>
> They say we are gone, but here we are,
> willingly dancing оро in the middle of Solun.
> They say we are gone, but here we are,
> proudly singing Macedonian songs.
>
> Here in Solun, Drama, Kavala
> in Kukush, Voden, pretty Grevena,
> up there on Vicho, heroic Gramos,
> they dance and sing again in the Macedonian language.
>
> They say we are gone, but here we are,
> willingly dancing оро in the middle of Thessaloniki.
> They say we are gone, but here we are,
> proudly singing Macedonian songs.
>
> We have been here since before Jesus Christ,
> on our Macedonian land.
> We are here, we remain here,
> and we do not change our holy name.
>
> They say we are gone, but here we are,
> willingly dancing оро in the middle of Solun.
> They say we are gone, but here we are,
> proudly singing Macedonian songs.
