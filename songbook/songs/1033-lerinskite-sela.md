## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Modern song about the Aegean Macedonia (Lerin/Florina) refugees; origin unclear and no author named. Village names are kept as transliterated, and the second line of stanza 1 is my reading (the refugee 'you' addressed to the mother).
