## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Last line 'збори по мен' меракот' is my reading: 'speak as I like / according to my wish'; it could also mean 'speak by my fancy'. The first line of the reply is my reading as well.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| чифлик | estate, large farm |
| бег | beg (Ottoman landlord title) |
| ага | aga (Ottoman title, 'sir, master') |
| мерак | desire, pleasure, whim (Turkish loan) |
| моме | maiden, girl |
