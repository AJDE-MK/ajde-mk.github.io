## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Love song to Biljana (Bile is her pet name); origin unclear, I cannot name an author. The line 'врвој Биле Пелистерски' is my reading ('over', i.e. like the heights of Pelister).
