## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** A partisan-era song; author not known to me, so origin unclear. 'Со песни и радост се мрее' is my reading: 'we yearn/long with songs and joy'; 'Goce' refers to Goce Delchev.
