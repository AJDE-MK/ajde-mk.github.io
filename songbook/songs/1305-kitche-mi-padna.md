## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Величе' and 'дадо' are vocatives; I read 'дадо' as a refrain address, not necessarily a literal father. 'Тој бело лице' is my reading: he wants a kiss on the face as payment.
