## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I'm coming back from town drunk,
> Velika, you beautiful girl,
> I'm coming back from town drunk.
>
> I met a girl in the meadow,
> Velika, you beautiful girl,
> while she was plucking grass.
>
> Where she was plucking, there she fell asleep,
> Velika, you beautiful girl,
> and covered herself with grass.
>
> She put a stone under her head,
> Velika, you beautiful girl,
> and covered herself with grass.
>
> I tried to kiss her,
> Velika, you beautiful girl,
> but the girl broke into a run.
>
> - Stay girl, wait for me,
> Velika, you beautifull girl,
> you are mine, mine again.
