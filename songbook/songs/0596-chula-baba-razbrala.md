## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Ќе те дарвам' read as 'I will give you a gift'; 'до вечера мој да си' is my reading (may you be mine until evening). Humorous song.
