## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, treated as traditional. The refrain 'достум тривели' is left untranslated because I am unsure of its meaning (possibly 'my friend' plus a nonsense word); 'ј'ожени' is my reading of a contracted dialect form.
