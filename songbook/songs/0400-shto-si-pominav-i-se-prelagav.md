## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Се прелагав' is my reading (dawdled or idled as I passed); 'ѝ потропнав' with the foot is taken as a stamp or foot-tap signal.
