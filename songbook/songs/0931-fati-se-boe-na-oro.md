## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Boe is a name, probably a girl's, given her sex in the 'фустанче' exchange (my reading). 'Лудо' is an affectionate address, rendered 'you fool'.

**Words to learn**
