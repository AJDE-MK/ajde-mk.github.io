## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> We Macedonians
> are lovers of life's pleasures,
> that is how we were born,
> we have a passion for everything.
>
> For strong, clear rakija,
> and for rich red wine,
> to revel in the taverns,
> to revel with other men's wives.
>
> We Macedonians
> live like beys,
> that is how we were born,
> we have a passion for everything.
>
> For strong, clear rakija,
> and for rich red wine,
> to revel in the taverns,
> to revel with other men's wives.
>
> We Macedonians
> have one weakness:
> when we start drinking,
> we think only of women.
>
> For strong, clear rakija,
> and for rich red wine,
> to revel in the taverns,
> to revel with other men's wives.
