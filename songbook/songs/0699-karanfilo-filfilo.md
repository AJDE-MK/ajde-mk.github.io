## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'филфило' is unclear to me and I left it untranslated (possibly a made-up or dialect epithet). 'Друшките' I read as 'дружките' (her girl friends); that and the exact sense of 'на каранфил мирисаш' are my reading.
