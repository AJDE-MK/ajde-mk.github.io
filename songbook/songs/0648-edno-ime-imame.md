# Едно име имаме

*Edno ime imame* · *We have one name*

Pesna.org #648: <https://pesna.org/song/648>

## Background

Credited to Jonče Hristovski in your songbook. A patriotic song; the title is a statement of shared Macedonian identity. Qwen's draft says the verses name Macedonians along the Aegean, along the Vardar and across Pirin, and calls it a modern folk-style song rather than a folk song (unchecked).

*Source of these notes: your 6-song songbook, with a note from Qwen's draft.*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Alan Moore)*

> By the Aegean, Macedonians,
> by the Vardar, Macedonians
> and below Pirin, Macedonians
> a glorious name, defiant.
>
> We have one name
> in our soul we keep it
> we give our lives for you,
> Macedonia!
>
> As a carpet, fields,
> and mountains stretching to the sky,
> golden rivers,
> and lakes, pearls.
>
> We have one name
> in our soul we keep it
> we give our lives for you,
> Macedonia!
>
> Mother Macedonia
> Balkan beauty,
> whoever was born in you
> will also give his life for you.
>
> We have one name
> in our soul we keep it
> we give our lives for you,
> Macedonia!
