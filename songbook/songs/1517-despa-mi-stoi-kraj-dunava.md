## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional ballad. 'Девет рала тапани' (literally 'nine ploughs of drums') is obscure; 'nine ploughs' worth of drums' is my reading.
