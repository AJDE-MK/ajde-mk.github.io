## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> The moment I see you, I lose my head,
> why wait, my dear?
> Let’s seize the moment,
> let’s make something happen.
> Money, gold, a new car,
> whatever you want, my dear.
>
> Let’s make love, let’s go wild,
> for a fire burns within us,
> we’ll burn up.
> We’ll grow old and then regret it,
> because by then, there’ll be nothing left
> for us to do.
>
> Don’t be shy, we aren’t children,
> you know what awaits us, my dear.
> Let’s seize the moment,

> whatever you want, my dear.
