## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Ајде' is an opening filler ('come on'). The last line 'нас двајца ќе нè однеси' is my reading: the dew will carry the two away (the verb form 'однеси' looks irregular).
