## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> From high above
> the young policeman
> he's coming straight
> to the village square.
>
> In the village square,
> by the white fountain
> there he found her,
> the beautiful young Rumena.
>
> Where she fills
> cold spring water.
> - Give me water,
> cold spring water.
>
> So I can quench my thirst,
> to cool my young heart,
> for my yearnings
> to pass through me
> and if I die,
> never to regret anything.
