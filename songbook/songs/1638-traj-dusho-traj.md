## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - It's hard for me, my mother,
> the night is falling, he's not coming.
> I haven't slept for so many nights,
> he's sitting in a cold tavern.
>
> - Hold on, my dear, hold on,
> hold on, don't fall asleep.
> Hold on, my dear, hold on,
> don't shed a tear.
> - He'll come back for long nights,
> sleepless nights.
> While I'm waiting for him to return,
> he's getting drunk.
>
> It's hard for me, my mother,
> midnight has passed, he's not coming.
> I'm sitting alone in the night,
> body is burning, heart is traveling.
>
> - Hold on, my dear, hold on,
> hold on, don't fall asleep.
> Hold on, my dear, hold on,
> don't shed a tear.
> - He'll come back for long nights,
> sleepless nights.
> While I'm waiting for him to return,
> he's getting drunk.
>
> It's hard for me, my mother,
> dawn is breaking, he isn't coming.
> Damn fate, what have I done to you,
> what have I done wrong to you, that you burn me?
>
> - Hold on, my dear, hold on,
> hold on, don't fall asleep.
> Hold on, my dear, hold on,
> don't shed a tear.
> - He'll come back for long nights,
> sleepless nights.
> While I'm waiting for him to return,
> he's getting drunk.
