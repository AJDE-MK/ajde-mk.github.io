## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Nevena, oh dear, marigold in the garden,
> Nevena, my dear, a mountain marigold.
> Nevena, thin, tall,
> marigold, Nevena, a wide shadow.
>
> Who were you, oh dear, waiting for last night
> and who, my dear, did you look into the eyes?
> Who did you speak to like that
> when, when did you burn with love?
>
> - Since you ask me, oh dear Jovan,
> my brother's oh, faithful friend.
> Who was I waiting for last night
> who, who did I look into the eyes?
>
> When the moon showed up
> my heart, my dear sang.
> And she no longer listens,
> I have only one love in my soul.
