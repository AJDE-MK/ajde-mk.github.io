## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'ј'око мори калешо' is my reading (a filler addressed to the beloved, rendered 'hey, my dear'); 'моја прва изгоро' is my reading ('my first one, my burnt one'). 'Селвие, сен ѓузел' is Turkish-influenced (sen guzel = you are beautiful).
