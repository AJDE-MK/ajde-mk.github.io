*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Custom song from Struga Malesija, apparently with a final line demanding payment. 'Ивчекало в' година' is my reading as 'stayed through a year'.
