## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Young Stoyan lies wounded,
> young Stoyan, young voyvoda,
> through that green mountain,
> through that Pirin mountain.
>
> There was no one anywhere
> to give him water,
> to give him some water,
> to make him a shadow.
>
> A great mountain was saying:
> - Drop leaves, drop,
> drop leaves, drop,
> cover my cold grave.
