## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> When I left, Cveta, for foreign lands,
> You shed bitter tears,
> Bitter tears, Cveta, you shed,
> And swore to God.
>
> That you would be faithful, Cveta,
> Take care of the house and children,
> But I, Cveta, had no faith,
> So I quickly returned.
>
> Our gate, Cveta, was closed,
> The children fast asleep,
> The little lamp was dimmed,
> And you, Cveta, at the neighbour’s house.
>
> And you, Cveta, at the neighbour’s,
> Drinking black coffee,
> Drinking black coffee, Cveta,
> Having a pleasant chat.
>
> As you wish, Cveta, so you act,
> Not thinking of me.
