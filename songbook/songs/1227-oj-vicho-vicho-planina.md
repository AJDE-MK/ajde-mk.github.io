## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Lerin is Florina and Kostur is Kastoria. 'Подздиската' (Podzdiska) is a place name I am unsure of. Origin unclear, possibly a modern patriotic song, but no author known to me.
