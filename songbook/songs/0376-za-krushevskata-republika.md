# За Крушевската Република

*Za Kruševskata Republika* · *For the Krusevo Republic*

Pesna.org #376: <https://pesna.org/song/376>

## Background

Listed in your songbook as one of the Ilinden songs to explore ("For the Kruševo Republic"). Qwen's draft describes it as a history lesson set to music: it names Nikola Karev as the republic's first president and lists Ilinden leaders such as Damjan Gruev, Pitu Guli and Jane Sandanski (unchecked). Not checked whether the text is traditional or a later composition.

*Source of these notes: your 6-song songbook and Qwen's draft.*

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; a patriotic song about the 1903 Ilinden uprising and the Krusevo Republic. 'Гора смени лисја' is rendered literally; my reading is that it means the forest passed through many seasons.
