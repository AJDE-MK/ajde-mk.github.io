## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, origin unclear. 'Вездeн' (written 'вездeн') is my reading of 'везден' = 'all day, always'; the line could also be read differently. 'Од една аир да нема' is my reading: 'to get no joy from one of them'.
