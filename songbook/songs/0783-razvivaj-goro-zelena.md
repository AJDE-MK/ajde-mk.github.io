## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional revolutionary song about Gotse Delchev. The last two lines are unclear: 'потера да дадам' I read as 'to send a pursuit/posse', but it may mean the bey's son wants to hide in the forest; my reading.
