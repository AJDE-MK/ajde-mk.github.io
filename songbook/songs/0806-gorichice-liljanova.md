## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Лилјанова, лилјакова' and 'чакарака петстотини' are unclear; my reading of the latter is 'five hundred worthless ones' (чакарака perhaps a Turkish-derived word for something cheap). Marked as guesses.
