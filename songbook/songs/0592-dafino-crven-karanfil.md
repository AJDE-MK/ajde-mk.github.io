## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Лудо' is read as a teasing term of address, not literally 'mad'. 'Ветила' as 'promised in marriage' and the last line of verse 7 are my reading.
