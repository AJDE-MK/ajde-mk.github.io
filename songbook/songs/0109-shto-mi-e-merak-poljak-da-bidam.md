## English

*From Pesna.org*

> I have a desire1 to be a field guard2
> My dear Bozhano3,
> To be a field guard in your fields,
> Dear one (young girl).
>
> In your fields, in your vast fields,
> My dear Bozhano,
> You to cut (harvest), me to watch you,
> Dear one (young girl).
>
> I have a desire to be a field guard,
> My dear Bozhano,
> To be a field guard in your grape fields,
> Dear one (young girl).
>
> In your grape fields, your white grapes,
> My dear Bozhano,
> You to gather, me to watch you,
> Dear one (young girl).
>
> I have a desire to be a chauffeur4,
> My dear Bozhano,
> To be a chauffeur, to drive you,
> Dear one (young girl).
