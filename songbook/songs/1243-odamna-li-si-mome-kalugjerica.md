## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Are you, girl, a nun
> for a long time?
> - I am neither for a long time, pretty lad,
> nor recently.
>
> - For whom are you keeping, girl,
> those black eyes?
> - I am keeping them, pretty lad,
> for the black earth.
>
> - The black earth, girl,
> will not be grateful.
> It will not be grateful, girl,
> and it will not be happy.
>
> Give them to me, girl,
> I will be grateful.
> I will be grateful, girl,
> and I will be happy.
