# Дафино вино црвено

*Dafino vino crveno* · *Dafina, red wine*

Pesna.org #421: <https://pesna.org/song/421>

**Notes.** Каракамен планина е планински масив во Егејска Македонија, јужно од градот Воден и источно од Кожани. Грчкиот назив ѝ е Vermio (Βέρμιο).

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Alan Williams)*

> Dafina, red wine,
> your boyfriend was asleep.
> Your boyfriend was asleep,
> on Karakamen mountain.
>
> On Karakamen mountain,
> on a slope without water.
> Along came hajduks
> and stole his vest.
>
> They stole his vest
> and sold it in a tavern.
> They stole his vest
> and sold it in a tavern.
>
> Sold it in a tavern,
> exchanged it for red wine,
> Exchanged it for red wine,
> and fine strong rakija.
>
> I don't care if they stole his vest,
> as long as my boyfriend is alive.
> I don't care if they stole his vest,
> as long as my boyfriend is alive.
>
> Note: "hajduk" means a rebel, but can also mean a thief. "Rakija" is a strong alcoholic beverage, spirits.
