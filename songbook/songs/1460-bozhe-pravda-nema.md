## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Modern song about Aegean Macedonia; origin unclear. 'Душмани јазикот ни бранеа' is my reading (the verb form is odd, could mean 'enemies banned our language'); 'македонски децата' is as written.
