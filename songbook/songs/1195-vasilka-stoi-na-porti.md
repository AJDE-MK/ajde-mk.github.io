## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. 'бела коконо' is my reading as 'white lady' (fair young woman); the last stanza is a short back-and-forth between the two lovers.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| порта | gate, front gate |
| коконa | lady, young woman of standing (from Greek) |
| лично моме | fair, handsome girl |
| бекрија | carouser, tavern-goer |
| пијаница | drunkard |
| умирам за | I am dying for (someone) |
| изгорев | I burned up |
| земиме | we marry, we take each other |
| џанам | my dear (filler) |
