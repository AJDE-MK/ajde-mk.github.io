## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> - Maiden, dear maiden,
> dear maiden, golden turtledove,
> you pass by me but won’t speak,
> why do you hate me so?
>
> Your mouth is honey-sweet,
> your neck is so sugary,
> your teeth are pearls.
>
> - You foolish lad, don’t speak,
> don’t shake my heart,
> because my heart is already shaken.
>
> My father died recently,
> yesterday my mother died,
> my brothers are doomed.
