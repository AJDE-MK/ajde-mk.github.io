## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Коja ли гора да фатам' is my reading as a wail about where to flee or wander; the basil-and-flower exchange is a folk courtship image.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| босилче | basil (little basil, a courtship plant) |
| зумбулче | little hyacinth |
| бавча | garden |
| молчи | hush, be quiet |
| либе | sweetheart |
| никнало | came up, sprouted |
| гора | forest |
| фатам | I take, I catch |
