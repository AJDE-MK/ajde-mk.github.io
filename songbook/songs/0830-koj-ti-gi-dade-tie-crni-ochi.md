## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Who gave you those black eyes?
> Give them to me, my first love.
> - Those black eyes, crazy lad, my mother gave to me,
> get away from here, crazy lad, my big love.
>
> - Who gave you those black eyebrows?
> Give them to me, my first love.
> - Those black eyebrows, crazy lad, my mother gave to me,
> get away from here, crazy lad, my big love.
>
> - Who gave you that tall body?
> Give them to me, my first love.
> - That tall body, crazy lad, my mother gave to me,
> get away from here, crazy lad, my big love.
