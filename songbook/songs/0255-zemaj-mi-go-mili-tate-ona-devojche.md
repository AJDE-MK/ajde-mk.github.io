## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Миразличе' is my reading (a girl who brings a dowry); 'их' is an interjection. 'Тук'' is dialectal and my reading.
