## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Who 'Јана мајка' is (a mother named Jana, or Jana cursing her mother) is ambiguous; I took it as written. 'Да се видам' translated as 'see anyone' is my reading; 'метам' (spread/lay out) with courtyards is also a loose reading.
