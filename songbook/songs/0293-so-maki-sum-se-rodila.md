*So maki sum se rodila* · *I was born with suffering*

Pesna.org #293: <https://pesna.org/song/293>

**Notes.** „Алов“ значи црвен, а „катмер“ значи слоевит, во слоеви.

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I was born with suffering,
> with sorrow I'll die.
> I want you to write down my sorrows
> on top of my grave.
>
> I'll climb up the mountain,
> I'll enter dark caves.
> Let my eyes go blind,
> never to see the Sun again.
>
> I'll go to the rose garden,
> to get that red-layered carnation.
> To get that red-layered carnation,
> that early white basil.

Also on Pesna.org: Dutch, Turkish, Polish, Greek.
