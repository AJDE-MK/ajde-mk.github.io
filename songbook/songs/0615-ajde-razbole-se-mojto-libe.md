## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Па да си умре' is my reading ('and then let him die'); it may mean 'before he dies'. 'Јазе' is the dialect form of 'јас'.
