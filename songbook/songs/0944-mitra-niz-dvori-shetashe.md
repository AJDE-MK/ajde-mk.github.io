## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, origin unclear. The agha stanzas are a curse with a double sense ('let my agha come' reads ironic); 'рамна си снага кршеше' as bending her slim waist is my reading.
