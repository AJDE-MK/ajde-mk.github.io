## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Modern patriotic song; origin unclear (no author I can name). 'Два Илиндена' probably refers to 1903 and 1944.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |

| крај | region, homeland |
| земја | land, country |
| извира | rises, springs |
| Илинден | St. Elijah's Day, 2 August (1903 uprising) |
| слобода | freedom |
| крв | blood |
| лееше | was pouring/shedding |
| натопени | soaked |
