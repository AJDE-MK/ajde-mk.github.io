# Малечко убо Лазаре

*Malečko ubo Lazare* · *Little boy Lazar*

Pesna.org #1070: <https://pesna.org/song/1070>

**Notes.** Песната им се пее на децата за време на празникот Лазарица (Лазарева Сабота).

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional Lazarica song, sung to children at the Lazarica custom (Lazar's Saturday). Dialect forms данж, догледат, поклонит (third person without -ат/-т ending), my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| малечко | little (child) |
| убо | poor lad, little one (dialect) |
| диван | hall, sitting room |
| јаболко | apple |
| цар | emperor, tsar |
| царство | kingdom |
| здогледа | to catch sight of |
| данж | some day (dialect) |
