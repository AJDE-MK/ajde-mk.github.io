# Зурли трештат на сред село

*Zurli treštat na sred selo* · *Zurnas are blasting in the middle of the village*

Pesna.org #239: <https://pesna.org/song/239>

## Background

A dance song with a grandfather as the hero. Zurnas blast in the middle of the village, the drum beats, and young girls and bachelors wind two ora. An old white-bearded grandfather comes down, moustache twisted up, and heads straight for the oro to join. Grandma Neda catches his hand and asks where he is off to, to criticise the dancing. The point is that he joins.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Zurnas are blasting in the middle of the village,
> The drum beats rum-dum-dum,
> Young girls and boys
> dance in pairs and swivel.
>
> From below comes an old white-haired grandpa,
> Rolling up his moustache,
> Heading straight to the dance,
> Wanting to join in.
>
> Grandma Neda sees him,
> And grabs him by the hand:
> "Where are you going, you old goat
> To spoil their dance?"
>
> "Wait, hold on, Grandma Neda,
> I want to join the dance,
> To catch up with the young girls,
> And make my heart rejoice."
>
> Rum-dum-rum-dum-rum-dum-rum,
> Rum-dum-rum-dum-rum-dum-dum!
