## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - It's been three years now
> since it started
> to madly torment me
> my love.
>
> Oh, it's so hard,
> oh, it's so sad,
> for me to love you
> and someone else to marry you.
>
> Here, take this knife,
> and tear my heart open,
> you'll find there
> honest love.
>
> What was your mother like
> who gave birth to you,
> so that the whole world is amazed
> by your beauty?
>
> - My mother was
> pretty, cute,
> and she made me
> even prettier.
>
> - If I don't marry you
> as a young beauty,
> I will marry you
> as a black widow.
>
> If I don't marry you
> down on the Earth,
> I will marry you
> up in the Heavens.
