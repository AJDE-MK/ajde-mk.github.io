## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Dudule's* mother sits by the sea
> Hey Dudule, God give us rain.
>
> She sits by the sea and prays to God
> Hey Dudule, God give us rain.
>
> Let those fine dewdrops fall
> Hey Dudule, God give us rain.
>
> To water the field
> Hey Dudule, God give us rain.
>
> Abundance to be born in the field
> Hey Dudule, God give us rain.
