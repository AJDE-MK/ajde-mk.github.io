## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Чикма одаја' is a guess, left as 'chikma room' (my reading: some kind of room or hall). Song honours the Kavadarci komita Lazo Ridov-Asijata; no composer known to me, so treated as folk.
