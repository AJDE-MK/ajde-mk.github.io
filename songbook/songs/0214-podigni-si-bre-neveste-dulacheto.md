## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Lift up, hey bride, your veil,
> let me see, hey bride, your white face.
> No matter if you see it, crazy lad,
> my white face, crazy lad, is sold.
>
> Lift up, hey bride, your veil
> let me see, hey bride, your black eyes,
> black eyes, hey bride, dark eyebrows.
> No matter if you see it, crazy lad,
> my black eyes, crazy lad, are sold,
> my dark eyebrows, crazy lad, are sold.
>
> My father took, crazy lad, twenty liras,
> my mother took, crazy lad, silver-embroidered vest.
