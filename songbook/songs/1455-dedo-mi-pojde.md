## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> My grandfather went to the market,
> there he bought a rooster,
> the rooster calls: - cock-a-doodle-doe,
> I will sing, grandfather, I will sing!
>
> My grandmother went to the market,
> there she bought a chicken,
> the hen calls: - cluck cluck,
> I'll bring you eggs, grandma, for you to eat!
>
> My grandfather went to the market,
> there he bought a dog,
> the dog shouts: - woof, woof,
> I will bark grandpa, I will bark!
>
> My grandmother went to the market,
> there she bought a cow,
> the cow shouts: - moo,
> I will give you milk, grandma, to drink!
>
> My grandfather went to the market,
> there he bought a donkey,
> the donkey shouts: - eee-aaaah,
> ride me grandfather, ride me!
>
> My grandmother went to the market,
> there she bought a sheep,
> the sheep calls: - baa, baa,
> I will give you wool, grandma, to spin!
