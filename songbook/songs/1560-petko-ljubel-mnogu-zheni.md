## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (comic, probably modern, no author I can name). 'На суво брич' and 'меша бак'рдан' are my reading; the second is probably a joking euphemism, the word бакардан is unknown to me and may be a dialect or invented word.
