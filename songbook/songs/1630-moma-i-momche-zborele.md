## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> A maiden and a young man were speaking,
> speaking, even whispering:
> – Tell me, my love, tell me,
> when will we have our wedding?
> Whisper wildly, so my heart can hear what you say,
> whisper wildly, my heart will burn for you.
>
> The young man speaks to the maiden:
> – Wait, my love, wait,
> we haven’t gathered the vineyards,
> we have no rakija nor wine.
> Whisper wildly, so my heart can hear what you say,
> whisper wildly, my heart will burn for you.
>
> The maiden speaks to the young man:
> – I cannot wait, my love,
> tonight the matchmakers are coming,
> they will marry me to another boy.
> Whisper wildly, so my heart can hear what you say,
> whisper wildly, my heart will burn for you.
>
> The young man speaks to the maiden:
> – I will not give you to another,
> prepare your home by tonight,
> I am coming with the matchmakers.
> Whisper wildly, so my heart can hear what you say,
> whisper wildly, my heart will burn for you.
