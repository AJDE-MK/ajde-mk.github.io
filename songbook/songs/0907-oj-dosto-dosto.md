## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional; the song cuts the last syllable of each first line and repeats it whole in the next. 'немо ѝ вадила' is my reading ('no one to water it'); 'врато' for 'врат' is dialect.

**Words to learn**

| босилек | basil |
| рид / рида | hill, rise |

| китка | flower, bouquet |
