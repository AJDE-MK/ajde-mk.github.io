## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I like a lot, grandma,
> your girl.
>
> Tell me, old grandma,
> will you give it to me?
>
> Wait, wait stranger,
> Easter to pass.
>
> Easter to pass, my son,
> she will tend our guests.
>
> Then you come, stranger,
> come, look at her.
>
> Come, look at her, my son,
> come, and take her.

Also on Pesna.org: German, Turkish, Polish, Greek.
