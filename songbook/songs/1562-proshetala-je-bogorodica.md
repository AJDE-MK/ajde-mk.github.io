# Прошетала је Богородица

*Prošetala je Bogorodica* · *The Virgin Mary walked*

Pesna.org #1562: <https://pesna.org/song/1562>

*Литурја- литургија.

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> The Virgin Mary walked
> through beautiful fields, through beautiful vineyards.
> All the fields stood up straight,
> stood up straight and bowed down.
> The cursed ervil neither stood up straight,
> neither stood up straight, nor bowed down.
> The Virgin Mary cursed it:
> - Hey ervil, ervil, cursed ervil,
> may you bear fruit, may you bear fruit,
> but never enter the liturgy,
> may animals and dust eat you.
