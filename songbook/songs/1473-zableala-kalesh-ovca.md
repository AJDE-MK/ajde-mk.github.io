## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional St George's Day song (lambs are slaughtered for the feast). 'Калеш' is a guess of mine: I read it as 'tame/gentle', but the exact meaning is uncertain. Last line 'сè моето јагне колат' is my reading: they keep slaughtering my lamb.
