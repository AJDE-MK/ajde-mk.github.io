## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional humorous song. 'Гарагачка' (magpie) and 'зелник' (a savory pie, here the place or dish) are my reading; the last verses are a joke about the new bride not knowing household things.
