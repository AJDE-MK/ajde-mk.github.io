## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Jana has a sore throat,
> it's not a throat ache as usual.
>
> It's not a throat ache as usual,
> instead it is a painful wound.
>
> Nobody believes Jana,
> neither her mother, nor her father.
>
> He who believed Jana
> is not here, he is far away.
>
> He is not here, he is far away,
> far away in the city of Solun.
