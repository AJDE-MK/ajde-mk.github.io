## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Partisan (WWII) song, apparently traditional or folk-style; no author known to me. 'Мi викна' is rendered as 'called out to me' (my reading).
