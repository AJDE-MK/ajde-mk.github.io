## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Beautiful girl Janika
> the sun is setting
> behind that flat hill.
>
> Behind the hill is a green pine,
> under the pine sit the workers.
> They were making a big board.
>
> Whoever picks a pomegranate,
> will take Janinka,
> the beautiful girl Janinka.
