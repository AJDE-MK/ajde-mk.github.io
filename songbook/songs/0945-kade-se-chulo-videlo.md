*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'к'носани' is my reading of 'кина-носани' (hennaed nails), and 'набелено' as whitened/made up with cosmetics; both are guesses. 'јамди аман' is a refrain exclamation, rendered as left-in-transliteration.
