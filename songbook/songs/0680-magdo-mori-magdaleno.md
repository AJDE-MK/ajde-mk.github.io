## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Magda dear, Magdalena,
> come sit, Magda, in my lap,
> unbutton my shirt,
> nor washed for three years.
> Please, oh please, please,
> Magda, the times are very hard.
>
> I don't have soap Gjorgji, dear,
> to wash your shirt,
> I don't have thread Gjorji, dear,
> to patch your shirt.
> Please, oh please, please,
> Magda, the times are very hard.
>
> Your hands are, Magda dear,
> like soap from Pirin,
> your hair is, Magda dear,
> like silk thread from Solun.
> Please, oh please, please,
> Magda, the times are very hard.
