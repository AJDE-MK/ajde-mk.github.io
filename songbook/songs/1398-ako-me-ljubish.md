## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> If you love me, if you want me,
> take off your shoes, put on my shoes,
> so we can go for a walk,
> so, golly, we can go for a walk.
>
> Up and down the gardens,
> to gather a scarf full of pears,
> a pear tree, maiden’s soul,
> a pear tree, golly, maiden’s soul.
>
> If you love me, if you want me,
> take off your shoes, put on my shoes,
> so we can go for a walk,
> so, golly, we can go for a walk.
>
> Up and down the vineyards,
> to gather a scarf full of grapes,
> me munching on grapes, you nibbling on grapes,
> the grape is a maidens’ eye.
