# Што ми омилело

*Što mi omilelo* · *What I have come to love*

Pesna.org #734: <https://pesna.org/song/734>

## Background

A gentle song of longing for a place, dressed up as longing for girls: what captured the singer is the Kumanovo plain, the Kumanovo girls and the *saie*, the embroidered woolen apron-dress, worked with little flower bouquets.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Kumanovo dialect forms (-е endings, к'д ќе ги погледну). Line 3 meaning ('when they look') and 'сае' as a woollen coat are my reading; the object of 'look' is unstated.
