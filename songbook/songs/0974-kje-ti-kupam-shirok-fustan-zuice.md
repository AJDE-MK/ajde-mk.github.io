## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding; origin unclear. Zuica is a girl's name (diminutive). 'Пусто да остане... гледање' is my reading: a girl's retort that his looking is worthless without a sweetheart beside her.
