## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, Pirin region. 'Јаница' I read as a young woman (could be a name or 'lamb'); 'пат збркала' is my reading as 'lost the way'.
