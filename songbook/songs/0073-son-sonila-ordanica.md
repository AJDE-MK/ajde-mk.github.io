## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Ordanica1 had a dream
> about Jordan Piperka2.
> She woke him up at midnight,
> and grabbed him by the hand.
>
> - Stand up, Jordan, why didn't you stand up?
> I have a bad dream about you!
> Don't go into Vevchani3,
> because the village is besieged.
>
> - Don't be scared, Ordanica,
> give me a sharp sword,
> and my Berdan rifle.
> Let me lead my troops
> to go there Ordanica
> and see who besieged the village.
>
> When Jordan saw, to his surprise,
> the entire village besieged,
> he gave his troops a sign by waiving his rifle
> and the asker4 cried to God.
