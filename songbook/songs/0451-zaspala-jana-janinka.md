## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Луди' (madmen) and 'аџамии' are uncertain dialect or archaic words; 'пукнала, треснала' as a curse-like outburst, 'зелен да биде как' него' and 'низ него да се провира' are my reading.
