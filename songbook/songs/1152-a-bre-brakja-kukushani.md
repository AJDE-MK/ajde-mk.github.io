## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Song about the death of Goce Delchev (1903, Banica); no named author, so translated. The marker line '(Друга верзија)' is copied unchanged. Kukush is the Macedonian name of Kilkis.
