## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Where are you going, Kita,
> barefooted to harvesting?
> Kita hey, my first love,
> Kita hey, my first heartbreak.
>
> Barefooted to harvesting, Kita,
> with a scythe on your shoulder.
> Kita hey, my first love,
> Kita hey, my first heartbreak.
>
> With a scythe on your shoulder, Kita,
> with a child in your hands.
> Kita hey, my first love,
> Kita hey, my first heartbreak.
>
> Leave the scythe, Kita,
> and come to me.
> Kita hey, my first love,
> Kita hey, my first heartbreak.
