## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I have a modern wife,
> my friends,
> a citizen from a good house,
> and of noble descent.
> If you see her, you will be surprised,
> you will be scared, oh,
> you will run far away for three days
> just to avoid seeing her.
>
> She combs her hair,
> like it is plucked,
> like an irritated chicken
> with a hundred chicks.
> If you see her, you will be surprised,
> you will be scared, oh,
> you will run far away for three days
> just to avoid seeing her.
>
> She painted her mouth
> with a kilogram of lipstick,
> and on her eyes she poured
> some grey ink.
> If you see her, you will be surprised,
> you will be scared, oh,
> you will run far away for three days
> just to avoid seeing her.
>
> (Oh, this is a miracle never seen before!)
