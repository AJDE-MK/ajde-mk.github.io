## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. 'Меанџика да те успива' is my reading: a tavern woman lulls you to sleep; it could also mean the tavern itself. Ile and Ilija are forms of the same name.
