## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Гајлелија' is a guess (lovesick or pining). Speakers swap between Lena, Klime and Stavre in the first version, so the dialogue attribution is my reading. 'Понудица/понадица' read as a gift for a sick person; 'пo бре лудо младо' translated as 'you silly young one'.
