## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (lively wedding song, possibly modern); no author named. 'Нунко' is rendered 'godfather' (my reading; nunko is the wedding sponsor/godfather in Macedonian usage).

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| свадба | wedding |
| се спрема | is being prepared |
| свекор | father-in-law (groom's father) |

| гајле | worry, care (Turkish loan) |
| абер | news (Turkish loan) |
