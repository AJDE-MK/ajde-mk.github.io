## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> A mother asks her son:
> - "Where have you been, son, out in the night?
> All night long your mother hasn't slept,
> waiting for you to return."
>
> A mother asks her son:
> - "Where have you been, son, out in the night?
> You are hiding something, you are full of sorrow,
> you do not open your heart."
>
> - "Oh, Mother, you do not know,
> my heart is broken.
> The girl I fell in love with
> has been promised to another."
>
> "Oh, Mother, you do not know,
> my heart is broken.
> Her mother will not give her to me;
> I am too poor for her."
>
> - "Do not say that, my son, they do not know,
> to me, you are the richest of all.
> Your heart is like a mountain,
> your soul is like the gateway to a church.
> Whoever enters within
> will find love waiting there.
>
> Oh, my dear son!"
