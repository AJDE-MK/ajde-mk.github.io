# Не плачи мило либе, не жали (Параходот)

*Ne plači milo libe, ne žali (Parahodot)* · *Don’t cry, my love, don’t be sad (The steam boat)*

Pesna.org #427: <https://pesna.org/song/427>

## Background

The goodbye at the dock (also known as *The Steamer*). The steamship has arrived; give me your hand, don't cry, I will come back. Her eyes are wet but her mouth is smiling. Take a white handkerchief and wipe your dark eyes.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Jutta Malzbender)*

> My steam boat is coming in,
> give me your hand to say good-bye.
> Don’t cry, my love, don’t be sad,
> I shall come back again.
>
> Your eyes full of tears,
> although your lips are smiling.
> Don’t cry, my love, don’t be sad,
> I shall come back again.
>
> Take a white handkerchief,
> wipe your black eyes.
> Don’t cry, my love, don’t be sad,
> I shall come back again.
