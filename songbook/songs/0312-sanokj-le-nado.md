## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Гајтан веѓи' (eyebrows like a braided cord) is my reading of a poetic image. 'Пуста' is rendered as 'wretched'.
