## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Down there in Krivolak, Crveni Bregovi,
> a hero fell for freedom, for Macedonia.
>
> With fascist police he fought
> always telling them:
>
> - "Hey you murderers of the people, get out of here,
> Macedonia is rising to drive you out.
>
> I am an honest Macedonian, with Macedonian blood,
> I am not falling in your hands, I'll rather kill myself".
>
> Sleep in peace, our hero Nikola Petrov,
> you died for freedom, for Macedonia.
