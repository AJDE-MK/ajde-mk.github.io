## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Place names Кршисер and Гари are as written; I do not know them. 'кумити' is my reading as a plural of 'кум'. 'Дал бог добро' is a standard reply greeting. Line 'долап мусандра' is my reading.
