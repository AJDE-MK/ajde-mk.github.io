## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Sounds like a modern kafana (tavern) song; origin unclear, I cannot name an author. 'ликој' in 'ликој гледам' is my reading: 'a face/likeness'.
