## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> She was distributing, that white Rada,
> she was distributing, that white Rada, hey.
> To each girl, one young man,
> to white Rada, a black Arab, hey, hey!
>
> So she wondered what to do with him,
> so she wondered what to do with him, hey.
> So she bought him four kilos of soap,
> and she took him to Debar Spa, hey, hey!
>
> To Debar Spa, in the hot water
> she washed him for three days, she rubbed him for two days, hey.
> And she rubbed too much his right ear,
> his right ear, his left shoulder, hey, hey!
>
> So she mounted him on a white horse,
> so she mounted him on a white horse, hey.
> The horse is white, he is black,
> the horse is white, he is black, hey, hey!
