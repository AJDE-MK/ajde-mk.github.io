## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'си оставив' is a reflexive past ('I left'). The 'three parts' refer to the partition of Macedonia; Australia as the 'fourth' refers to emigration.
