## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Reads as a wedding blessing sung to the groom about the bride. 'Блага да ти биде' is my reading (may she be gentle/blessed to you).
