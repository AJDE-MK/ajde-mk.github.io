## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. Dialect and rare words: 'ока' is an old unit of weight (about 1.2 kg), 'драм' a small old unit; 'По тро да си клаваш' is my reading (roughly 'you need not put on make-up'), 'тнонка' is my reading of 'тенка' (slim).
