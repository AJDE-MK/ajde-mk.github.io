# Митре Влао, тешко ранет

*Mitre Vlao, teško ranet* · *Mitre Vlao, Badly Wounded*

Pesna.org #5: <https://pesna.org/song/5>

**Notes.** Во спомен на Митре Панџаров - Влаот, македонски војвода.

## Background

A death scene in a few lines. The IMRO fighter Mitre the Vlach, grievously wounded, asks his comrades for his rifle. He wants to fire it toward Vich mountain, across the heights and hollows, so that it says goodbye to the Kostur region and to all of Macedonia. A rifle used to whisper goodbye across a landscape.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'дулбија' is a rare word I am guessing; 'ја врла ... да распула' is my reading that he wants to cast his gaze over the mountains and valleys. Commemorates Mitre Panџаров the Vlach, as stated in the source note.
