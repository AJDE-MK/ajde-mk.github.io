## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'ран бел босилок' is my reading as 'early white basil'; the song ends with Mitra preferring death to changing her faith.
