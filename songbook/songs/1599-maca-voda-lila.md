## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. The last stanza is cheeky; my reading is that 'сакаш ич не питај' means 'if you love me, never ask' and the numb arm is a joke about their night together. 'Ја подај' is a dialect form of 'подај'.
