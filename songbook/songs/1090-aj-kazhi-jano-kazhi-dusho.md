## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, Ottoman-era conscription theme. The line starting 'Оти Јано' is my reading as 'Or ... to pay my debt?'.
