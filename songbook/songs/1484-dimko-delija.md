## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Dialect past forms in -ја (играја, пољуљаја). The refrain 'бај бири дипли билбил џанум' is a Turkish-flavoured refrain, my reading: roughly 'oh dear nightingale'. 'Стан' rendered 'camp' and 'perо рестато' are guesses. Origin unclear, folk.
