## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Last night I saw, my dear,
> white Maria,
> white Maria, dear,
> our neighbor.
> She has gone for water, for water,
> for a cold one.
>
> With her friends, my dear little one,
> friends, beauties.
> All are maidens, my dear,
> maidens above maidens.
>
> As soon as I saw her, my dear,
> my heart started burning for her.
> The most beautiful, my dear,
> among maidens, the most flashy.
>
> Her face, my dear,
> is pretty as a mirror.
> Her eyes, my dear,
> are like black cherries.
> Her body, my dear,
> are thin as a poplar.
>
> Go and ask her for me, my dear,
> white Maria, my neighbor.
> If they don't give her to me, my dear,
> I will die for her, my dear,
> I will die for her.
