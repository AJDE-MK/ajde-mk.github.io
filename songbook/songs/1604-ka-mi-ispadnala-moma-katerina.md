## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Јаска' is read as 'I would' (dialect, my reading). 'Да ти кршам таја рамна снага' is my reading as 'to hold/embrace your figure'; literally 'to bend/break'. Origin unclear, treated as traditional.
