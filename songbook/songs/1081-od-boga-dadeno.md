## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Enough with the dividing, enough with the suffering,
> when we are all fighting for the same thing.
> When, when will we be united,
> when, when, will we be one?
>
> All this time I'm thinking, all this time I am asking,
> I'm losing my mind, I'm burning out.
> When, when will we be united,
> when, when, will we be one?
>
> Blood is thicker than water, brothers,
> we are all Macedonian blood.
> When, when will we be united,
> when, when, will we be one?
