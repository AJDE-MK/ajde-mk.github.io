## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'кадуша' is a dialect word I am unsure about; 'capable girl' is my reading. The last stanza ('редум') is my reading: 'order/propriety', meaning Kata is unmatched in good conduct.
