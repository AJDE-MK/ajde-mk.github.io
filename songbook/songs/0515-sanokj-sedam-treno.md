## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> All night Treno, I sit, all night I think,
> all the girls Treno, I remember.
> When I remembered you,
> a glass of tears, Treno, I filled up.
>
> A glass of tears, Treno, I filled up
> my scarf, Treno I made wet.
> The scarf, I watered
> on my chests, Treno, I dried it up.
>
> All night, Treno, I sit, all night I drink,
> all the songs, Treno, I am singing.
> All the songs, I am singing,
> all the girls, Treno, I am remembering.
>
> When I think of you, Treno,
> my tambura, Treno, I am breaking.
