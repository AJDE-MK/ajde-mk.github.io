## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: John Pappas)*

> A small young girl
> did not have a mother.
>
> As she grew up
> she searched for her mother.
>
> - Tell me father, dear dad
> where is my mother?
>
> - Your mother, dear child
> is sleeping in the dark ground.

Also on Pesna.org: Turkish, Greek.
