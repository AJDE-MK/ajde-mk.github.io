## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Modern patriotic pop-style song; I cannot name an author, so origin unclear. 'Титов Велес' is the old name of Veles, and the names in the third stanza are first names of performers (my reading).
