## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Dialogue between a boy (Gjorgji) and a girl (Stojna) who refuses to elope because she has linen to bleach. The last two lines are my reading: the boy says to leave the chore to her mother.
