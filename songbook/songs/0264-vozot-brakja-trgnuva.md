# Возот браќа тргнува

*Vozot braḱa trgnuva* · *The Train, Brothers, Is Leaving*

Pesna.org #264: <https://pesna.org/song/264>

## Background

A song of the guest-worker era. The train leaves straight for Germany; old mothers stay behind to mind the small children. They go, they sing, they shed tears, and curse the first one who left. They go with their loved ones to earn money and build a house; friends will meet them there, the bachelors will sing and the wives will mourn.
