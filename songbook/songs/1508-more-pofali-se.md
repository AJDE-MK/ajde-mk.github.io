## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional song about the saying that the old woman who drives her goats out early gets caught by Baba Marta's cold snap. 'Превари' as 'outwit' and the reading of the last stanza are my reading.

**Words to learn**
