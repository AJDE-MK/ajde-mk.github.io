## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Sounds like a composed emigrant song, but I cannot name an author, so origin unclear. 'Бигор в' душава' is my reading of a figurative stone in the soul.
