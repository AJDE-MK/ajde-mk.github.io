## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; modern patriotic song, no author I can name. 'Гемиџии' refers to the Boatmen of Thessaloniki (my reading). 'Неврокопските' is Nevrokop, now Gotse Delchev in Bulgaria.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| алов | scarlet, bright red |
| бајрак | banner, flag |
| марама | kerchief, headscarf |
| навезеш | you embroider |
| развеам | I unfurl |
| камбани | bells |
| гемиџии | boatmen (the Thessaloniki bombers) |
| огништа | hearths, homes |
