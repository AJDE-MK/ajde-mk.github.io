## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, sounds like a modern song, no author I can name. 'Везден да е со тебе' (rendered 'may she be an embroidered one with you') is my reading and likely not accurate; the line may mean something like 'to be always with you'.
