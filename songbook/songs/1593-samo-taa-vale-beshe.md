## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; it sounds like a modern composed song but I cannot name an author. The first stanza is syntactically loose; my reading is a wish that whoever loves you can calm and follow another, while the singer spends his youth with 'me'.
