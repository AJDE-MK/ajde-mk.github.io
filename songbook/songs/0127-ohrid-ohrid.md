## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Emigrant's longing song for Ohrid; origin unclear, no author I can name. 'мила сека вечер' is my reading as 'dear sister, every evening' (it could also be 'dear one, every evening'); 'сон за мене останаа' is also my reading.
