## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Here I come from afar,
> to see you, sister.
> Raise your head from your bed,
> stand up straight.
> Here I come from afar,
> to see you, sister.
> In our white house,
> may joy and laughter return.
>
> Get up, get up, dear sister,
> God, God, give her strength
> to straighten her arms, her wings,
> to overcome this wild illness,
> to overcome my heavy sorrow,
> heavy sorrow, dear sister.
