## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> If I die or perish
> don't cry for me.
> Drink some red wine
> and break the glasses.
>
> Hey, my faithful friends,
> sing a song
> and remember me.
>
> If I die or perish
> don't call a priest,
> but come to my grave
> and start dancing.
>
> Hey, my faithful friends,
> sing a song
> and remember me.
>
> If I die or perish
> the memories will remain
> of the crazy young lad I was
> in the years of my youth.
>
> Hey, my faithful friends,
> sing a song
> and remember me.
