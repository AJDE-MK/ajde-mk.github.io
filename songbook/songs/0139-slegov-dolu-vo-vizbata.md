## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Чепчето' rendered as 'spigot' is my reading (a plug or tap on the barrel); it could also mean a small cap. Bawdy-humorous wedding style.

**Words to learn**
