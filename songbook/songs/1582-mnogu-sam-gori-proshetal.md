## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional haiduk-type song; 'биро' is my reading as 'bailiff/officer' (guess). 'вршник да гори' is my reading: probably 'to tend the fire' or burn something for the roast; uncertain.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| гори | forests (dialect plural of гора) |
| прошетал | (I have) strolled through |
| расплакал | (I have) made cry |
| наредба | order, decree |
| испече | be roasted |
| дрва | firewood |
| кавал | kaval (end-blown flute) |
| оро | round dance |
