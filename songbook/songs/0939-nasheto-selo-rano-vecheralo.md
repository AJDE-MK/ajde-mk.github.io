*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, origin unclear. Verb forms like 'избегав' and 'успало' shift person oddly; I read 'избегав' as 'ran away' (my reading), and the speaker in line 6 is Stojanica.
