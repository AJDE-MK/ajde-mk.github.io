## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin noted as Demir Hisar region. The last stanza's 'малике (маликера)' I read as a Mannlicher rifle, and the last two lines are my reading (the hero threatens the eagle with the rifle); 'испија' read as 'drink out'.
