## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Regretful love song about a couple who never married; origin unclear, I cannot name an author. 'ветивне' and 'зедојме' in the repeat are variant spellings of the same verbs.
