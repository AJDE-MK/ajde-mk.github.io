## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'чучук Митра' I read as 'little Mitra' (чучук from Turkish küçük, small), a guess. 'Дуње' as a village name in Mariovo is my reading; 'вапца' is dialect for dye.
