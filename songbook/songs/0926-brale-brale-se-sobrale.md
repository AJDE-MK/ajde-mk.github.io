## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding, no author known (origin unclear). 'ќучук' is a Turkish loan for 'little/dog'; I render it 'dog-faced' as my guess, it may be a nickname. 'Брале' in the title is a mistranscription-like variant of 'збрале'.
