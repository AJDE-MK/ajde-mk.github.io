## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Стои диван' means to stand in respectful attendance (my reading, the phrase is guesswork). The speaker changes between stanzas; the third stanza's 'ојде' is dialect for 'went'.
