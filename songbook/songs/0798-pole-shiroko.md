## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Wide field,
> who will walk over you,
> now that Delchev vojvoda is gone?
>
> Pirin Mountain,
> who will lead his platoon over you,
> now that Delchev vojvoda is gone?
>
> Young girls,
> who will protect you from the devil,
> now that Delchev vojvoda is gone?
