## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. The marker '(Друга верзија)' differs from the standard marker and is copied unchanged. 'Да би не станал' and 'преди' (probably 'сестро') are my reading; 'сардиса' is a dialect guess.
