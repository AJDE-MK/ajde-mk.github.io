## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; it reads like a modern composed song but I cannot name an author. 'Пророси' as 'shed' and the grammar of line 1 ('земјо ми те надвило') are my reading; 'Лешочки' refers to the Lesok monastery near Tetovo.
