*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding, no author known (origin unclear). 'каурка' is a dialect word I am unsure of; my reading is a peasant woman (possibly from 'gavur', non-Muslim). 'налани' read as wooden clogs.
