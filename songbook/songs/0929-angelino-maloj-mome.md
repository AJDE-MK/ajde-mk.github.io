## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'аџамија' and 'малој' are Turkish-influenced/dialect forms I am fairly but not fully sure of; 'посвшено' is my reading of 'посвршено' (engaged).
