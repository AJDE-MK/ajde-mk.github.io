## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'сè одбор јунаци' rendered 'all chosen heroes' is my reading. The song is about the Mariovo voivode Gjorgji Sugarev; whether it is old folklore or a later composition I cannot tell, so I treated it as traditional.
