## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, sounds like a newer folk-style song, but I cannot name an author. 'Пиле' means bird/chick and is used here as an address to the falcon.

**Words to learn**
