## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, no author known. Several lines are in a dialect with unusual forms (ју, треста, терезија); lines 4, 7 and 9 are my reading and partly guessed, in particular 'терезија' as a balance or measuring scale and 'да не ѕида' as 'so he builds'.
