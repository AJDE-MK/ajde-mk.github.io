# Кондисал Ајрадин паша

*Kondisal Ajradin paša* · *Hayreddin Pasha settled*

Pesna.org #562: <https://pesna.org/song/562>

**Notes.** Кондиса значи да отседне на едно место, да се смести. Стамбол е архаично име за Истанбул. Ќутук е пењушка, цепаница, некакво парче дрво. Упија е име на чешма во Галичник. Ќеаи се сопственици на овци. Јагне сугаре значи јагне кое е родено подоцна. Сељам (селам) значи поздрав.

## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Hayreddin Pasha* settled, aman*,
> settled on the opposite side of Galichnik.
> And pointed strong cannons, aman,
> to burn down Galichnik.
> Galichnik, a village so huge, aman,
> it was written on a log in Stambol*.
>
> He took out his spyglass, aman,
> to check at the fountain Upija*.
> At the fountain there were two maidens, aman,
> two maidens and one bride.
> The maidens were bleaching cloth, aman,
> the bride was carrying water.
>
> Todor Poposki got out, aman,
> accompanied with sheep owners.
> Bringing a lamb that was born late, aman,
> to pay his respects with salaam* to Pasha.
> To pay his respects with salaam to Pasha,
> so he won't burn down Galichnik.
>
> Great sorrow fell on Pasha's heart, aman,
> he picked up his strong cannons.
> He picked up his strong cannons, aman,
> didn't burn down Galichnik.
> Galichnik - a village so huge, aman,
> it was written on a log in Stambol.
