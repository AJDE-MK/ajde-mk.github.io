## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Oh young girl, young girl,
> you apple from Tetovo.
> Show me your white face
> I want to see it.
>
> Hey boy, you fool,
> beautiful foreigner.
> A white simitche1 in the bakery,
> haven't you seen it?
>
> Oh young girl, young girl
> you apple from Tetovo.
> Show me your black eyes,
> I want to see them.
>
> Hey boy, you fool,
> beautiful foreigner.
> In the bazaar, the black eyebrows
> haven't you seen them?
>
> Oh young girl, young girl
> you apple from Tetovo.
> Show me your thin body,
> I want to see it.
>
> Hey boy, you fool,
> beautiful foreigner.
> There is a poplar tree in the garden
> haven't you seen it?
