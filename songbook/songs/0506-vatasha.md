## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear: reads as a 20th-century anti-fascist song but I cannot name an author. 'носат роса' (line 3) is my reading as 'carried, dewy'; 'Мокришко' is presumably a place name.
