## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Stojna, black-eyed girl,
> this crazy lad is consumed by love for you.
> - If he is consumed, let him be.
>
> Last night another one was consumed.
> Stojna cast a spell on him,
> as many stars there are in the skies...
>
> (Hey, you musician, come on musician, spin that instrument)
>
> ... That many cities may he travel
> and not find a girl like Stojna.
>
> Neither a girl, nor a bride.
> Neither a widow,
> nor a cheerful damsel.
