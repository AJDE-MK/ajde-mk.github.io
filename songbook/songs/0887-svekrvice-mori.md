## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. The nonsense-looking refrain 'Иљче море џан, медор мол сенга, ајде елем дотурма' is my reading only and seems to be Turkish-flavoured filler, so those words are left mostly untranslated; 'чифтели чапуљ' (çiftetelli dancer, scoundrel) and 'пуш' (worthless) are also guesses.
