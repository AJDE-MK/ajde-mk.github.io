## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, sounds like an urban merchant song (cloth seller). 'Маршин' seems a rhyming nonsense echo of 'аршин'. The '(Друга верзија)' marker line is copied unchanged.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| аршин | arshin, an old cloth-measuring length (about 70 cm) |
| либе | sweetheart, love |
| младост | youth |
| верувај ми | believe me |
| зборој | words (dialect form of зборови) |
| думи | words |
| се заљубив | I fell in love |
| до века | forever |
