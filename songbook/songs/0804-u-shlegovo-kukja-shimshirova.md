# У Шлегово куќа шимширова

*U Šlegovo kuḱa šimširova* · *In Shlegovo a boxwood house*

Pesna.org #804: <https://pesna.org/song/804>

**Notes.** Зборот „мерџан“ е турцизам и означува „корал“. Шимшир е вид растение (лат. Buxus sempervirens).

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, no author known. 'Шлегово' is a place name I take as a village; 'бре дилбере' is an address to a beloved, my reading.
