## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Армасани' in the plural form is slightly odd here; my reading is 'betrothed'. 'Рудо' is an old poetic word, translated 'ruddy'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| армасани | engaged, betrothed |
| невидени | unseen, not having seen each other |
| чешма | fountain, spring tap |
| калап сапун | a bar of soap |
| бело лице | white face (fair complexion) |
| рудо грло | ruddy/golden-red throat |
