## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'анама' is my reading ('my lady', from Turkish hanim); I am not sure of it. 'сека недела Велигден' is rendered 'every Sunday is Easter' (недела = Sunday or week).
