## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Hey Dushanka from Bitushe,
> has your bachelor come yet?
>
> - He came, he came, may he didn't come,
> he is preparing the horse to go away again.
>
> I tell him - take me with you,
> he tells me - I won't take you.
>
> He tells me - I won't take you,
> my company is bad,
> they'll kill me and
> they'll take you 'cause you are young.
