# Река газит малој моме

*Reka gazit maloj mome* · *A young girl wades through the river*

Pesna.org #147: <https://pesna.org/song/147>

**Notes.** 1Променица е нова облека, а најверојатно во песната се мисли на свадбена облека.

2Одменица е термин кој се користи кога снаата ја заменува или одменува свекрвата во работата, односно и помага во сите домашни работи.

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> A young girl wades through the river,
> Her legs are white,
> In her hands, she carries a basket
> Full of violets.
>
> A girl and a boy meet
> In a narrow alley.
> The young girl speaks
> to the young lad:
>
> - “Are you going to the army, crazy one,
> Or are you coming back from the army?”
> - “Neither am I going to the army, crazy one,
> Nor am I coming back from the army,
> I was just at the horse market
> To buy a horse.”
>
> Give me your hand, foolish young one
> Lift me onto the horse.
> Lift me onto the horse, foolish young one,
> And take me home.
> Take me home, foolish one,
> To a colorful room.
> To a colorful room, foolish one,
> To a soft bed.
> Father promised a new dress
> Mother - a helping hand.
