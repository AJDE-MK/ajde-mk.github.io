## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (diaspora-themed). Lerin = Florina, Kostur = Kastoria; Banitsa, Armenochori and Nevoleni are villages in Aegean Macedonia. Stanza 3 is partly my reading ('Ора коњчето' perhaps 'ора' = oh).
