## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. The last stanza is my reading: she says that if he is unmarried she will return in a year with a young lad and a baby boy, meaning she will marry him.
