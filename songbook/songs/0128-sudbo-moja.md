## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Sevdah-style lament, treated as traditional though I am not certain. 'Субдо' in stanza 3 is a typo for 'судбо'. 'Нема кој да ме превира' is my reading: 'no one to look after / console me'.
