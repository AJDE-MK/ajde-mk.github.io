## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Нарачал Стојан, порачал, како да знае мајка му' is my reading: he sent word so his mother would know, asking her to send a picture of Kalina. 'во зандани' kept as 'in a dungeon'.
