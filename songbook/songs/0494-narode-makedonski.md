# Народе Македонски

*Narode Makedonski* · *Macedonian people*

Pesna.org #494: <https://pesna.org/song/494>

## Background

An agitation song from before the Balkan Wars, calling the people to wake up and know themselves. Qwen says it refers to a massacre in the village of Nagoričani in 1902 and to real people of the time; none of these details have been checked.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft; names and dates unchecked.*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Macedonian people,
> what are your ideals?
> Enough, wake up,
> come to your senses, don't sleep.
>
> You don't know who and what you are,
> others know about you.
> It's a big shame for you
> not to know yourself.
>
> They beheaded women and children
> in the village of Zagorichani,
> but before the Turkish army
> you dropped your weapons.
>
> They have thin tassels
> and Berdan rifles,
> their clothes are torn out
> and their bullet cases empty.
>
> Where is Karaivanov
> and his company?
> Melas, Karavangelis,
> he is in Bitola, in prison.
