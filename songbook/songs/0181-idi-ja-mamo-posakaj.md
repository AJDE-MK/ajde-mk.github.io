## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, southwestern/Aegean-type dialect (-т endings like 'имат', 'давет'). 'ако је неја' and 'плевната ќе му' are my reading; 'му' (his) left out of the English as unclear.
