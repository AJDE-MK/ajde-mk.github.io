## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. The refrain lines (mother, 'оф леле') are as written; the last stanza and the meaning of 'момче не чини' (my reading: the lad is no good for her) are guessed. 'вој дилбер', 'воа аро' are dialect, my reading.
