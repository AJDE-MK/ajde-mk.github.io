## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Two versions of a courting song; 'јас кај тебе Ристе на душа' in version one is my reading (roughly 'with you, at heart'). Origin unclear, treated as traditional.
