## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear, sounds like a modern wedding song. 'Аирлија' is a Turkish-origin blessing word ('hayırlı'); meaning is my reading. 'Какo што се чаши' I render as 'as there are glasses', my reading.
