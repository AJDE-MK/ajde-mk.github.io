# Каул се фатиле момче и девојче

*Kaul se fatile momče i devojče* · *A Boy and a Girl Made a Bet*

Pesna.org #1239: <https://pesna.org/song/1239>

**Notes.** „Каул“ значи облог, „фурлини“ се елементи од накит, а „боравење“ значи мешање. :-)

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'таксала' I read as 'ordered/arranged for herself'; the last line 'стани да не станиш' is idiomatic, my reading is a playful curse.
