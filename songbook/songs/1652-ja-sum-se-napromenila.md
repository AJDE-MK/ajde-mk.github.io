## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I'm all dressed up,
> but there's no one to look at me.
>
> My love went to be a soldier,
> and made his corporal angry.
>
> He made his corporal angry,
> the captain won't let him go.
>
> The captain won't let him come
> and take me for a walk.
>
> To come and take me for a walk
> through those white blackberries.
>
> Through those white blackberries,
> to that royal fountain.
>
> To that royal fountain,
> to eat bread and cheese.
>
> To eat bread and cheese,
> to drink cold water.
