## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Epic song about Marko Kraljevic. Several lines are my reading: 'шчо/што' in the girl's speech, 'кај му се кули' as 'where are his towers', 'минци/минцови' as coins, 'тога стегна' as 'tightened up/tightened the horse', and the final line 'заплатила десница' as 'has paid'. The original text contains a few garbled dialect forms.
