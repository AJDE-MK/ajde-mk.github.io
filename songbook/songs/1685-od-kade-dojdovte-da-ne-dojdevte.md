# Од каде дојдовте, да не дојдевте

*Od kade dojdovte, da ne dojdevte* · *Where did you come from, you shouldn't have come*

Pesna.org #1685: <https://pesna.org/song/1685>

**Notes.** Песната е реакција на терористичките напади врз Македонија во 2001 година, кои резултираа со убиства на околу 70 бранители (војници и полицајци), но и цивили.

## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Where did you come from, you shouldn't have come!
> What harm did we do to you, that you should devour us?
> In this beautiful land, we gave you everything;
> upon our ancestral hearths, you built your homes.
>
> And that is not all you have taken from us;
> you have cloaked our mothers, brothers, and sisters in mourning.
> You have brought tears to all of Bitola,
> you have cast a pall of sorrow over Macedonia.
>
> Enemies, beasts—may you be utterly destroyed!
> The time has come, the hour is here to settle the score with you.
> For years you have been preparing, taking up arms,
> inflicting evil upon this peaceful, ancient people.
>
> You know full well what you have done;
> you have all too quickly forgotten the kindness we showed you.
> With guns in hand—may you be cursed!—you marched against us,
> cloaking our mothers, brothers, and sisters in mourning.
>
> You know full well what you have done;
> you have defiled this sacred native land.
> If need be, we shall give our lives, every last one of us;
> Macedonia, beloved land, we shall defend you.
>
> And I say with courage: we shall not yield, we shall not surrender;
> we will defend this beautiful land.
> And the good Lord sees all; He will repay you in full
> for the lives you took from my comrades.
>
> And that is not all you have taken from us;
> you have cloaked our mothers, brothers, and sisters in mourning.
> You have brought tears to all of Bitola,
> you have cast a pall of sorrow over Macedonia.
>
> Enemies, beasts—may you be utterly destroyed!
> The time has come, the hour is here to settle the score with you.
> For years you have been preparing, taking up arms,
> inflicting evil upon this peaceful, ancient people.
>
> You know full well what you have done;
> you so quickly forgot the kindness we once showed you.
> With guns—curse you!—you turned against us,
> plunging our mothers, brothers, and sisters into mourning.
>
> You know full well what you have done;
> you have defiled this sacred, native land.
> If need be, we shall give our lives, every last one of us—
> Macedonia, dear land, we will defend you!
>
> Oh, my heart aches, dear land,
> a heavy sorrow has coiled within my soul;
> vile enemies trample upon you once again,
> once again they set your villages ablaze,
> once again, dear land, they seek to tear you apart—
> oh, may they be cursed, forever cursed!
>
> But trust, O Mother, in your children,
> who do not hesitate to give their lives so that you may remain whole.
> Glory and honor to all those who gave their lives—
> for you, for you, dear Macedonia!
