## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Дарко Стефановски)*

> And the time will come and the day, oh yes,
> you are gonna cry, you'll wither for me.
> But, then it will be too late
> I will be in my cold grave.
>
> You keep thinking 'till it's still early,
> because I love you, you should love me too.
> Don’t let me die young,
> you developed dreams in despair.
>
> Don’t make too much suffering for me,
> much suffering, sighs, tears.
> Hug me and tell me that you love me
> and put an end to my suffering.
>
> You’ll look for others like me,
> you’ll not find, remember that.
> After death for you will happen
> bitterest and most difficult days.
