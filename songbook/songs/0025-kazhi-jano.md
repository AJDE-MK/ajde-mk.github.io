## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Изим даваш' is unclear to me; 'give way' is my reading. The sense seems to be that her unmarried state holds her siblings back from marrying (custom of marrying in order).
