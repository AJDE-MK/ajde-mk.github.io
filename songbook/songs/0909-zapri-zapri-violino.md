## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> An old violin
> this night quietly plays,
> a stream of pain in my heart,
> how do I calm it down?
>
> Stop, stop violin,
> don't play, don't play for me,
> my heart, violin,
> yearns for her.
>
> I hear this song
> which tells a story about us
> and our love
> until the reddish dawn.
>
> Stop, stop violin,
> don't play, don't play for me,
> my heart, violin,
> yearns for her.
