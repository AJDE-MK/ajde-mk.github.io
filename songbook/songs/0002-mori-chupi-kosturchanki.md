# Мори чупи костурчанки

*Mori čupi kosturčanki* · *Hey, girls from Kostur*

Pesna.org #2: <https://pesna.org/song/2>

## Background

A flirting dance chant from the Kostur area (Kastoriá in Greece). *Чупи* is the local word for unmarried girls, and the word itself shows where the song comes from. Widen the oro ring so we can see the dresses; whoever has the finest dress, I will become her devoted admirer.

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Hey, girls from Kostur,
> spread the oro a little wider,
> let us see your dresses,
> whose dress has marks on it,
> and I will fall in love,
> in that girl's dress.

Also on Pesna.org: Dutch, German, Greek.
