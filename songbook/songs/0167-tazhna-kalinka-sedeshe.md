## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Kalinka was sitting sad,
> talking to herself:
> - "Dear God, you wonderful,
> I was also a girl".
>
> I was also a girl
> on this white deceiving world.
> The bright Sun was warming me,
> from high, from far away.
>
> I had my faithful love
> his mother didn't want me.
> His mother didn't want me
> my father didn't give me.
>
> So I took white canisters,
> white canisters in my hands.
> I went to Ivan's
> where I saw a big crowd.
>
> When Ivan saw me
> he took a sharp knife.
> He took a sharp knife
> and stabbed himself in the heart.
>
> I left my white canisters,
> my white canisters on the ground.
> I bent over Ivan
> I saw, I saw, blood I saw.
>
> So I took the sharp knife,
> the sharp knife in my hands.
> I stabbed it in my heart
> and fell down dead next to Ivan.
>
> She fell down dead, speaking:
> - "May the white world understand,
> two hearts that love each other
> will embrace each other even if they are dead".
