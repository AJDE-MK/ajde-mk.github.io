## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Speaker roles are unclear; my reading is that a daughter asks to be given to the young man and the mother refuses. 'Женето' and the lines about the fourth child 'on his heart' are my reading.
