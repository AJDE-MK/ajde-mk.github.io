# Отвори ми кузум Лено

*Otvori mi kuzum Leno* · *Open to me, my darling Lena*

Pesna.org #1133: <https://pesna.org/song/1133>

**Notes.** „Кузум“ доаѓа од турскиот збор „kuzu“ со превод: мила, драга или јагне.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Dialect first-person forms in -у (можу, стану, отвору). Line 'Налегна ми русе косе' is my reading (her hair has fallen over her, as she lies beside her mother).
