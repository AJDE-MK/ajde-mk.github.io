# Artists to search for

Names collected from Qwen's draft and your songbook. I have not checked each claim.

| Artist | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Наум Петревски (Насе) | Qwen calls this "the voice" of the repertoire. |
| Александар Сариевски | Singer of many classics; recorded *Зајди, зајди* (your songbook) and, per Qwen, the standard recording of the rabbit's wedding. |
| Васка Илиева | Her name is in your songbook's search links for several songs. |
| Никола Бадев | Named in your songbook's search for *Билјана платно белеше*. |
| Тоше Проески | Recorded *Зајди, зајди* (your songbook). |
| Влатко Стефановски | Guitarist; per Qwen, he and Андријана Јаневска recorded a jazzy version of *Елено ќерко*. |
| Ансамбл ТАНЕЦ | The national ensemble; per Qwen, dance versions of many songs. |
| Jonče Hristovski | Composer of several songs in the book (see *Македонско девојче*, #432). |

Qwen also listed other artists and ensembles. I left out the entries I could not check, and I corrected one error: Qwen's list put Kuzman Šapkarev among chalgija ensembles, but he was a 19th-century collector of folklore, not a musician.
