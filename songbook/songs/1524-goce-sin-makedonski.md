## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; a modern-sounding tribute to Gotse Delchev with no author I can name. Kukush is today's Kilkis in Greece.
