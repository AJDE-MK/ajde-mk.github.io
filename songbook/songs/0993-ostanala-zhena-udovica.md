## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, no author known. 'Потуреша' means a woman from the Potur (Muslim Slavic) community; 'ми' in several lines is a dialect filler. 'Калеши јаганца' I read as 'speckled lamb', my reading.
