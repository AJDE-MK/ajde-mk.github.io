## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Шума ќе берам' is my reading: 'gather forest' most likely means gathering mulberry leaves for silkworms. 'саи' is a guessed garment word.

**Words to learn**

| буби | silkworms |

| точа | I spin/wind |
