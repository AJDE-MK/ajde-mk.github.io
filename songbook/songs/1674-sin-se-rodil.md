## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Each of us longs to welcome such joy,
> whether a boy or a girl is born.
> And from our love, new life has sprung,
> my love, my wife, has given birth to my son today.
>
> Oh, the life I live,
> my heart is full of joy, I’ll raise a glass.
> Let’s toast, let’s sing,
> a son is born today, let’s celebrate, godfather.
>
> I am happy today, tears of joy are flowing,
> a baby boy is born to carry on our lineage.
> Prepare a name, godfather, to christen the boy,
> for grandma and grandpa are waiting to hear it.
>
> Oh, the life I live,
> my heart is full of joy, I’ll raise a glass.
> Let’s toast, let’s sing,
> a son is born today, let’s celebrate, godfather.
