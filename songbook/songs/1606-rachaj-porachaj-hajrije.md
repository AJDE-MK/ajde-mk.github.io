## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional (Bitola area). 'Хајрије' is a woman's name. 'црвилце' as lip colour is my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| порачај | order, send word what you want |
| лудо | dear, lad (endearment) |
| белилце | face powder (lit. little white) |
| црвилце | red colour, lip rouge |
| усте | mouth |
| аман | oh (plea, from Turkish) |
| се земиме | we will marry |
| Битола | Bitola (city) |
