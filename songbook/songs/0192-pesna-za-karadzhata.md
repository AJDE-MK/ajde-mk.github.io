## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> A cloud is swirling, the eagles are screeching,
> mountains are singing about the Karadzha.
> Sing to me, sing Kozhuf Mountain
> the old song about the Karadzha.
>
> - Tell me, tell me Karadzha
> how far does Macedonia stretch?
> - Up over there, Kumanovo Plain,
> Ohrid, Bitola, all the way to Pirin.
>
> Splinters are torn, needles are made,
> inserted below the nails of Macedonians,
> in the fire that's burning, iron is melted
> and put in the armpits of Macedonians.
>
> - Tell me, tell me Karadzha
> how far does Macedonia stretch?
> - Down over there, Prilep, Kavadarci,
> Negotino, Dojran, all the way to Solun.
>
> May all Europe know
> how far does Macedonia stretch:
> up over there, Tetovo Plain,
> Gostivar, Struga, all the way to Kichevo.
>
> Is there anyone else who still does not know
> where is the land of Macedonia?
