## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; it honours Jane Sandanski (a historical revolutionary) but no author is known to me. Forms like 'в'' and 'с'' are shortened prepositions (во, со); 'аа' in line 16 is my reading of 'так/ah'.
