# Ој Месечино, новино

*Oj Mesečino, novino* · *Oh Moon, new moon*

Pesna.org #75: <https://pesna.org/song/75>

## Background

An image-song about time passing over one night of love. A young man asks the new moon to shine bright tonight so he can go to white Jane. On the way there the cherries are in blossom, the wheat is in the ear and the springs are running; on the way back everything is picked, harvested and dried up.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*
