## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> I don't have dear, I don't have darling
> and I don't even want to know,
> like a boat without a steering wheel,
> I'll stray accross the world.
>
> I will wander around the lakes,
> without hope, without love,
> until I find
> my cold grave today.
>
> Accross the fields, accross the forests,
> I'll sleep wherever I can,
> I will crawl through the grass
> and flowers in the meadows.
>
> I will climb to Venus,
> the brightest star,
> and I will tell her
> about my sufferings through the world.
>
> I will get on a steam boat
> and I will shout with a loud voice:
> - "Goodbye dear, goodbye darling,
> I will go far away".
