## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-style song about the revolutionary Goce Delchev; origin unclear. 'трае, не збори' is my reading: 'keeps silent'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| гора | forest |
| врвеше | was walking |
| збореше | was speaking |
| војвода | voivode, leader of a band |
| трае | stays, endures (here: stays silent) |
| секира | axe |
| стада | herds |
| газеле | gazelles (here likely wild game, my reading) |
