## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Кирајџија' rendered 'hired-carriage boy' is my reading (a carter for hire). 'Ручек' and 'ручок' are variant spellings of the same word.

**Words to learn**
