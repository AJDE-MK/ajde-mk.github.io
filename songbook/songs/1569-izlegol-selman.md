*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'анами' is my reading of Turkish hanım (ladies, plural), not certain. 'црвена' in the last line means red or rosy-cheeked, my reading.
