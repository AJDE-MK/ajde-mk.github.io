## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Young maiden, little young maiden,
> look out the window
> so that I can see your face.
>
> So that I can see your face,
> little maiden,
> to check if your face
> suits me.
>
> To draw it in a picture
> little maiden,
> to place it in a book
> to place it in a book.
>
> To place it in a book,
> little maiden,
> when I join the army
> when I join the army.
>
> Whenever I think about you,
> little maiden,
> I'll just open the book,
> and my longing of you will pass.
