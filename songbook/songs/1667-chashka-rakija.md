## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> How can I come home
> so that they can see me
> when my eyes see,
> and my soul seeks
> a glass of rakija,
> and some red wine?
> My eyes immediately light up
> when I see someone else's.
>
> Oh, wow, wow,
> let it be like this,
> nothing matters, people,
> what will happen tomorrow.
> Oh, wow, wow,
> youth flies by quickly,
> nothing matters, people,
> what awaits us tomorrow.
