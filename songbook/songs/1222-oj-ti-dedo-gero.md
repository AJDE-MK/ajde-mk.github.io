## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional village song from Ljubojno (Prespa). 'Бура Ристо, бура' may be a name or address; I rendered it as 'Storm, Risto, storm' as my reading.
