## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Old regional (Kostur area) song about a real figure; the name appears as Papatrajkov and Poptrajkov in the source. 'деспот' is my reading as 'bishop'; 'Лисец' is a mountain name.
