## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (war ballad, no author I can name). 'т'гата' is probably 'тагата' (grief) and 'з'шчо' is 'зашто' (because); Krivolak is a place associated with a WWI battle (my reading). 'ронеја' I read as 'fell, crumbled'.
