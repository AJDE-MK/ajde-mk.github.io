## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Humorous complaint about a sickly wife. 'злото ме нападна' I read as 'trouble attacked me'; 'на рака лежало' as she has been lying on his arm, my reading.
