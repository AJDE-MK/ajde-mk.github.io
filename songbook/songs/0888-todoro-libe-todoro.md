## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. The speaker roles in the last stanza are unclear (it says 'your mother' but Todora is the one being addressed), so that part is my reading; 'изгоро' is an affectionate form from 'изгорува'.
