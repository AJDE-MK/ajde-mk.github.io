## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Oh, if only you were mine,
> the things I’d do to you,
> how I’d caress
> that slender, shapely form.
>
> Driving me wild
> and bringing such delight,
> until dawn breaks, my darling,
> lost in passion's sway.
>
> Oh, if only you were mine,
> I’d give you everything,
> just to caress
> those snowy-white breasts.
>
> Driving me wild
> and bringing such delight,
> until dawn breaks, my darling,
> lost in passion's sway.
>
> Oh, if only you were mine,
> I’d go mad with desire,
> longing to kiss
> those sweet, sweet lips.
>
> Driving me wild
> and bringing such delight,
> until dawn breaks, my darling,
> lost in passion's sway.
