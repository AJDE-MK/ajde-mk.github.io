## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> I strolled early in the morning, early in the morning,
> I saw a beautiful maiden, beautiful maiden,
> where she swept her court.
>
> A lad spoke to the beauty, he said:
> - Hey girl, young girl, you young girl,
> do not sweep the porch.
>
> Instead open the doors, open the doors,
> instead open the doors, open the doors,
> ‘cause a tired young lad is coming.
>
> - Hey, you young lad, you young lad,
> if a tired young lad is coming, a tired lad,
> I’ll have nothing to do with him.
>
> How beautiful and gorgeous she was,
> with a fine hat over her eyes,
> with the gold coin on her forehead,
> with fine pearls around her neck.
