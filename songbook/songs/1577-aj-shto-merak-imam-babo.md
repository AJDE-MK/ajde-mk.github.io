# Ај што мерак имам бабо

*Aj što merak imam babo* · *Oh, what a wish I have, father*

Pesna.org #1577: <https://pesna.org/song/1577>

**Notes.** Раклиш и Ораовица се села во општина Радовиш.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Local folk song from the Radovish area. 'раклишкото џаде' is my reading (the Raklish road); the town on the right is probably Radovish (my guess).
