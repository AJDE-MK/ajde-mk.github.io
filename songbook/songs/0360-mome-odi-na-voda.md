## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Изгора' (rendered 'izgora') is unclear to me, probably a rhyming echo of 'збора'; my reading. 'Тук'' is a dialect form of 'туку' (instead, but).
