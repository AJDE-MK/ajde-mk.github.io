## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Солунско' read as the Salonica front (probably WWI), my reading. 'сум добила' is a dialect form of 'имам добиено' (I have received).
