## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Sma, sma, sma
> I married a real lady,
> ugh, she is right for me:
> thin as a watermelon,
> quiet as a mill.
>
> As soon as she touches the door,
> the doorknob is gone,
> and if I shrug,
> throws trees and stones at me.
>
> Dzma, dzma, dzm, dzma, dzma, dzm,
> dzma, dzma, dzm, dzm, dzm.
>
> She loves the song very much,
> sings like a mad magpie,
> once she gets excited,
> she dreams of a fairy tale prince.
>
> Dzma, dzma, dzm, dzma, dzma, dzm,
> dzma, dzma, dzm, dzm, dzm.
>
> He married a real lady,
> ugh, she is right for him
> who does not want such a prize,
> if you want her, take her.
>
> Dzma, dzma, dzm, dzma, dzma, dzm,
> dzma, dzma, dzm, dzm, dzm.
