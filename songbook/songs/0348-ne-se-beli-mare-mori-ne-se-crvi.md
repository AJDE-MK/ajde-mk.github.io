## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Don’t powder your face, Mare, don’t put on rouge,
> Don’t, Mare, don’t dress up so much.
>
> The bachelors laugh at me, Mare,
> The bachelors, Mare, the craftsmen.
>
> Whom have you looked at, Mare,
> You’ve given everyone a sickness.
>
> To some for two days, Mare, to some for three days,
> But to me, Mare, for three years.
>
> You’ve made me, Mare, a dry tree,
> A dry tree, Mare, a maple tree.
>
> Take kindling, Mare, and light me up,
> Set me on fire, Mare, and burn me.
