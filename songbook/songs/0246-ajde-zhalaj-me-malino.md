# Ајде жалај ме, Малино

*Ajde žalaj me, Malino* · *Come, Mourn Me, Malina*

Pesna.org #246: <https://pesna.org/song/246>

## Background

A going-into-the-cheta farewell. A young man tells his girl to mourn him while he goes over Pirin mountain to the committee's gathering places. Go to the garden, pick a bouquet of roses and adorn the voivoda Delčev and his faithful band.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Оти' is rendered 'for' and 'накити Делчев војвода' is rendered as adorning Voivode Delchev and his band (with the roses), my reading. The refrain 'Малино моме' is kept in every line.
