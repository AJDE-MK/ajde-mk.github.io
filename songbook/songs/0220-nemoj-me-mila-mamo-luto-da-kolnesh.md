# Немој ме, мила мамо, луто да колнеш

*Nemoj me, mila mamo, luto da kolneš* · *Don't curse me bitterly, dear mother*

Pesna.org #220: <https://pesna.org/song/220>

**Notes.** „Спаија“ е турцизам и значи коњаник или земјопоседник.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Колниш' in line 1 is a dialect form of 'колнеш'. 'Благино село' read as 'Blaga's village'. The last stanza is a violent threat; translated literally.
