## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Стројник' (my reading: a matchmaker or wedding emissary) and 'дејче' (my reading: girl) are dialect words I am not fully sure of; 'ја мама одменеш' is my reading of 'take her away from her mother'.
