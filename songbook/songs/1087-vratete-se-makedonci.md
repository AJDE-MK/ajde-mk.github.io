## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Emigrant song, probably a modern composition, origin unclear (no author I can name). Last refrain line 'жално плаче' repeated; the third-stanza 'гласој наши' I read as 'our voices'.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| заминува | to leave, depart |
| земја | land, country |
| непозната | unknown |
| работи | to work |
| вратете се | come back (plural) |
| родна мајка | native mother, motherland |
| камен на срце | stone on the heart |
