## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. Last line is my reading: 'for Janeta' probably means promised to a man named Janeta/Jane (a male name) in the neighboring family.
