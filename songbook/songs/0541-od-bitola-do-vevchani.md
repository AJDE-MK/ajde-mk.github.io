## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Марио Христовски)*

> From Bitola to Vevchani, everything was encircled
> Hurrah, hurrah, Stavre the Vojvoda!
> Young Stavre was encircled in Shekutko's
> Hurrah, hurrah Stavre the Vojvoda!
>
> Stavre has a manly heart, he is not surrendering
> Hurrah, hurrah, Stavre the Vojvoda!
> Stavre has a Berdan rifle, he is not surrendering
> Hurrah, hurrah Stavre the Vojvoda!
>
> The first bomb he threw hit the hemp field
> Hurrah, hurrah, Stavre the Vojvoda
> Then he hit ten soldiers
> Hurrah, hurrah, Stavre the Vojvoda!
>
> He turned his rifle to his chest, Stavre the Vojvoda
> Hurrah, hurrah he killed himself
> Hurrah, hurrah, for Macedonia!
