## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, origin unclear. 'Лудо' in folk songs is a tender word for a young lover, so I rendered it 'my dear'.

**Words to learn**
