## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Tell me, Stana, my love
> why are you so worried?
> Do you need clothes, Stana,
> or maybe money?
>
> I have everything, Dimcho my love,
> there's just one thing that I don't have,
> just one thing, Dimcho,
> a child from the heart.
>
> I will go, Stana my love,
> to the city of Solun
> and buy you, Stana my love,
> a golden child.
>
> May it be damned, Dime,
> that golden child,
> because it won't tell me
> from his heart: "sweet mama".
