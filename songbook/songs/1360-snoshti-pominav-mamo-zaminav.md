## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. The raven/nightingale verse is a metaphor (the small girl can manage by herself); 'му' in the raven lines is my reading as 'for him'.
