## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear: 'after 100 years' suggests a modern patriotic song, but no author can be named. Delchev, Karev and Sandanski are names of historical Macedonian revolutionaries.
