## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Танка је топола' has an odd 'је' which I read as a dialect 'as/like' (my reading); the last line turns the refrain into an insult.

**Words to learn**
