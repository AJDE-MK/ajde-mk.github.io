## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Old urban song; 'аман' is a Turkish-origin cry, left as is. 'Мерак фрлам' I render 'throw my longing' (my reading); the third-person 'зеде, даде' in verse two is as sung.
