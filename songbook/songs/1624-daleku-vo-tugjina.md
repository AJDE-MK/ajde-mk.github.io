## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> My fate, my darkest fate,
> everything I had, my fate took from me.
> Why did you send me so far,
> will you return me to my native land?
> To see my dear brothers, sisters,
> mother, father, are they alive?
>
> Far away in a foreign land my life flows,
> and my heart burns for the land of my birth.
> Wild foreign winds tear my soul,
> may be damned the years spent in a foreign land.
>
> Far away from mother, from old land,
> you cannot go, happiness cannot be sought.
> There is no sun there to warm you,
> there is no one to sing a song to.
> You will give everything to shorten it,
> just to return home happy.
>
> Far away in a foreign land my life flows,
> and my heart burns for the land of my birth.
> Wild foreign winds tear my soul,
> may be damned the years spent in a foreign land.
