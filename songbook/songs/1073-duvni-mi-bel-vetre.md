## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Alan Moore)*

> Blow for me fair winds,
> roll the green forest,
> melt the white snow.
> Melt the white snow
> open the road to Drama,
> to Drama I want to go.
>
> To Drama I want to go
> there my love is ill.
> From home to take to him,
> from home to take to him
> roast lamb of Gergovo,
> red wine of Melnik.
