## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'таксало' rendered as 'boasted' is my reading; 'живо не станало' is an idiomatic curse rendered literally, my reading.
