## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** The refrain 'ѓене бабам', 'кузум' is Turkish-derived (yine = again, kuzum = my lamb/dear); my reading. The '-арка' trade words are my reading as sellers or workers.
