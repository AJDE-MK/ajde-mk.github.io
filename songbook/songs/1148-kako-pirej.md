## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Modern patriotic song; origin unclear, I cannot name the author. 'во пусти мугри бели' is my reading ('in the empty white dawns').

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| пиреј | couch grass (hard to uproot weed) |
| векови | centuries |
| раздели | divide, separate |
| корни | uproot |
| истреби | wipe out, eradicate |
| мугри | dawns, twilight |
| маки | hardships |
