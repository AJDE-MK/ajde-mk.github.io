## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Why are you so worried, my love,
> worried, my love, and sad?
>
> - How can I not be, my soul, worried,
> when I see, my soul, at the folks,
> how they are getting married, my soul,
> with their soulmates?
