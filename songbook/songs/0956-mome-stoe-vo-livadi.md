## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> A girl stands, dear, a girl stands,
> a girl stands, dear, in the meadows, in the meadows.
> Where she stands, girl, where she stands,
> where she stands, girl, the Sun shines, the Sun shines.
>
> In her hands she holds, girl, in her hands she holds,
> in her hands she holds, girl, a mirror, a mirror.
> She is looking at herself, girl, she is looking at herself,
> she is looking at herself, girl, and says, and says.
>
> - Am I pretty enough, God, am I pretty enough,
> am I pretty enough, God, for a bride on Sunday.
