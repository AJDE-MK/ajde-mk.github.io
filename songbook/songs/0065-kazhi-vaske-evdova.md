## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Евдова' and 'Ергова' look like a surname (my reading: Vaska, daughter of Evdo). 'Посврши' read as 'got you engaged'; the refrain 'Трани нани' is a nonsense refrain. The asterisk and the original note about каврак (Turkish kıvrak, headscarf) are kept as in the source.

**Words to learn**
