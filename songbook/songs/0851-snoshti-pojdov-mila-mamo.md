## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'на бостано' and 'ѓердано', 'фустано' carry a dialect definite or vocative ending -о; I read them as plain nouns. 'пустото ми лудо ... мирно не остана' is my reading: 'the wretched lad would not stay still'.
