## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-style urban love song, no author known. 'Пајтонџиска' is my reading as 'the carriage driver's (girl)'; 'прегори' I render as 'burn through' (burn out completely).
