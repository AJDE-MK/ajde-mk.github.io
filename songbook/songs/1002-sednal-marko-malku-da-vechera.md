## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional epic about Krale Marko; his mother is Evrosima. Rendering of 'венча' and 'крсти' as marrying off / being godfather is my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| абер | news, message (Turkish loan) |
| вечера | supper |
| венча | to marry off, to crown (as best man) |
| крсти | to christen, be godfather |
| крвава војна | bloody war |
| себап | good deed, spiritual merit |
| побожност | piety |
| ја послуша | he obeyed |
