## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional town song from Veles; origin otherwise unclear. 'прааџика' and 'јорганџика' are my reading (a woman associated with a trade or with quilts), and the Asija lines are obscure, so those are guesses.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| мамо | mother (vocative) |
| либе | sweetheart, beloved |
| налони | wooden clogs |
| ѓердани | necklaces (beaded or coin) |
| ѕвецка | jingles |
| бакшиш | tip, gratuity |
| мерак | fancy, passion, delight |
| накити се | adorn yourself |
| којничани | people of Kojnik (a Veles neighborhood) |
