## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Stojan walked, walked,
> many villages, cities,
> he looked for a beautiful girl,
> he decided to get married.
> To settle down at home,
> to settle down,
> like all good young men,
> to become a man.
>
> He took a beautiful wife,
> but very lazy,
> she just lies around
> and gives orders about everything,
> she just dresses up
> and hums,
> waits for dawn
> and takes off (goes out).
>
> Today, tomorrow, every day,
> poor Stojan turned white,
> Stojan started drinking,
> started to come home drunk:
> - What devil made me
> to get married,
> to bring such a big plague
> in my home?
