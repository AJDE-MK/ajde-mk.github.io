## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'Трипатанке' (Tripatanka) is probably a nickname; 'од греови, од грев' is my reading ('because of sin'), the exact sense is uncertain.
