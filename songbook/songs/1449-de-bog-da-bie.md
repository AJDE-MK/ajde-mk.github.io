## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Let God smite him, King Marco,
> That he got up early on Sunday
> And sat down to dine;
> A grey falcon swooped in.
>
> Followed by Philip the Hungarian.
>
> How did he know, King Marco?
> How did he know, hither to come?
> I am, I am a better hero than him!
>
> - I have slain seventy kings
> And captured brides - all young ones.
