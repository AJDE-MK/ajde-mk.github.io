# Никнало цвеќе шарено

*Niknalo cveḱe šareno* · *Colourful flowers sprouted*

Pesna.org #219: <https://pesna.org/song/219>

## Background

A flower-and-window miniature. A bright flower grows under the girl's window; by day she tends it, by night a boy steals it. The scolding: you only know how to steal flowers, not how to kiss a girl. Flowers are for smelling; a boy is for kissing. Often sung by children, though the double meaning is there even then.
