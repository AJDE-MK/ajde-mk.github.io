## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> The army encircled Delchev's company
> in the Macedonian village Banica,
> hero after hero, the entire company fell,
> black cloud covered Macedonia.
>
> Goce Delchev lies gravely wounded,
> slavery weighs more on him than his wounds.
> - I am dying, I am not sorry - the hero speaks,
> even my death will not separate me from Macedonia.
>
> All Macedonians will forever grieve
> for the famous hero, Delchev voivoda,
> he gave his heart and soul without regrets,
> mother Macedonia will forever treasure him.
>
> Goce Delchev died so young,
> pride of all Macedonia.
> Men and women Macedonians
> will forever fight tyranny.
