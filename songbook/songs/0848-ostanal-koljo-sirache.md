# Останал Кољо сираче

*Ostanal Koljo sirače* · *Kolyo Was Left an Orphan*

Pesna.org #848: <https://pesna.org/song/848>

**Notes.** Јашмак е вел, фереџе.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding ballad, no author known. 'Анама' (in 'бела анама') is a dialect or Turkish-derived word I am guessing means 'lady' (cf. hanım); 'анамче' is then 'young lady'; my reading.
