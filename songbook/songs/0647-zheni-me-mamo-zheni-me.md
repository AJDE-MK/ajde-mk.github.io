## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Let me marry, mother, let me marry,
> let me marry, sweet mother,
> while I am still a young man.
>
> While still, mother, while still,
> while still, sweet mother,
> girls want me.
>
> While still, mother, while still,
> while still, sweet mother,
> bouquet goes well with me.
>
> Let me marry, mother, let me marry,
> let me marry, sweet mother,
> a beautiful girl,
> for you and my father,
> that will be a great joy.
