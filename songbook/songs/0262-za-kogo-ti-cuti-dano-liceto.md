## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> For whom is your face blooming, Dana,
> on whom are your eyes glancing, Dana,
> for whom are you saving your body, Dana,
> that slim body, Dana, like marble?
>
> Oh my caressed one, caressed, Dana,
> who will fondle your blonde hair, Dana,
> unbutton your silver jelick, Dana
> and kiss your lips of honey, Dana?
>
> Soon, worries will enter my heart
> my white and red face will wither
> that slim body will quickly fade away
> and these honey lips will wither, Dana.
>
> May god punish all the mothers, fathers
> those who are greedy for wealth and money
> who give in marriage their dear children, however they wish
> and enslave their children until the grave.
