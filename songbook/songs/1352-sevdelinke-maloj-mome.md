# Севделинке малој моме

*Sevdelinke maloj mome* · *Sevdelinka, Little Girl*

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, southern dialect (je for e, 'купу'). 'Ќе му купу' is rendered 'I will buy her' as my reading; 'ѕм, ѕм, ѕм' is a sung refrain syllable, left as 'zm'.
