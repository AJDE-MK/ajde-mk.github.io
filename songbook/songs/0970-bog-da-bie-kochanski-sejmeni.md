## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, historical-sounding song, no author known. 'Ги однеле' is ambiguous (who was taken up to the middle of the village); I kept it literal and read it as the seymens being led there.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| бог да бие | may God strike (a curse) |
| сејмени | seymens, Ottoman irregular armed guards |
| спаднале | came down, descended |
| селани | villagers |
| комити | komitas, rebel fighters |
| џанам | my dear, my soul (Turkish filler) |
| чешма | fountain, tap |
