## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Katushe, poor Katushe,
> what destiny was written for you.
> Oh, dear Katushe,
> I am burning, my lamb, for you.
>
> You married a drunkard,
> Nikola from the Kocare family.
> Oh, dear Katushe,
> I am burning, my lamb, for you.
>
> All the boys are at the market,
> your boy is by the barrel.
> Oh, dear Katushe,
> I am burning, my lamb, for you.
>
> All the boys go abroad to work,
> your boy goes to the tavern.
> Oh, dear Katushe,
> I am burning, my lamb, for you.
>
> Rows of florins for Robe,
> but peppers for Kocareta.
> Oh, dear Katushe,
> I am burning, my lamb, for you.
