# Коледа леда

*Koleda leda* · *Koleda leda*

Pesna.org #475: <https://pesna.org/song/475>

## Background

A comic carol tied to the *koledување* custom: on Christmas Eve, and through the twelve days after, bands of men and boys went door to door singing. The host had to give them food, wine and money or be cheerfully insulted. This one is the comic kind; the blessing kind wishes the house good harvests and health.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Children's koleda carol. 'леда' is probably a rhyming nonsense word. 'квачи' and 'гускини, шаткини' are my reading ('clucks', 'goose/duck eggs'); 'шаткини' is uncertain. The version marker is '(друга верзија)' as in the original.
