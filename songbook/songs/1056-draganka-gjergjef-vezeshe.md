## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, with a Mariovo version after the marker. 'Пркна' (darted) and 'кондиса' (perched) are my reading of dialect verbs; 'у таја' is dialect for 'in that'. In the Mariovo version the outcome is reversed: the nightingale wins.
