## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Лудо' is an endearment here (crazy one/darling); 'ќ'' in 'ќ' одам' is a contraction of 'ќе'. Last line is my reading: 'земја скрила' as 'the earth has hidden'.
