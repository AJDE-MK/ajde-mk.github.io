## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional/historical song about the 1903 Ilinden uprising at Krushevo (Pitu Guli was a real leader). 'Даскалчето' is a nickname meaning little teacher; Mechkin Kamen is a place name.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| бој | battle |
| се заврза | started, broke out (lit. was tied) |
| пушки | rifles |
| бомби | bombs |
| се затемни | grew dark |
| извика се | cried out |
| аскер | army, soldiers (from Turkish) |
| растераш | you scatter, drive away |
| патот | the road |
