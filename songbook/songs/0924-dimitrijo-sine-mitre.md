## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Dimitar, my son Mitre,
> mother's only child.
> For three years, my son Mitre,
> your mother lies sick in bed.
>
> You didn't come, my son Mitre,
> to see your mother.
> To ask me, my son Mitre,
> why am I lying sick.
>
> Dimitar, my son Mitre,
> mother's fool.
> Can't you see, my son Mitre,
> that your wife is cheating on you?
>
> - Let her, let her, mother,
> let her cheat on me,
> at least she is pretty.
