## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Tell me, Dana, fair Dana,
> who gave you those dark eyes?
> Who gave you those dark eyes,
> for which I drink through the long, long nights?
>
> To some you bring joy, to others sorrow,
> but to me, only a bitter lie.
> To some you bring happiness, to others joy,
> but to me, fair Dana, only a bitter youth.
>
> What have you done to me, Dana? Have you no soul?
> You will regret this, why won't you take me as your own?
> The wine burns within me, the rakija sears,
> this wretched longing, Dana, can no longer be cured.
>
> To some you bring joy, to others sorrow,
> but to me, only a bitter lie.
> To some you bring happiness, to others joy,
> but to me, fair Dana, only a bitter youth.
>
> If you still love, if your heart still burns,
> say something, Dana, open up your heart.
> Where are you now, so far away from me?
> What have you done to me, Dana? May the devil take you!
>
> To some you bring joy, to others sorrow,
> but to me, only a bitter lie.
> To some you bring happiness, to others joy,
> but to me, fair Dana, only a bitter youth.
