# Ајде што ти текна што побегна

*Ajde što ti tekna što pobegna* · *Come On, What Came Over You That You Ran Away*

Pesna.org #440: <https://pesna.org/song/440>

## Background

A cheeky wedding song. A young bride runs away from her marital home before dawn, and everyone guesses why: the house is fine, the father-in-law is a tame lamb, the mother-in-law is a hot snake. The real reason, the last verse says, is the young husband himself. It is played at weddings amid howls of laughter. A second version of the song is under #772.
