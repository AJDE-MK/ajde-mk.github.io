## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Revolutionary-era patriotic song, treated as traditional. Last line 'среде војска и јатаган' is rendered literally; 'алово знаме' as 'crimson banner' is my reading.
