*Molitva* · *Prayer*

Pesna.org #1472: <https://pesna.org/song/1472>

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional rain-prayer song. 'навади' is read as 'water/pour water on' (my reading). Ojlijarche is a girl who takes part in rain rituals, per the source note.
