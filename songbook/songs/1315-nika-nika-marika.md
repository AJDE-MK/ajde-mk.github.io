## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> God, God what have you done to me,
> my heart is still burning?
> To dream about her face,
> to dream about Marika?
>
> Nika, Nika, Nika, beautiful Marika,
> she left me only a picture,
> She run away, my Marika,
> with two new fiancés.
>
> Was it not enough that I begged you
> and talked to you.
> Were they not enough, the houses, yards,
> my songs, my words.
>
> Nika, Nika, Nika, beautiful Marika,
> she left me only a picture,
> She run away, my Marika,
> with two new fiancés.
>
> God, God what have you done to me,
> my heart is still burning?
> And I don't have much strength
> to bring you back, my dear.
>
> Nika, Nika, Nika, beautiful Marika,
> she left me only a picture,
> She run away, my Marika,
> with two new fiancés.
