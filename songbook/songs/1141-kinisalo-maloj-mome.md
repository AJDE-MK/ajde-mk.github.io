## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Jutta Malzbender)*

> A young girl went out
> up to the field
> to do the harvesting
> of beautiful wheat.
>
> While she was harvesting
> that beautiful wheat,
> from the beautiful wheat,
> something came up,
> oh yes, something came up,
> oh yes, an unmarried boy.
>
> Oh abandon, girl,
> your sharp sickle,
> so we can go to my house,
> go to my house,
> so you will be my bride.
>
> - Stop, wait, you crazy boy,
> so I will go home
> and talk to my mother,
> that I am going to marry,
> that I am going to marry, crazy lad,
> a rich boy.
