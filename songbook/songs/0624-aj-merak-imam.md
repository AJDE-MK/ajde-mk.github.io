## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'комшиче' is grammatically neuter, but it is read as a girl; 'ми го дадоа' keeps the neuter. Stanzas 4 and 5 are obscure; my reading is that the speaker cursed the match and she will be widowed by the following week.
