# Се собрале комитите

*Se sobrale komitite* · *The komiti gathered*

Pesna.org #199: <https://pesna.org/song/199>

**Notes.** „Метеризи“ е турски збор, кој значи „заседи“.

## Background

One of the further Ilinden songs your songbook listed to explore. Its title means "The Komiti Gathered". Not checked whether it is traditional or a later composition.

*Source of these notes: your 6-song songbook.*

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Марио Христовски)*

> The komiti gathered
> They will go to Dry Valley
> In front walks the Vojvoda
> The Vojvoda, Jordan Piperka.
>
> They will be creating an ambush
> They will be waiting for Alil Pasha
> Who kidnapped blonde Stojna
> Blonde Stojna, from Small Cersko.
>
> The vojvoda speaks:
> -Let’s go down to Brezovo,
> So we can wed Alil Pasha
> With the bride the sharp sword.
