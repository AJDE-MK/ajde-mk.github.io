## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Dialogue between son and mother; the forest band refers to haiduk or rebel fighters in my reading. 'фајде си немаш' I read as 'you have no benefit'.

**Words to learn**
