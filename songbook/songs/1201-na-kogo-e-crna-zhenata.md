## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> He who has a dark wife
> has no need for cinnamon.
> He who has a fair wife
> has no need for sugar.
>
> He who has a fat wife
> has no need for a quilt.
> He who has a tall wife
> has no need for a ladder.
>
> He who has a beautiful wife
> has no need for company.
> And he who has an ugly wife
> – his mind is at ease.
