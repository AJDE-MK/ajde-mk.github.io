## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Ressen and Jankovec are places near Lake Prespa. The last two lines are my reading of the dialect 'сакам' as 'I love you'.
