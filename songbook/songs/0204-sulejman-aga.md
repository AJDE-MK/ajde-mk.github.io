## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Јоти' is a dialect particle, my reading 'that / that very'. Whose point of view 'дава / не дава' takes is ambiguous; I read it as the agha.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| ага | agha (Ottoman landlord, title) |
| чифлик | estate, farm held by a landlord |
| џанам | my dear, my soul |
| тенка | slender |
| висока | tall |
| бела ем црвена | fair and rosy (white and red) |
| овци | sheep |
| дава | gives |
