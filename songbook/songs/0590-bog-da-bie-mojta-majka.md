## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Што ме даде' means 'because she gave me (in marriage)'; the curse is a conventional bride's lament, not literal. 'Јано' is a vocative of Jana.
