## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'анамче' rendered 'mother' (my reading; likely a dialect form of 'dear mother'). The linen may be a metaphor for the daughter; that is my guess, not stated in the text.
