## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Southern/Pirin-type dialect forms (гу, шта, чину, појду). 'Божинче' is a girl's name or pet name (my reading); 'алтани' I read as gold coins, the dowry money.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| тужу | I grieve, I pine (dialect form of тужам) |
| лудо | darling, dear (lit. crazy one; endearment) |
| шта | what (dialect, standard: што) |
| да чину | to do (dialect, standard: да правам) |
| гу | her/him (dialect, standard: ја/го) |
| појду | I go (dialect, standard: одам) |
| лојзе | vineyard (dialect of лозје) |
| алтани | gold coins |
| не ме дава | does not give me away (in marriage) |
