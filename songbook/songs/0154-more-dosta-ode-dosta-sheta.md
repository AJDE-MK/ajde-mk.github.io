## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Џан Софијо' (also 'Софилој') is read as an endearment plus the name Sofia. 'Бело ле, без белило' is a refrain I am not sure of (my reading: 'white one, without whitener').
