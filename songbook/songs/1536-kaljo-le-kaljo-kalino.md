*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, origin unclear. 'Каља' is a short form of the name Калина. 'Абре' is a Turkish-origin interjection, here roughly 'hey you'.
