## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Магија сторила' is a birth-time charm so that suitors will circle the girl; 'нашата' shortened as 'наш'та'. Mostly clear, no major guesses.
