## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Јули ваби' (line 5) is unclear to me; my reading is 'are gathering/calling together'. 'малихери' is read as Mannlicher rifles. 'Ваја вечер' is dialect for 'this evening'.
