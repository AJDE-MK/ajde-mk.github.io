# Зачукале тапаните

*Začukale tapanite* · *The Drums Have Started Pounding*

Pesna.org #260: <https://pesna.org/song/260>

## Background

A wedding-day mini-tragedy with a comic shrug. The drums beat and the wedding party gathers, with a nonsense refrain that means roughly "what a big thing!". The swollen Vardar then carries off the bride; the groom cries and the guests start arguing about gifts.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Wedding song with a refrain in a 20th-century dance style; origin unclear, no author I can name. 'најавнале, ми јавнале' is my reading of 'јавнале' (rode off).
