## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Note says it commemorates voivoda Apostol Petkov of Enidze Vardar. 'Постоле' is the vocative of Postol, short for Apostol. 'први шпиони' translated as 'first-rate spies' is my reading.

**Words to learn**
