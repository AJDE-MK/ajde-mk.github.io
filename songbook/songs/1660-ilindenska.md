## English

*From Pesna.org*

> I prepare a horse, I sing songs, I will go on my way,
> on Ilinden1, Mechkin Kamen2, to bow down.
> Where Pitu's company gloriously died
> for the defense of the Krushevo Republic.
>
> Glory, glorious heroes,
> for you died bravely
> and today let us celebrate you
> on Ilinden, glorious Krushevo.
>
> In 1903, on Ilinden,
> you raised your head in the midst of the empire.
> Ten days of the republic, in the midst of the Balkans,
> the entire empire shook, the emperor went mad.
>
> Glory, glorious heroes,
> for you died bravely
> and today let us celebrate you
> on Ilinden, glorious Krushevo.
>
> Goce Delchev, Dame, Karev, Pitu, Sandanski3,
> may you have eternal glory for everything you have done.
> You have spent your youth in prisons and across mountains,
> for the freedom of your native land Macedonia.
>
> Glory, glorious heroes,
> bravely you died
> and today let us celebrate you
> on Ilinden, glorious Krusevo.
