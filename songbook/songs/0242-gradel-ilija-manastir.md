## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'црно морасо' (black habit) is my reading; 'морасо' is a dialect word I am not sure of. ' Илино' (vocative) may be a variant of Irina.
