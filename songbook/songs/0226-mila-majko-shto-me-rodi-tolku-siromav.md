## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: John Pappas)*

> Dear mother why did you give birth to me so poor?
> Poor, poor, so damn poor (I have become wasted).
>
> I have a house, but I have no wife.
> Poor, poor, so damn poor (I have become wasted).
>
> I have a bed, but I have no blanket.
> Poor, poor, so damn poor (I have become wasted).
>
> I have a stove, but I have no wood (to burn in the stove).
> Poor, poor, so damn poor (I have become wasted).
>
> I have a wallet, but I have no money.
> Poor, poor, so damn poor (I have become wasted).
