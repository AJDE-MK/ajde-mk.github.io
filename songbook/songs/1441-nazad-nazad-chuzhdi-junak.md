## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> - Go back, go back foreign hero,
> my mother is watching from inside,
> go back, go back foreign hero.
> - If she’s watching let her watch,
> our mother, my mother-in-law.
>
> - Go back, go back foreign hero,
> my father is watching from inside,
> go back, go back foreign hero.
> - If he’s watching let him watch,
> your father, my father-in-law.
>
> - Go back, go back foreign hero,
> my brother is watching from inside,
> go back, go back foreign hero.
> - If he’s watching let him watch,
> your brother, my brother-in-law.
>
> - Go back, go back foreign hero,
> my sister is watching from inside,
> go back, go back foreign hero.
> - If she’s watching let her watch,
> your sister, my sister-in-law.
