## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Wedding song as a dialogue between the wedding party and the host; origin unclear, I cannot name an author. 'тук' сме' is read as 'but we have', my reading.
