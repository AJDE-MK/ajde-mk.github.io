## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding song with the transformation motif. 'Мен'' = мене (me), 'ај' here is a filler; 'најг'ста' is my reading of 'най-густа'.

**Words to learn**

| преминеш | cross over |
