## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional holiday song. 'Набели си бело лице' means whiten (powder) your face, as I read it; 'да пукат душманите' is literally 'let the enemies burst' (with envy).
