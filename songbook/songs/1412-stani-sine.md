## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Виктор Синадиноски)*

> - Get up my son, the pasha is calling you
> to transfer to you the Albanian estate.
>
> - He is not calling me, mother, for the transfer,
> the pasha is calling for my head.
>
> The pasha’s daughter came out of a bath house,
> all washed, mother, beautifully rubbed dry.
>
> I seized the pasha’s daughter, mother,
> well I kissed her and I made love to her.
>
> For that, mother, the pasha is calling me
> the pasha is calling for my head.
