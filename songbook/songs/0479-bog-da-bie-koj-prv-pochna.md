# Бог да бие кој прв почна

*Bog da bie koj prv počna* · *May God strike whoever started it first*

Pesna.org #479: <https://pesna.org/song/479>

## Background

A pechalba song (see the guide *History, places and customs*). Curse whoever first went on pechalba to America. Three years without work or money; the steamship has arrived and he cannot afford the fare. So she must sell her coin necklace, then her jacket, to buy his ticket home.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*
