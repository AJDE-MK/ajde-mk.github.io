## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear; a modern-sounding song about Tsar Samuel and his blinded army. Line 'Јас не можам ослепени да ве гледам и умирам' is my reading.
