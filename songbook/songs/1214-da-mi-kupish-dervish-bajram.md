## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding; origin unclear. 'Пуш елече' I read as a light tulle-like vest; 'распеглаш' as 'iron/smooth out', both my reading.
