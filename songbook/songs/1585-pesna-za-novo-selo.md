## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Novo Selo is a village name; 'Л'џите' is a place name by the spring, I am unsure of its meaning (my reading: a locality name). Possibly a modern local song, origin unclear, no author I can name.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| запеј | sing! (imperative, start singing) |
| се чуе | can be heard |
| извор | spring (water source) |
| жалосна | sad, mournful |
| радосна | joyful |
| вечна | eternal |
| љубов | love |
