## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Бела руба' (white clothes, versus mourning black) and the wish to burn his heart are my reading of the dying girl's demand for a year of mourning.
