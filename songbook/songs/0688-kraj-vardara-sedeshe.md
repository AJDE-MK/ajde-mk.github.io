## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Да пукнеш ќе те земам' I read as 'I will take you even if you burst/die', an idiom of defiance (my reading). The last refrain has 'пукниш' as written.
