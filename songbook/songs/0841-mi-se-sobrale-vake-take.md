## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional village song, no author known (origin unclear). 'ваке-таке' left as a refrain; 'ситноно писмо' is my reading as a carefully written letter.
