## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-style song. 'Од што се лудо зарадвало, дури не разбрало' is my reading (the lover rejoiced before even understanding); the final stanza mixes the mother's question and the girl's answer in one block.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| легна | lay down, went to bed |
| лудо | darling, love (endearment, lit. crazy one) |
| бре | hey (emphatic particle) |
