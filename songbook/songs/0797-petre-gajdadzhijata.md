## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional-sounding dance song. Forms like фанаја/фанаа are dialect past tenses of 'to take hold'; лидо I read as 'sweetheart', somewhat unsure.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| гајда | bagpipe |
| гајдаџија | bagpiper |
| оро | circle dance |
| се фати на оро | to join the dance |
| бате | older brother (form of address) |
| моми | girls, young unmarried women |
| ергени | bachelors, young men |
| лидо | sweetheart (dialect) |
