## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Somewhere they will silence you,
> somewhere they will hide you,
> with grave upon grave they will cover you.
> Only you are immortal,
> living water from the underground,
> where you sink, you spring forth.
>
> Centuries of hell, together we shall pass,
> ancient Macedonian land,
> so it is written that we shall be nothing,
> you without me, and I without you.
>
> They may disown you,
> others may betray you,
> they can invent anything.
> Only you are immortal,
> living water from the underground,
> where you sink, you spring forth.
>
> Centuries of hell, together we shall pass,
> ancient Macedonian land,
> so it is written that we shall be nothing,
> you without me, and I without you.
