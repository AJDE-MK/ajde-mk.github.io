## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Unchecked machine translation. 'Ат-пазар' (Horse Market) in Bitola is my reading; the amount in the penultimate line is 'сто и пејсет' (dialect, probably one hundred and sixty) and then 'сто и пеесет' (one hundred and fifty), a variant in the source.
