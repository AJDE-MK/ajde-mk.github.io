## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Two or three citizens loved me,
> but my mother didn't give me to citizens.
>
> Two or three villagers loved me,
> my mother gаve me to а villager.
>
> On Sunday, they organized my wedding,
> on Monday, I put my sickle on my shoulder.
>
> On Monday, I put my sickle on my shoulder,
> they sent me right away to harvest the field.
