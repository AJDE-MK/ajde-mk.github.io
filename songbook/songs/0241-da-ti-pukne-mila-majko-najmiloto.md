## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Варош' is a place name (probably a district of Prilep, my guess); 'Митриќевци' is probably a family or household name. The last stanza ('сум каснала кај мајка ми') is my reading: they said I had already eaten at my mother's house.
