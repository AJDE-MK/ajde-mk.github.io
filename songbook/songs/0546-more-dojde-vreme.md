## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** WWII partisan song, origin unclear (no author I can name). Gorna Dzhumaya is the old name of Blagoevgrad in Bulgaria.
