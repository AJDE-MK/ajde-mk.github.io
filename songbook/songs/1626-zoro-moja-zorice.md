## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> - Zora, my Zora,
> I hear words about you,
> tell me who you are with
> besides me?
>
> - Oh, you foolish boy,
> you beautiful boy.
> Didn't I caress you,
> given you love?
> What "where am I", "who am I with",
> here, I am yours again.
>
> - Zora, my Zora,
> they saw you with another last night,
> tell me, you unfaithful,
> what were you doing?
>
> - Oh, you foolish boy,
> listen to the words.
> Didn't I caress you,
> send you to sleep with a kiss?
> What "where am I", "who am I with",
> here, I am yours again.
>
> - Zora, my Zora,
> why are you coming to me late,
> tell me, you unfaithful,
> what were you doing?
>
> - Oh, you foolish boy,
> don't annoy me.
> Didn't I caress you,
> send you to sleep on my chest?
> What "where am I", "who am I with",
> here, I am yours again.
