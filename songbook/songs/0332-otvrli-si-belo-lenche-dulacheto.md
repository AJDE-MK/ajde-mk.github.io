## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Кара Коце' rendered as 'dark Koce' (my reading, 'кара' = dark/black); 'дулаче' as veil and 'Јонета' as a name are guesses. 'Подигни си, бре невесте дулачето' is rendered as 'Lift your veil, hey bride'.
