## English

*From Pesna.org*

> Osman travels along the road, asking the way,
> And on the road, he meets an old woman:
> "Oh, good woman, dear old woman,
> Where does fair Rada live?"
>
> "Fair Rada, agha, is not at home;
> Turn your path toward the upper field,
> Rada is reaping the short barley there
> With her little brother, brother Kosta."
>
> Upon hearing this, Osman turned his path;
> He headed straight for the upper field,
> Looking out into the distance to spot
> Where fair Rada was reaping.
>
> And little Kosta,
> He cast a glance toward the road,
> Spied the accursed Turk,
> And spoke to his sister Rada:
>
> "Oh, sister mine, fair Rada,
> Do you see, sister, what I see?
> For the accursed Turk is coming this way,
> He will wreak great havoc, sister!"
>
> Fair Rada spoke up:
> "Oh, little brother, dear brother,
> Hand me the slender rifle;
> You take aim, brother, and I shall fire,
> To kill the accursed Turk."
>
> But little Kosta,
> He grew frightened and began to weep,
> Then hid himself beneath a sheaf of grain.
> Kosta cried out in alarm:
> "Don't let him get me, sister Rada!"
>
> When Rada saw there was no help,
> she unwound her red headscarf,
> she tightened her sash,
> she rolled up her sleeves,
> then Rada lifted the Berdan1 rifle;
> she neither took aim nor even looked -
> the rifle fired, and the Turk fell.
