## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> When we were young,
> when we were young,
> When we were young, dear,
> we didn't know what love is.
>
> And now we have grown older,
> and now we have grown older,
> and now we have grown older, dear,
> and we ache because of love.
>
> Your red tie,
> your red tie,
> your red tie, dear,
> will explode my heart.
>
> I will buy you a sewing machine,
> I will buy you a sewing machine,
> I will buy you a sewing machine, dear,
> and you can sew at home.
>
> I don't want you to go to work,
> I don't want you to go to work,
> I don't want you to go to work, dear,
> I want you to be next to me.
