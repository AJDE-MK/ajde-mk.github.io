## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Ѓуро is a male name (Gjuro, from Gjorgji/George). 'прсти крши' is a gesture of grief; 'to feed' with broken fingers is rendered literally and may mean he feeds the bird crumbs from his hands, my reading.
