## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional bride-lament. 'Кандиса' is my reading ('bring yourself to'); 'Канинци' per the notes relates to the village Kanino near Bitola. 'Лика-прилика' in my reading means he is not a match for me.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| кандиса | persuaded yourself, brought yourself to (my reading) |
| да ме дадеш | to give me (in marriage) |
| бргу | quickly (dialect) |
| далеку | far away |
| лика-прилика | a good match, one's likeness |
| либе | sweetheart |
| севда | love, passion (Turkish loan) |
| Канинци | Kanino-area village (per notes, near Bitola) |
