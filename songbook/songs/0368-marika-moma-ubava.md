## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Кристина Боцеска)*

> Marika, the beautiful maiden,
> Marika, a Turkish carnation,
> carried for nine years
> in the pockets of bachelors.
>
> The first year I went
> to ask for Marika’s hand,
> her mother said:
> - Our Marika is too young.
>
> The second year I went
> to propose to Marika,
> her mother said:
> - Our Marika is unwell.
>
> The third year I went
> to take Marika,
> her mother said:
> - Our Marika has passed away.
