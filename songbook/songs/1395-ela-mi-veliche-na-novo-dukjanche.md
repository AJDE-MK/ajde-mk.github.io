## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional playful dialogue song, origin unclear. 'Величе' is a name used as vocative (possibly a girl's name Velichka); 'мазник' (mazhnik) is my reading as a pastry (no certain translation).
