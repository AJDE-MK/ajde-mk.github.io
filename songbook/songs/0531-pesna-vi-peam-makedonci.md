## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear. 'клетва' is literally 'curse' but here reads as a solemn charge from the mother; 'Gotse' is my reading of Гоце (possibly Gotse Delchev, not stated).

| клетва | curse, oath |
| бори се | fight, struggle |
| тирански | tyrannically |

| исековте | you cut off |
