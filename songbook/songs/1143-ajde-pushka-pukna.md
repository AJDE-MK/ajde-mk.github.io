## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional revolutionary-era song naming Dame Gruev and Goce Delchev as those who will take over the dying man's horse and gun. 'на мосто' I read as 'on the bridge'; Karamfilo is the one struck.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |

| пукна | went off, cracked (a shot) |
| мост | bridge |
