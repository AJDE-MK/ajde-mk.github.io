## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Dialect forms with -it for 3rd person (трептит, седнит, кажиш). 'Трептит да седнит' as 'trembling, about to set' is my reading.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| с'нце | sun (short dialect form) |
| трептит | trembles, flickers (dialect -it ending) |
| седнит | sits down, sets (dialect) |
| леле | oh dear (interjection) |
| почекај | wait a little |
| опитам | I ask |
| босилок | basil |
| рида | hill, ridge |
