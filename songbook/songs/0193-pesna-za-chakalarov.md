# Песна за Чакаларов

*Pesna za Čakalarov* · *A song for Chakalarov*

Pesna.org #193: <https://pesna.org/song/193>

**Notes.** Во чест на Васил Чакаларов (Смрдеш, 22 февруари 1874 - Бел Камен, 9 јули 1913) — македонски револуционер, член и војвода на Македонската револуционерна организација.

## English

*From Pesna.org*

> The battle began in the ravine
> Oh, Chakalarov!
> “Capture him alive, hang him!
> That restless dog, surround him!”
>
> A Macedonian girl was harvesting
> Oh, Chakalarov!
> In the short barley.
>
> Bahtiar Pasha is coming from below
> With 30,000
> Chakalarov is with 300 lads.
>
> Bahtiar Pasha was yelling:
> “Soar, brothers!”
> Chakalarov was yelling:
> “Hurrah, lads!”
>
> “Capture him alive, hang him!
> That restless dog, assault him!”
> Take him up to Smilevo
> To the Macedonian committee.
