## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, no author known. 'Калешат' (ram) I read as 'speckled, with a mottled coat'; my reading. The last stanza's meaning (hanged in the girls' chamber) is likely a joke or euphemism; I kept it literal.
