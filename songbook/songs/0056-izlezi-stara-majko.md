# Излези, стара мајко

*Izlezi, stara majko* · *Come Out, Old Mother*

Pesna.org #56: <https://pesna.org/song/56>

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Малихера' is read as the Mannlicher rifle (my reading). I judged this a traditional komita song; it is not certain.
