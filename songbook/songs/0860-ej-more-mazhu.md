## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional humorous song; the second version has the husband beat his wife with a stick instead of fetching the food, as I read it. 'Манџија' (I rendered 'gourmand'), 'суровица' and 'недокастрена' are guesses; the '?' is in the source text.
