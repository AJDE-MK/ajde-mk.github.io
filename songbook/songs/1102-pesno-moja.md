## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> My song, my light,
> sky high, my mountain,
> clear water, paradise valley,
> my sister, my homeland.
>
> My song, my dear faith,
> you support me, you give me strength,
> you made me, you guide me,
> heavenly happiness,
> may you be as eternal as the sun,
> dearest song.
