## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (no author I can name). 'двте' in the original is a typo for 'двете' (both); 'дваете во еден вљубени' I read as both girls in love with one man.
