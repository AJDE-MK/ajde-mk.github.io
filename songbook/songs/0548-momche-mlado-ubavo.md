## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Origin unclear (sounds like a modern composition but no author can be named). The addressee shifts between a young man and a girl (моме), and the second stanza is loosely worded, so 'Whether you want to or not, be mine and love another' is my reading.
