## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional dialogue between a person and a wilted plant (a metaphor for the unhappy bride). 'Зеленико' I render as a name 'Green Plant', my reading.
