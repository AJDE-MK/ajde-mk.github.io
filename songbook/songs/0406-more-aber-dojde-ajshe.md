## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> There is a note,
> Ajshe hey,
> I should go to the army,
> Ajshe, up to nine years.
>
> You should get up
> Ajshe hey,
> very early
> on Monday.
>
> You should wear,
> Ajshe hey,
> brand new clothes,
> new clothes.
>
> You should walk me,
> Ajshe hey,
> down to the meadow,
> to the meadow.
>
> I am giving you a permission,
> Ajshe hey,
> for you to wait for me,
> Ajshe, up to nine years.
>
> In the tenth year,
> Ajshe, hey,
> if you still wait for me,
> I will come back.
>
> I will give you,
> Ajshe hey,
> a big fortune,
> Ajshe, a silver-embroidered vest.
