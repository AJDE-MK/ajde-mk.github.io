## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. Guessing: 'којничанче' (horseman or horse-dealer), 'дурмаџивче' (my reading: carter), and the last line 'од Милета Алду', which I could not understand and translated literally. 'Р'жаново' is probably a family or place name.
