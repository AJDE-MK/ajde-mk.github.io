## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> Maca, Maca, cold water is running,
> cold water is running.
> Maca, Maca, it is pushing a green branch,
> it is pushing a green branch.
>
> Maca, Maca, on the branch there is a white rock,
> on the branch there is a white rock.
> Maca, Maca, on the rock there is a bed,
> on the rock there is a bed.
>
> Maca, Maca, on the bed is sitting,
> on the bed is sitting.
> Maca, Maca, that young tailor,
> that young tailor.
>
> Maca, Maca, he is sewing for Maca,
> sewing for Maca.
> Maca, Maca is sitting next to him,
> sitting next to him.
>
> Maca, Maca, she is threading his threads,
> she is threading his threads.
> Maca, Maca, she is throwing them over the shoulder,
> she is throwing them over the shoulder.
