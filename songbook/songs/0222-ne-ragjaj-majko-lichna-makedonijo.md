# Не раѓај мајко, лична Македонијо

*Ne raǵaj majko, lična Makedonijo* · *Don't give birth, mother, fair Macedonia*

Pesna.org #222: <https://pesna.org/song/222>

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Patriotic song from the era of Ottoman rule; 'синој' in line 9 is likely a typo for 'синови' (my reading). Could be a composed patriotic song, but it is treated as folk-style.
