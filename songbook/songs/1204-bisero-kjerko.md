## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional. 'со девет рала мори тапани' is a guess: my reading is 'drums enough for nine plowlands', i.e. a huge wedding procession.
