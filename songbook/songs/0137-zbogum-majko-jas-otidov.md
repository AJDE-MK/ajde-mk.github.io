# Збогум мајко, јас отидов

*Zbogum majko, jas otidov* · *Farewell, Mother, I Have Left*

Pesna.org #137: <https://pesna.org/song/137>

## Background

A farewell song with a heavy feeling. Goodbye to the father's hearth, brothers and sisters, friends old and young; wherever I go I will remember you forever. The last verse is for the mother alone: in a foreign unknown land I will spend my life.

*Source of these notes: Qwen's draft.*
