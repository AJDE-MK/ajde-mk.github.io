*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Шума градила' is odd; I translated it literally, the idiom may mean something else (my reading). The first version has Bulgarian-like forms (всичко, усте) and the lilce-belilce line is my reading as cosmetics; 'ќускија' is a guess for a basket or pack.
