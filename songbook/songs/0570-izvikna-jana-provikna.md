## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Сега је в котел Турција' is my reading ('now Turkey is in the cauldron', i.e. in trouble); 'је' is dialect. 'Маликери' read as Mannlicher rifles.
