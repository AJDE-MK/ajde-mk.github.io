*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> My heart does not sleep,
> waiting for you to come,
> to bring me dew at dawn.
>
> My life with you
> is like a fairy tale,
> love has decided
> to keep us together.
>
> My soul is looking for a song,
> a song to give you.
> Love, my eyes will tell you,
> when the lips cannot.

> the Lord gave you to me,
