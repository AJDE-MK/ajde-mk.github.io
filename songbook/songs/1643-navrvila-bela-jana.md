## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> White Jana went to the vineyard.
> She picked white grapes,
> white grapes and red grapes.
>
> She didn't pick her own,
> she picked her neighbor's.
> But the field keeper saw her
> he caught her, he tied her up.
>
> Jana's mother heard about it,
> Jana's old mother.
> - Oh, field keeper, my dear brother,
> Oh, field keeper, my dear brother,
> let white Jana go,
> let white Jana go.
>
> I will give you yellow groshes,
> twelve yellow groshes,
> twelve yellow groshes,
> thirteen black groshes.
>
> - Oh, field keeper, my dear brother,
> Oh, field keeper, my dear brother,
> let white Jana go,
> let white Jana go.
