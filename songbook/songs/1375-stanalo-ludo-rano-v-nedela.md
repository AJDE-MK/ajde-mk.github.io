## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** WWII partisan song, no author named. 'лудо' in line 1 is my reading as 'young man' (literally 'crazy/young one'). Karaorman is a mountain and Debarca a region near Ohrid.
