## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Сара Сталевска)*

> Elena girl, Elena
> Elena girl, Elena,
> don’t step on the green hay,
> don’t step on the green hay.
>
> Don’t step on the green hay
> don’t step on the green hay,
> young boys were mowing it,
> they were proposing you, Lenche.
>
> They were proposing you, Lenche
> they were proposing you, Lenche
> to a handsome boy,
> handsome and rich.
>
> Comment: Elena, Lena and Lenche are a variation of the same name.
