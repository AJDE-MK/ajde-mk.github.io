## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** 'Не ме кани ... при теб' да седам' is my reading: 'do not invite me to stay with you' (the lad is speaking, probably the girl being told not to ask). Speaker roles in the middle stanzas are my interpretation.
