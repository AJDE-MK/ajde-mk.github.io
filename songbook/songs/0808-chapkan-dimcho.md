# Чапкан Димчо

*Čapkan Dimčo* · *Flirtatious Dimcho*

Pesna.org #808: <https://pesna.org/song/808>

**Notes.** Зборот „чапкан“ доаѓа од турскиот збор „çapkın“ и значи „женкар“, „уличар“ или „никаквец“.

## English

*From Pesna.org (translation: Pesna.org)*

> You burned me, you destroyed me,
> you, flirtatious Dimcho.
> Hey flirtatious Dimcho,
> my first love.
>
> When you married Mitra,
> you forgot about me.
> Hey flirtatious Dimcho,
> my first love.
>
> You turned me into a dry wood,
> a maple wood.
> Hey flirtatious Dimcho,
> my first love.
>
> Note: the Macedonian word „чапкан“ („chapkan“) comes from the Turkish word „çapkın“ and means „flirtatious“, „crook“, or „bum“.
