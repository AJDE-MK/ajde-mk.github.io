# Ој Јоване

*Oj Jovane* · *Oh Jovan*

Pesna.org #1336: <https://pesna.org/song/1336>

**Notes.** Песната е дел од ритуалите за Св. Лазар.

## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Part of the Lazarica (St Lazarus) ritual songs. 'зару' is a filler particle, here rendered 'indeed' (my reading); 'во седела' is unclear, I read it as a variant of 'седеле' (sat).
