## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Tribute song about the Miladinov brothers of Struga (Dimitar and Konstantin, folk-song collectors who died in Istanbul prison); origin unclear, no author I can name. 'галеби лисја ви вртат' is my reading ('seagulls turn your pages').
