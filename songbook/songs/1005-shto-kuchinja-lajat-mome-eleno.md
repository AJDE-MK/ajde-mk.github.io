## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional ballad with a Bulgarian-flavoured dialect (това, у, тија); 'коси ѝ секоја' I read as 'they cut her hair' and 'џанам' as a filler/exclamation, my reading. 'Ќе ти заколеме' is 'we will slaughter'; the ballad is violent.

**Words to learn**

| Macedonian | English |
| --- | --- |
| лаjат | bark |
| тражат | they look for (dialect) |
| руса коса | fair hair |
| извадиме | we will take out, gouge out |
| на гости | visiting, as a guest |
| арамија | bandit, outlaw |
| изба | cellar |
| фучија | barrel |
