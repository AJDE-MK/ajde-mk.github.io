## English

*Machine translation by Claude (my reading), not on Pesna.org and not checked by a native speaker. Treat it as a study aid.*

**Translator's notes.** Traditional, no author known. 'Да зажарват' I render 'will be jealous' (literally 'will burn'); 'Клепки има, очи нема' is a poetic image I read as 'I have lashes but no eyes (nothing to see with) since he does not come'; my reading.
